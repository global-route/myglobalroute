## What is the blocked account?

A Sperrkonto (blocked account) is a special German bank account you must open and fund before your student visa is approved. It proves to the German embassy that you can support yourself financially during your studies.

**Required amount:** €11,208 (as of 2026 — equivalent to €934 per month for 12 months)

The funds are released to you monthly after you arrive in Germany. You cannot withdraw the full amount at once.

## Key requirements at a glance

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Blocked account amount | €11,208 |
| Monthly release | €934/month |
| Account providers | Fintiba, Expatrio, Deutsche Bank |
| Setup time | 5–10 business days |
| Visa type | National visa (D) for study |

## How to open a blocked account from Nigeria

1. Choose a provider — Fintiba and Expatrio accept international transfers and are the most commonly used by Nigerian applicants
2. Register online and receive your IBAN
3. Transfer €11,208 from a Nigerian domiciliary account (USD → EUR) via Wise or your bank
4. Receive your Finanzierungsnachweis (proof of funds certificate) — this goes into your visa application

## Costs beyond the blocked account

The blocked account covers living expenses only. You will also need:

- **Visa application fee:** €75
- **Health insurance:** ~€110/month (statutory German insurance)
- **University semester contribution:** €150–€350 per semester (admin/transport — not tuition)
- **Flight:** varies
