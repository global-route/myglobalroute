Saskatchewan is one of Canada's Prairie provinces with a documented labour shortage in healthcare, agriculture, engineering, technology and trades. The Saskatchewan Immigrant Nominee Program (SINP) is one of the most accessible provincial nominee programs for African applicants - particularly those with a connection to the province through study or work.

## Why Saskatchewan matters for African applicants

**The 600-point advantage:** A provincial nomination from Saskatchewan adds 600 CRS points to your Express Entry profile. Most Express Entry draws have cut-offs between 470 and 550 points. With 600 additional points, you will receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA) in the next draw regardless of your base CRS score.

**Lower base CRS requirement:** Because the provincial nomination provides the 600-point boost, you do not need a high base CRS score to get Canadian PR through the SINP. Applicants with CRS scores of 300-400 who would never receive an ITA in a general draw can receive one through the SINP.

**Primary source:** `https://www.saskatchewan.ca/residents/moving-to-saskatchewan/immigrate-to-saskatchewan/saskatchewan-immigrant-nominee-program`

## SINP streams relevant for African applicants

### Stream 1 - International Skilled Worker: Saskatchewan Express Entry

**Who it is for:** Skilled workers in the Express Entry pool who want to live and work in Saskatchewan.

**Requirements:**
- Active Express Entry profile
- Work experience in an eligible occupation (NOC TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3)
- Intention to live and work in Saskatchewan
- Meet the points threshold on the SINP points assessment grid

**No job offer required** for this stream - your Express Entry profile and occupation are the primary criteria.

**Process:**
1. Create an Express Entry profile with IRCC
2. Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) to the SINP
3. Receive a nomination from Saskatchewan
4. Accept the nomination and update your Express Entry profile (+600 CRS points)
5. Receive an ITA from IRCC in the next draw
6. Submit a complete application for permanent residence

### Stream 2 - International Skilled Worker: Occupations In-Demand

**Who it is for:** Skilled workers with a job offer from a Saskatchewan employer in an in-demand occupation.

**Requirements:**
- Job offer from a Saskatchewan employer
- Work experience in an eligible occupation
- Language proficiency (CLB 4 minimum for most occupations)
- Sufficient funds

**Does not require an Express Entry profile** - this stream operates outside the Express Entry system.

### Stream 3 - International Graduate

**Who it is for:** Graduates of Saskatchewan post-secondary institutions (including the University of Saskatchewan).

**Requirements:**
- Degree, diploma or certificate from a Saskatchewan post-secondary institution
- Graduated within the past 2 years
- Work experience in Saskatchewan (some streams require this)
- Intention to live and work in Saskatchewan

**Why this matters for USask graduates:** If you studied at the University of Saskatchewan and want to stay in Canada, the International Graduate stream is specifically designed for you. It has lower requirements than the skilled worker streams.

## The Saskatchewan connection - why it matters

Saskatchewan actively recruits immigrants to address labour shortages. The province has documented shortages in:

| Sector | In-demand occupations |
|---|---|
| Healthcare | Nurses, doctors, pharmacists, physiotherapists |
| Technology | Software developers, data analysts, IT project managers |
| Engineering | Civil, mechanical, electrical, agricultural engineers |
| Agriculture | Agricultural managers, agrologists, equipment operators |
| Trades | Electricians, plumbers, welders, heavy equipment operators |
| Education | Teachers (especially French immersion and STEM) |

## The full pathway for African applicants

**Option A - Study then stay:**
1. Study at University of Saskatchewan (or another Saskatchewan institution)
2. Graduate and receive Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP)
3. Work in Saskatchewan in a relevant occupation
4. Apply for SINP International Graduate stream
5. Receive provincial nomination (+600 CRS)
6. Receive ITA and apply for permanent residence

**Option B - Skilled worker direct:**
1. Create an Express Entry profile
2. Submit EOI to SINP International Skilled Worker stream
3. Receive provincial nomination (+600 CRS)
4. Receive ITA and apply for permanent residence

**Option C - Job offer route:**
1. Secure a job offer from a Saskatchewan employer
2. Apply for SINP Occupations In-Demand stream
3. Receive provincial nomination
4. Apply for permanent residence
