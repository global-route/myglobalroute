# UK Innovator Founder Visa 2026: Business Route to British Residency

## Quick Summary

- **What it is:** UK visa for founders establishing an innovative business
- **Replaced:** Innovator visa and Start-up visa (both closed April 2023)
- **Minimum investment:** None set by Home Office
- **Key gate:** Endorsement from an approved endorsing body
- **Maintenance funds:** GBP 1,270 held for 28 consecutive days
- **Initial visa:** 3 years
- **Path to settlement:** 3 years (ILR) if business milestones met
- **Source:** gov.uk/innovator-founder-visa

**Bottom line:** The route is open to African founders with genuinely innovative business ideas. The endorsement process is the real filter, not a capital threshold.

---

## What Changed from the Old Innovator Visa

The original Innovator visa (closed April 2023) required GBP 50,000 investment. The Innovator Founder visa removed that requirement entirely. The gate is now endorsement quality, not capital.

Key changes:
- No GBP 50,000 minimum investment
- Endorsing body list expanded and updated
- Business must be new or recently established (not an existing business you are buying into)
- You can work for another employer part-time while building your business

---

## The Three Criteria Your Business Must Meet

Every endorsing body assesses the same three criteria set by the Home Office:

**1. Innovative** - A genuine, original business idea that is different from anything already in the UK market, or that significantly improves on what exists.

**2. Viable** - A credible business plan with realistic financial projections. The endorsing body must believe the business can succeed.

**3. Scalable** - Potential to grow and create jobs in the UK. The business should not be a lifestyle business or a sole-trader operation with no growth path.

Your business idea is assessed against all three. Failing any one means no endorsement.

---

## Endorsing Bodies

The Home Office publishes the full list at gov.uk/government/publications/endorsing-bodies-innovator-founder.

Endorsing bodies include:
- UK universities with entrepreneurship programmes
- Accelerators and incubators approved by the Home Office
- Business support organisations

Each endorsing body has its own application process, fees and sector focus. Some specialise in tech, others in social enterprise, others are generalist. Research which body fits your sector before applying.

**Important:** You apply to the endorsing body first. Only after receiving an endorsement letter do you apply to the Home Office for the visa.

---

## Financial Requirements

The Home Office sets two financial requirements:

**1. Maintenance funds:** GBP 1,270 held in your bank account for 28 consecutive days before you apply. This is the same maintenance requirement as other UK visa routes.

**2. Business funds:** No fixed minimum. The endorsing body assesses whether your business plan is financially credible. You need to show you have enough to execute the plan you have presented.

If you are applying with dependants, additional maintenance funds apply per dependant.

## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Develop your business idea

Your idea must be genuinely innovative. A standard import/export business, a restaurant, or a consultancy replicating existing services will not pass the innovation test. Think: new technology, new process, new market approach, or significant improvement on existing solutions.

### Step 2: Choose an endorsing body

Review the published list. Match your sector and stage to the right body. Some bodies charge application fees (GBP 500-1,500 is common). Some require you to attend an interview or pitch.

### Step 3: Apply to the endorsing body

Submit your business plan, financial projections and supporting documents to the endorsing body. They will assess innovation, viability and scalability. If approved, they issue an endorsement letter.

### Step 4: Apply for the visa

With your endorsement letter, apply online at gov.uk. You will need:
- Valid passport
- Endorsement letter
- Proof of maintenance funds (GBP 1,270 for 28 days)
- English language evidence (B2 level or equivalent)
- Tuberculosis test results (required for Nigerian applicants)

**Visa fee:** GBP 1,486 for main applicant (2026 figure - check gov.uk for current fee)

**Processing time:** Typically 3 weeks from outside the UK

### Step 5: Arrive and build

Your initial visa is valid for 3 years. You must actively develop the business you were endorsed for. You can work for another employer for up to 20 hours per week.

---

## Path to Settlement (ILR)

After 3 years on the Innovator Founder visa, you can apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) if you meet business milestones.

The Home Office requires evidence of at least two of the following:
- Your business has generated at least GBP 50,000 in revenue in the last full year
- Your business has secured at least GBP 50,000 in investment
- Your business has created at least 5 full-time equivalent jobs for settled workers
- Your business has expanded into new markets (at least 2 countries outside the UK)
- Your business has filed at least 1 patent or registered at least 1 trade mark

You will need a new endorsement letter from your endorsing body confirming you have met the milestones.

---

## English Language Requirement

You must demonstrate English at B2 level (upper intermediate) or above. Accepted evidence:
- IELTS Academic or General Training: 5.5 in each component
- Pearson PTE Academic: 59 in each component
- Degree taught in English from a recognised institution
- Nationality of a majority English-speaking country (Nigeria qualifies - check the current list at gov.uk)

Nigerian passport holders should verify current nationality exemption status at gov.uk before applying.

---

## What African Founders Should Know

**Sector fit matters.** Endorsing bodies have sector preferences. A fintech idea targeting African remittance corridors may resonate strongly with a tech-focused endorsing body. A healthcare diagnostics startup may fit a university-linked body better.

**The business must be UK-based.** You are establishing a business in the UK, not running a business from Nigeria with a UK address. The endorsing body will assess whether the business genuinely operates in the UK.

**You cannot buy into an existing business.** The Innovator Founder route is for new businesses. Acquiring a stake in an existing UK company does not qualify.

**Dependants can join you.** Your spouse/partner and children under 18 can apply as dependants. They can work in the UK without restriction.

## Comparison: Innovator Founder vs. Skilled Worker

| Factor | Innovator Founder | Skilled Worker |
|---|---|---|
| Job offer needed | No | Yes |
| Employer sponsor | No | Yes |
| Minimum salary | None | GBP 38,700 (general threshold) |
| Capital required | None (Home Office) | None |
| Path to ILR | 3 years | 5 years |
| Work restriction | Own business + 20h/week other | Employer only |
| Best for | Founders | Employed professionals |
