# France Talent Passport 2026: Multi-Track Visa for Skilled Africans

## Quick Summary

- **What it is:** A family of 10 residence permit subcategories for highly skilled workers, researchers, founders and artists
- **French name:** Passeport Talent
- **Initial validity:** Up to 4 years
- **Path to long-term residence:** 5 years to 10-year carte de resident
- **Key gate:** Employment contract or endorsement, depending on subcategory
- **Source:** france-visas.gouv.fr/en/web/france-visas/talent-passport

**Bottom line:** France has one of the most flexible skilled migration frameworks in Europe. The Talent Passport covers professionals, researchers, founders, artists and investors under one umbrella with different thresholds per track.

---

## The 10 Subcategories

The Talent Passport is not a single visa. It is a framework with 10 distinct subcategories. You apply under the one that matches your profile.

| Subcategory | Who it is for |
|---|---|
| EU Blue Card | Highly qualified employees with salary above threshold |
| Highly Qualified Employee | Employees with salary above reference salary |
| Innovative Economic Project | Startup founders with public body endorsement |
| Researcher / Scientific Expert | Researchers with host agreement from French institution |
| University Professor / Expert | Academics and senior experts |
| Artist / Cultural Professional | Artists with contract from French cultural organisation |
| Investor | Investors meeting capital threshold |
| Economic Mission | Senior executives on assignment |
| Refugee with Recognised Qualifications | Refugees with professional qualifications |
| Family of Talent Passport Holder | Dependants of main applicant |

Most African professionals will apply under EU Blue Card, Highly Qualified Employee, Innovative Economic Project, or Researcher.

---

## EU Blue Card Subcategory

The EU Blue Card is the most structured subcategory with the clearest salary threshold.

**Requirements:**
- Employment contract of at least 1 year with a French employer
- Salary of at least 1.5 times the gross reference salary
- Higher education qualification of at least 3 years (bachelor's equivalent or above)

**Salary threshold (2026):**
The reference salary is EUR 59,373 gross per year (effective 21 August 2025). The EU Blue Card requires 1.5 times this figure.

1.5 x EUR 59,373 = **EUR 89,060 gross per year minimum**

This is a high threshold. It targets senior professionals in tech, finance, engineering and medicine.

**Verify the current figure** at france-visas.gouv.fr before applying. The reference salary is updated when the French minimum wage changes.

---

## Highly Qualified Employee Subcategory

A more accessible track than the EU Blue Card.

**Requirements:**
- Employment contract of at least 3 months with a French employer
- Salary of at least the gross reference salary (not 1.5x)
- Higher education qualification of at least 3 years

**Salary threshold (2026):**
EUR 59,373 gross per year (the reference salary itself, not multiplied).

This is more achievable for mid-career professionals. A software engineer, accountant or marketing director earning EUR 60,000+ qualifies.

---

## Innovative Economic Project Subcategory

For founders and entrepreneurs. No employment contract required.

**Requirements:**
- Endorsement from a recognised French public body (a list is published by the French government)
- A credible innovative project with economic impact in France
- Sufficient personal funds to support yourself

**No salary threshold** applies to this subcategory. The gate is the endorsement quality.

Recognised endorsing bodies include French public investment bank Bpifrance, regional development agencies, and accredited incubators. The full list is at economie.gouv.fr.

---

## Researcher / Scientific Expert Subcategory

For academics and researchers.

**Requirements:**
- Host agreement (convention d'accueil) with a French public or private research organisation
- The organisation must be approved by the French Ministry of Higher Education and Research
- No minimum salary threshold published - the host agreement terms govern

French universities, CNRS, INSERM and major research institutes can issue host agreements.

## Application Process (All Subcategories)

### Step 1: Secure your anchor document

Depending on subcategory:
- EU Blue Card / Highly Qualified Employee: signed employment contract
- Innovative Economic Project: endorsement letter from approved body
- Researcher: host agreement from approved institution

### Step 2: Apply for the long-stay visa (VLS-T)

Apply at the French consulate in Nigeria (Lagos or Abuja). You will need:
- Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity beyond intended stay)
- Anchor document (contract, endorsement or host agreement)
- Proof of qualifications (degree certificates, transcripts)
- Proof of accommodation in France
- Proof of sufficient funds (bank statements)
- Passport photos
- Completed visa application form (via france-visas.gouv.fr)

**Visa fee:** EUR 99 (long-stay visa fee - verify current amount at france-visas.gouv.fr)

**Processing time:** 2-4 weeks from the French consulate in Lagos

### Step 3: Validate on arrival

Within 3 months of arriving in France, validate your VLS-T online via the ANEF portal (administration-etrangers-en-france.interieur.gouv.fr). This converts your visa into a residence permit.

**Validation fee:** EUR 200 (tax stamp - timbre fiscal)

### Step 4: Receive residence permit

After validation, you receive a residence permit (titre de sejour) valid for up to 4 years. This is your Talent Passport residence permit.

---

## Bringing Your Family

The Talent Passport includes a family subcategory. Your spouse/partner and dependent children can apply simultaneously or join you later.

Family members receive a residence permit of the same duration as yours. Your spouse can work in France without restriction - they do not need a separate work permit.

---

## Path to Long-Term Residence

After 5 years of legal residence in France (including time on the Talent Passport), you can apply for a 10-year residence permit (carte de resident).

Requirements for the 10-year permit:
- 5 years of continuous legal residence
- Sufficient integration into French society (language, civic knowledge)
- Stable income
- No serious criminal record

The 10-year permit is renewable and gives you near-permanent status in France.

---

## French Language

The Talent Passport does not require French language proficiency at the application stage. However:
- Daily life in France is significantly easier with French
- The 10-year residence permit application requires evidence of integration, which includes language
- Many employers outside Paris require French

Recommended: reach B1 French within your first 2 years in France.

## Comparison: France Talent Passport vs. Germany Opportunity Card

| Factor | France Talent Passport | Germany Opportunity Card |
|---|---|---|
| Job offer needed | Yes (most subcategories) | No |
| Salary threshold | EUR 59,373+ (HQE) | EUR 1,091/month (living funds) |
| Qualification required | 3-year degree | Recognised qualification |
| Initial validity | 4 years | 1 year |
| Path to permanent residence | 5 years | 5 years |
| Language required | No (at application) | A2 German minimum |
| Best for | Professionals with job offer | Professionals without job offer |

## What African Professionals Should Know

**The salary thresholds are Paris-level.** EUR 59,373 is achievable in Paris tech, finance and consulting. It is harder to reach in regional cities or in sectors like education and social work.

**The EU Blue Card threshold is high.** EUR 89,060 targets senior professionals. If your offer is below this, apply under Highly Qualified Employee instead.

**Endorsement for founders takes time.** The Innovative Economic Project endorsement process can take 2-3 months. Plan ahead.

**Nigeria has two French consulates.** Lagos (for Lagos, Ogun, Oyo, Osun, Ondo, Ekiti, Kwara) and Abuja (for all other states). Check which consulate covers your state before booking an appointment.
