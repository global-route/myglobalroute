Express Entry is the most discussed Canada immigration route — but for Nigerian nurses, it is often not the most accessible one. Provincial Nominee Programs have dedicated healthcare streams that can be faster and have lower CRS requirements.

A provincial nomination adds 600 CRS points to your Express Entry profile. That effectively guarantees an Invitation to Apply regardless of your base CRS score.

Primary source: [canada.ca — Provincial nominees](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/provincial-nominees.html)

## The nursing registration barrier

Before any Canadian immigration pathway for nurses, there is a prerequisite: registration with the provincial nursing regulatory body.

Each province has its own regulatory body:
- Ontario: College of Nurses of Ontario (CNO) — `cno.org`
- British Columbia: BC College of Nurses and Midwives (BCCNM) — `bccnm.ca`
- Alberta: College of Registered Nurses of Alberta (CRNA) — `nurses.ab.ca`
- Manitoba: College of Registered Nurses of Manitoba (CRNM) — `crnm.mb.ca`

The registration process typically takes 3-6 months and requires:
- Credential assessment (your nursing degree evaluated against Canadian standards)
- English proficiency (IELTS or CELBAN — the Canadian English Language Benchmark Assessment for Nurses)
- Jurisprudence exam (knowledge of Canadian nursing law and ethics)
- Possibly a supervised practice period

Start this process before applying for any immigration pathway. It is the rate-limiting step.

## Ontario — OINP Healthcare stream

**Programme:** Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program (OINP) — Employer Job Offer streams include healthcare occupations.

**How it works:** Ontario employers in healthcare can nominate foreign workers directly. The employer must have a job offer for a permanent, full-time position. The worker must have a valid job offer and meet the occupation requirements.

**NOC codes covered:** Registered nurses (NOC 31301), licensed practical nurses (NOC 32101), and other regulated health professions.

**Requirements:** Job offer from an Ontario employer, registration with CNO (or application in progress), language proficiency, work experience.

**Verify at:** `ontario.ca/page/oinp-employer-job-offer-streams`

## British Columbia — BC PNP Health Authority stream

**Programme:** BC Provincial Nominee Program — Health Authority stream.

**How it works:** BC's six health authorities (Fraser Health, Interior Health, Island Health, Northern Health, Providence Health Care, Vancouver Coastal Health) can directly nominate healthcare workers. The health authority acts as the employer and the nominating body.

**How to apply:** Apply directly to the health authority's international recruitment team, not to BC PNP directly. The health authority submits the nomination on your behalf.

**Requirements:** Job offer from a BC health authority, registration with BCCNM (or application in progress), relevant work experience.

**Verify at:** `welcomebc.ca/immigrate-to-b-c/bc-pnp/skills-immigration/health-authority`

## Alberta — AAIP Healthcare stream

**Programme:** Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP) — Alberta Opportunity Stream covers healthcare workers with Alberta job offers.

**How it works:** Nurses with a job offer from an Alberta employer and temporary work authorisation in Alberta can apply for a provincial nomination.

**Requirements:** Job offer from an Alberta employer, temporary work permit or authorisation, registration with CRNA (or application in progress), language proficiency, work experience.

**Verify at:** `alberta.ca/aaip-alberta-opportunity-stream`

## Manitoba — MPNP Skilled Workers stream

**Programme:** Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program (MPNP) — Skilled Workers in Manitoba stream.

**How it works:** Nurses working in Manitoba on a temporary work permit can apply for nomination. Manitoba has active recruitment of internationally trained nurses.

**Requirements:** Working in Manitoba in a healthcare role, temporary work permit, registration with CRNM (or application in progress), language proficiency, settlement funds.

**Verify at:** `immigratemanitoba.com/immigrate-to-manitoba/skilled-workers`

## The typical pathway for a Nigerian nurse

1. **Register with a provincial nursing body** — start with CNO (Ontario) or BCCNM (BC) as they have the most active international recruitment. Allow 3-6 months.

2. **Secure a job offer** — contact hospital HR departments, health authority international recruitment teams, or use healthcare recruitment agencies that specialise in Canadian placements. Confirm the employer is willing to support your immigration.

3. **Apply for a temporary work permit** — while your PR application is processed, you will likely need a temporary work permit to start working. Your employer can support a Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) or you may qualify under an LMIA-exempt category.

4. **Apply for provincial nomination** — once you have the job offer and temporary work authorisation, apply to the relevant PNP stream.

5. **Receive nomination, apply for PR** — a provincial nomination adds 600 CRS points to your Express Entry profile, guaranteeing an ITA. Apply for permanent residence.
