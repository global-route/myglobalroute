# Germany Chancenkarte vs. Canada Express Entry 2026

## The Decision in One Paragraph

If you need permanent residency fast and have a strong profile (degree, 2+ years experience, IELTS 7.5+), **Canada Express Entry** gets you PR in 6-12 months. If your CRS score is below 480, you want to start working in Europe immediately, or you cannot afford the upfront costs of the Canadian process, **Germany Chancenkarte** is the more accessible entry point - though PR takes 5 years.

---

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Factor | Germany Chancenkarte | Canada Express Entry |
|---|---|---|
| Job offer required | No | No |
| Minimum points to qualify | 6 points (Chancenkarte scale) | 67 points (FSWP scale) + CRS ~480+ for ITA |
| Upfront government fees | ~€200 | ~CAD $1,215 |
| Processing time to permit | 2-4 weeks | 6 months from ITA |
| What you get on approval | 1-year job search permit | Permanent residency |
| Time to PR | ~5 years | 6-12 months |
| Time to citizenship | 8 years | 3 years after PR |
| Minimum salary (once employed) | €41,211/year (EU Blue Card threshold) | No minimum (PR is already granted) |
| Language requirement | German A2 minimum (B1 recommended) | English CLB 7 (IELTS 6.0 each band) |
| Credential assessment required | ANABIN database check (free) | WES ECA (CAD $220) |
| Settlement funds required | €1,091/month (blocked account) | CAD $14,690 (family size 1) |

## The Points Systems Explained

### Germany Chancenkarte points

The Chancenkarte uses a simple 4-factor scale. You need 6 points to qualify via the points route (alternatively, full recognition of a foreign qualification in Germany provides direct access without points).

| Factor | Max points |
|---|---|
| Recognised foreign qualification | 4 |
| Work experience (2+ years in field) | 3 |
| German language (B2) | 3 |
| Age under 35 | 2 |
| Previous stay in Germany | 1 |
| Spouse's qualification | 1 |

**Typical African tech professional:** Foreign degree (4) + 3 years experience (3) + A2 German (0) + age 30 (2) = **9 points** - qualifies comfortably.

### Canada Express Entry CRS

The CRS is a 1,200-point scale. Recent draw cut-offs have been in the 480-520 range for all-program draws.

| Factor | Max points (no spouse) |
|---|---|
| Age (peak: 20-29) | 110 |
| Education | 150 |
| Language (English/French) | 136 |
| Work experience | 80 |
| Transferable skills | 100 |
| Additional (job offer, PNP) | 600 |

**Typical African tech professional (age 32, BSc, 5 years experience, IELTS 7.5):**
- Age 32: 100 points
- Bachelor's degree: 120 points
- IELTS 7.5 (CLB 9): 136 points
- 5 years experience: 70 points
- **Total: ~426 points** - below current cut-offs without PNP or job offer

To reach 480+, this profile needs either a Provincial Nominee Program nomination (+600 points, automatic ITA) or a Canadian job offer (+200 points).

## Choose Germany Chancenkarte if...

- Your CRS score is below 480 and you cannot secure a PNP nomination
- You want to start working in Europe within 2-3 months
- Your upfront budget is limited (Chancenkarte costs ~€200 vs. CAD $1,500+ for Express Entry)
- You are willing to learn German (A2 minimum, B1 strongly recommended for job search)
- You are comfortable with a 5-year path to PR rather than 1 year
- You work in IT, engineering, healthcare or skilled trades - Germany has acute shortages in all of these

**Salary reality in Germany:** Once employed, tech salaries range from €40,000-€65,000/year. The EU Blue Card (available after finding a job) requires a minimum salary of €41,211/year for most occupations (2026 threshold).

---

## Choose Canada Express Entry if...

- Your CRS score is 480+ (or you can secure a PNP nomination)
- You want permanent residency within 12 months, not 5 years
- You have strong English (IELTS 7.5+ / CLB 9) - this is worth 136 CRS points
- You can afford the upfront costs (CAD $1,500+ in fees, plus WES assessment)
- You want a faster path to citizenship (3 years after PR vs. 8 years in Germany)
- You have family you want to sponsor - Canada's family sponsorship system is more accessible

**Salary reality in Canada:** Tech salaries in Toronto and Vancouver range from CAD $70,000-$110,000/year. Cost of living is also higher than Germany.

---

## The Dual-Track Strategy

These routes are not mutually exclusive. Many African professionals apply to both simultaneously:

1. Submit Chancenkarte application (2-4 weeks to decision)
2. Simultaneously build your Express Entry profile and enter the pool
3. If Chancenkarte approves first, travel to Germany and begin job search
4. If you receive a Canadian ITA while in Germany, you can still submit the full application remotely
5. Whichever produces the better outcome first, pursue that path

The main constraint is cost - running both processes simultaneously requires approximately €200 (Chancenkarte) + CAD $1,500 (Express Entry preparation) upfront.

---

## Language: The Deciding Factor

For many African professionals, language is the practical deciding factor:

- **English speakers (IELTS 7.5+):** Canada is the stronger choice. Your language score alone is worth 136 CRS points.
- **Willing to learn German:** Chancenkarte becomes viable. A2 German takes 4-6 weeks of intensive study. B1 (which significantly improves your job search in Germany) takes 3-4 months.
- **Neither strong English nor German:** Both routes become harder. Focus on language first.
