DAAD (Deutscher Akademischer Austauschdienst - German Academic Exchange Service) is Germany's primary international scholarship organisation. It funds thousands of international students annually, with Nigerian students among the most successful applicants. Here is what DAAD actually offers and how to apply.

## DAAD scholarship programmes for Nigerian students

DAAD offers multiple programmes. The most relevant for Nigerian applicants are:

### 1. EPOS - Development-Related Postgraduate Courses

**What it is:** Scholarships for postgraduate study (Masters or PhD) in Germany in fields relevant to development.

**Eligible fields:** Engineering, agriculture, economics, public health, environmental sciences, urban planning, information technology, and others with a development relevance.

**What it covers:**
- Monthly stipend: EUR 934/month
- Tuition fees: zero (German public universities)
- Health insurance
- Travel allowance (return flight)
- Study and research allowance

**Requirements:**
- Bachelor's degree with strong academic record
- At least 2 years of relevant work experience
- Commitment to return to Nigeria after completing the degree
- English or German language proficiency (depending on programme)

**Application:** Through the DAAD portal at `https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/`

### 2. DAAD Helmut Schmidt Programme

**What it is:** Scholarships for public policy and good governance studies.

**Target:** Mid-career professionals from developing countries working in public administration, civil society, or international organisations.

**What it covers:** Same as EPOS plus a family allowance if applicable.

### 3. DAAD Research Grants

**What it is:** Short-term research grants for doctoral students and postdoctoral researchers.

**Duration:** 1-6 months.

**What it covers:** Monthly stipend, travel costs, health insurance.

## The DAAD application process

**Step 1 - Find a programme:** Search the DAAD scholarship database at `https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/`

**Step 2 - Find a German university:** For most DAAD scholarships, you need to identify a German university and programme before applying. Contact the relevant department directly.

**Step 3 - Prepare documents:**
- Academic transcripts and degree certificates
- CV/resume
- Motivation letter
- Research proposal (for PhD applications)
- Two academic reference letters
- Language test results
- Passport copy

**Step 4 - Apply:** Most DAAD applications are submitted through the DAAD portal. Deadlines vary by programme - typically October-November for the following academic year.

## What makes a strong DAAD application

> DAAD prioritises applicants who demonstrate a clear connection between their proposed study and development challenges in their home country. The motivation letter must explain how the degree will contribute to Nigeria's development - not just your personal career goals.

**Strong application elements:**
- Specific research question or professional goal related to Nigerian/African development
- Evidence of work experience in a relevant field
- Strong academic record (minimum 3.5/4.0 or equivalent)
- Clear plan to return to Nigeria after completing the degree
