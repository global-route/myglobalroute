Commonwealth Scholarships are funded by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office (FCDO) and administered by the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (CSC). They are available to students from Commonwealth countries including Nigeria for postgraduate study at UK universities.

## Types of Commonwealth Scholarships

### Commonwealth Masters Scholarship

**For:** Masters degree study at a UK university.

**Duration:** 1-2 years depending on the programme.

**What it covers:**
- Full tuition fees
- Monthly living allowance: GBP 1,347 (London) / GBP 1,084 (elsewhere)
- Return airfare
- Thesis grant (if applicable)
- Health insurance

### Commonwealth PhD Scholarship

**For:** PhD research at a UK university.

**Duration:** Up to 3 years.

**What it covers:** Same as Masters scholarship plus a research allowance.

### Commonwealth Distance Learning Scholarship

**For:** Part-time distance learning Masters at a UK university while remaining in Nigeria.

**What it covers:** Tuition fees only (no living allowance - you remain in Nigeria).

## Requirements

**Academic requirements:**
- Bachelor's degree with minimum 2:1 (upper second class) or equivalent
- Strong academic record
- Relevant work experience (preferred but not always required)

**Development impact:**
- Clear statement of how your proposed study will contribute to development in Nigeria
- This is the most important non-academic element of your application

**Application documents:**
- Completed CSC application form
- Academic transcripts and degree certificates
- CV/resume
- Personal statement
- Development impact statement
- Two academic reference letters
- English language test results (if applicable)

**Primary source:** `https://cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk/scholarships/commonwealth-masters-scholarships/`

## The development impact statement - what the CSC wants

> The CSC is not just funding your education - it is investing in Nigeria's development. Your development impact statement must answer: what specific problem in Nigeria will you address with this degree, and how?

**Strong development impact statements:**
- Specific: "I will use this degree to improve maternal health outcomes in rural Kano State by training community health workers in evidence-based obstetric care"
- Measurable: "I aim to train 200 community health workers in the first 3 years after returning"
- Credible: Based on your existing work experience and professional network in Nigeria

**Weak development impact statements:**
- Generic: "I will contribute to Nigeria's development"
- Vague: "I will share my knowledge with colleagues"
- Personal: Focused on your career advancement rather than Nigeria's development
