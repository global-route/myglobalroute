## What is DSU?

**DSU** = *Diritto allo Studio Universitario* (Right to University Education)

It's a **regional scholarship program** run by Italian local governments to ensure low-income students can study for free. Unlike most scholarships that only cover tuition, DSU covers **everything**:

- ✅ Full tuition waiver
- ✅ Free university accommodation (dorm)
- ✅ Free meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner) at university canteens
- ✅ Cash stipend (€5,400-€7,200/year)
- ✅ Free textbooks and study materials (some regions)
- ✅ Transport pass (some regions)

**What makes it special?**  
It's **means-tested**, not merit-based. If your family income is low, you qualify—even with average grades.

## Who Qualifies?

### Income Requirement

Your family must earn **below the ISEE threshold**:

| Region | ISEE Limit (2026) | Family Income (USD) |
|--------|------------------|---------------------|
| Lombardy (Milan) | €23,626 | ~$25,000/year |
| Emilia-Romagna (Bologna) | €25,000 | ~$27,000/year |
| Tuscany (Florence, Pisa) | €23,000 | ~$24,800/year |
| Lazio (Rome) | €23,500 | ~$25,300/year |

**ISEE** = *Indicatore della Situazione Economica Equivalente* (family economic indicator)

**For African families:** If your parents earn **less than $2,000/month combined**, you likely qualify.

---

### Academic Requirement

| Level | Minimum GPA | Credits Required |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| Bachelor's Year 1 | High school diploma | N/A (first year) |
| Bachelor's Year 2+ | Pass 70% of credits | 25-40 CFU (credits) |
| Master's Year 1 | Bachelor's degree (2.5+/4.0) | N/A |
| Master's Year 2 | Pass 70% of credits | 25-40 CFU |

**Key insight:** You don't need a **high GPA**—just need to **pass most courses**. This is achievable for serious students.

---

### Language Requirement

- **Year 1:** No Italian required (courses often in English)
- **Year 2+:** Italian B1-B2 required (varies by university)

**Solution:** Take free Italian language courses (universities offer them). Many Nigerian students reach B2 in 6-12 months.

---

### Other Requirements

- ✅ Enrolled in an Italian public university
- ✅ Not receiving other full scholarships
- ✅ Family income below threshold
- ✅ Not more than 2 years behind in credits

**Citizenship:** **NOT required**. International students from any country can apply.

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Get University Admission

**Before applying for DSU**, you must:
1. Apply to an Italian public university
2. Get admission letter (conditional or unconditional)
3. Enroll officially

**Timeline:** University applications open Jan-May for September start.

**Top English-taught programs:**
- Engineering (Politecnico di Milano, Politecnico di Torino)
- Business/Economics (Bocconi, Bologna, Pisa)
- Computer Science (Padua, Bologna, Trento)
- Architecture (Milan, Venice)

**Application process:**
- Apply via [Universitaly](https://www.universitaly.it/) portal
- Submit documents (transcripts, diploma, passport)
- Take entrance exam (if required)
- Wait for admission (4-8 weeks)

---

### Step 2: Calculate Your ISEE

**ISEE** = Income + Assets + Family Size calculation

**For international students:**
- Download ISEE-U form from [INPS website](https://www.inps.it/)
- Fill in:
  - Family income (convert to EUR)
  - Property value (home, land)
  - Number of family members
  - Student status

**Example calculation (Nigerian family of 4):**

```
Father's income: ₦500,000/month × 12 = ₦6M/year = €7,900/year
Mother's income: ₦300,000/month × 12 = ₦3.6M/year = €4,740/year
Total income: €12,640/year
Property value: ₦15M family home = €19,737
Family size: 4

ISEE calculation:
(Income €12,640 + Property €19,737 × 20%) ÷ 2.46 (4-person scale) = €7,747

Result: €7,747 << €23,000 threshold → QUALIFIED
```

**Get ISEE certification from:**
- Italian embassy in Nigeria (after admission)
- CAF office in Italy (after arrival)

---

### Step 3: Apply for DSU

**When:** July-September (after university admission)

**Where:** Regional DSU office (ER.GO, EDISU, etc.)

**Documents needed:**
- [ ] University enrollment proof
- [ ] ISEE-U certification
- [ ] Passport copy
- [ ] Family income documentation
  - Parents' salary slips (last 3 months)
  - Bank statements (last 6 months)
  - Tax returns (if applicable)
  - Sworn declaration of income (notarized)
- [ ] Property ownership documents (if any)
- [ ] High school diploma + transcripts (translated)

**How to apply:**
1. Create account on regional DSU portal:
   - Emilia-Romagna: [ER.GO](https://www.er-go.it/)
   - Lombardy: [DSU Lombardia](https://www.dsu.regione.lombardia.it/)
   - Tuscany: [DSU Toscana](https://www.dsu.toscana.it/)
   - Lazio: [DiSCo Lazio](https://www.laziodisco.it/)

2. Upload documents online
3. Submit application by deadline (usually Aug 31)
4. Wait for ranking list (published in October)

---

### Step 4: Get Ranked

DSU is **partially competitive**. You're ranked based on:
- ISEE score (lower = better ranking)
- Academic performance (for returning students)
- Distance from university (some regions give priority to far students)

**Acceptance rate (2025 data):**
- Bologna (ER.GO): 38% of applicants
- Milan (DSU Lombardy): 42%
- Pisa (DSU Toscana): 35%
- Rome (DiSCo Lazio): 30%

**Key insight:** If your ISEE is **below €15,000**, you have **70-80% chance** of winning the scholarship.

## Income Calculation for Africans

**Challenge:** Nigerian/Ghanaian/Kenyan families often have **informal income** (no tax returns, no pay slips).

### How to Document Income

#### Option 1: Salaried Parents
- Provide salary slips (last 3-6 months)
- Bank statements showing deposits
- Employer letter confirming salary
- Tax ID or PAYE slips

#### Option 2: Self-Employed Parents
- Business registration documents
- Bank statements (business + personal)
- Sworn affidavit declaring income
- Invoices or receipts (if available)

#### Option 3: Informal Income
- Sworn declaration of income (notarized)
- Bank statements showing cash flow
- Letter from community leader/church confirming family status
- Property valuation (if you own home)

**Important:** Italian embassies understand African families often have **informal income**. They accept sworn declarations if notarized properly.

## Pros & Cons

### Pros ✅

1. **Covers EVERYTHING:** Tuition, housing, food, cash—no gaps
2. **Low barrier:** Need low income, not high grades
3. **Cash stipend:** You get paid while studying
4. **No upfront cost:** Unlike Germany (blocked account), Italy has no huge upfront requirement
5. **Post-study work:** 1-year job search visa after graduation
6. **EU access:** Italian degree = recognized across Europe
7. **Learn a new language:** Italian is useful for career
8. **Strong alumni network:** Large African student community

---

### Cons ❌

1. **Competitive:** Only 30-40% of applicants win DSU
2. **Italian language:** Need B1-B2 by year 2 (extra effort)
3. **Income documentation:** Can be tricky for informal African income
4. **Lower stipend than Germany work income:** €450-€600/month vs. €800-€1,200 from part-time work in Germany
5. **Italy's economy:** Weaker than Germany/UK (fewer post-grad jobs)
6. **Bureaucracy:** Italian admin is slow and frustrating

## FAQ

### Q: What if my family earns more than €23,000/year?

**A:** You won't qualify for **full DSU**, but you can apply for:
- **Partial DSU:** Reduced benefits (meal vouchers + partial tuition waiver)
- **Standard scholarships:** Merit-based scholarships (€3,000-€6,000/year)
- **Part-time work:** International students can work 20 hours/week

---

### Q: Do I have to speak Italian to apply?

**A:** No. You can:
- Apply in English
- Study in English-taught programs (Year 1)
- Learn Italian during Year 1 (free university courses)
- By Year 2, you'll need B1-B2 for continued DSU eligibility

---

### Q: Can I work while on DSU scholarship?

**A:** Yes! DSU allows part-time work (20 hours/week).

**Typical earnings:**
- Cafe/restaurant: €8-€12/hour
- Tutoring: €15-€25/hour
- Freelance (design, coding): €20-€40/hour

**Extra income:** €400-€800/month on top of DSU stipend

---

### Q: What happens if I fail courses?

**A:** You lose DSU if you fail to earn **70% of required credits**.

**Example:**
- Year 1 requires 60 CFU (credits)
- You must pass 42 CFU (70%)
- If you pass only 30 CFU → lose DSU for Year 2

**Solution:** Take studies seriously. Most Nigerian students pass if they attend classes and study.

---

### Q: Can I bring my spouse/children?

**A:** Not on DSU. Student visa doesn't allow dependents initially.

**Options:**
- Spouse gets separate student/work visa
- Wait until you graduate and get a job (then sponsor family)

---

### Q: How does DSU compare to Germany?

| Factor | Germany | Italy DSU |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Upfront cost | €11,300 (blocked account) | €0-€1,000 |
| Tuition | €0 | €0 (with DSU) |
| Living costs | €934/month (you pay) | €0 (they pay you) |
| Cash income | €800-€1,200/month (from work) | €450-€600/month (stipend) + work |
| Language barrier | German (difficult) | Italian (easier) |
| Job market | Strong | Moderate |
| Post-study visa | 18 months | 12 months |
| **Best for** | **Those with €11K upfront** | **Those with €0-€1K upfront** |

**Verdict:** If you have **€11,000 saved**, Germany might be better (stronger economy). If you have **< €2,000**, Italy DSU is your golden ticket.
