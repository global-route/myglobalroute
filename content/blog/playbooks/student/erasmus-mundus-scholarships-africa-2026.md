Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degrees are among the most valuable scholarships available to African students. Fully funded by the European Commission, they provide tuition, a monthly stipend, travel costs and health insurance - while you study at two or three European universities in different countries.

## What Erasmus Mundus actually is

Erasmus Mundus is not a single scholarship - it is a framework for Joint Master Degree programmes funded by the European Commission. Each programme is a consortium of 2-4 European universities that jointly deliver a master's degree.

**What makes it different from other scholarships:**
- You study at multiple universities in different European countries
- The degree is jointly awarded by all partner universities
- The scholarship is funded by the EU, not by individual universities
- African students are specifically targeted as a priority group

## What the scholarship covers

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Tuition fees | 100% covered at all partner universities |
| Monthly living allowance | EUR 1,400/month |
| Travel contribution | EUR 2,000 (nearby countries) / EUR 3,000 (distant countries including Africa) |
| Installation costs | EUR 1,000 (one-time, first year) |
| Health insurance | Covered |
| **Total value (2-year programme)** | **Approximately EUR 40,000-45,000** |

## How to find Erasmus Mundus programmes

The European Commission maintains a catalogue of all Erasmus Mundus programmes at `https://www.eacea.ec.europa.eu/scholarships/erasmus-mundus-catalogue_en`.

**Programmes relevant for African applicants include:**
- IMLEX (Imaging and Light in Extended Reality) - technology
- EMJMD in Public Health - healthcare
- EMJMD in Sustainable Energy - engineering
- EMJMD in International Development - social sciences
- EMJMD in Water and Coastal Management - environment

There are over 150 active programmes across all disciplines.

## Application requirements

**Academic requirements:**
- Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) in a relevant field
- Strong academic record (typically 3.5/4.0 GPA or equivalent)
- English language proficiency (IELTS 6.5+ or equivalent)

**Application documents:**
- Completed application form (programme-specific)
- Academic transcripts and degree certificates
- CV/resume
- Motivation letter (the most important document)
- Two academic reference letters
- English language test results
- Passport copy

## The motivation letter - what makes the difference

The motivation letter is the most important part of your Erasmus Mundus application. Selection committees read hundreds of letters from qualified candidates. Yours must answer three questions clearly:

1. **Why this programme?** Specific reasons related to the programme's content, partner universities, and how it fits your career goals.
2. **Why you?** Your academic background, work experience, and specific skills that make you a strong candidate.
3. **What will you do with it?** How will this degree contribute to your career and to your country/region?

> Generic motivation letters that could apply to any programme are the most common reason strong candidates are rejected. Research each programme thoroughly and write a specific letter for each application.
