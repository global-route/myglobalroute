## Why Form a US LLC from Nigeria?

### Problem: Nigerian Payment Barriers

As a Nigerian entrepreneur, you face these barriers:

❌ **Stripe banned in Nigeria** (can't accept online payments)  
❌ **PayPal limited** (receive only, no withdrawals)  
❌ **Domiciliary account fees** (high forex charges)  
❌ **Trust issues** (clients hesitant to pay Nigerian companies)  
❌ **Contract limitations** (can't sign US contracts easily)

### Solution: US LLC

A US LLC solves these problems:

✅ **Stripe account** (accept credit cards, subscriptions)  
✅ **PayPal business account** (full send/receive/withdraw)  
✅ **US bank account** (Mercury, Relay, Wise Business)  
✅ **Global credibility** ("MyCompany LLC" vs. "Ade's Services")  
✅ **US contracts** (can sign with US clients, platforms, investors)  
✅ **Lower fees** (Stripe 2.9% vs. Flutterwave 3.8%)

## Step-by-Step Setup Guide

### Method 1: Stripe Atlas (Easiest, Premium)

**What it is:** Stripe's official company formation service

**Steps:**
1. Go to [stripe.com/atlas](https://stripe.com/atlas)
2. Apply (takes 10 minutes)
3. Pay $500
4. Wait 1-2 weeks
5. Receive:
   - Delaware LLC
   - EIN (tax ID)
   - Stripe account (pre-activated)
   - Mercury bank account
   - IRS tax forms filed

**Pros:**
- ✅ Everything done for you
- ✅ Stripe account included
- ✅ Mercury banking integrated
- ✅ Tax setup handled

**Cons:**
- ❌ Delaware only (higher annual fees)
- ❌ $500 upfront (expensive)
- ❌ Less control over details

**Best for:** Founders who want zero hassle and plan to use Stripe

---

### Method 2: Northwest Registered Agent (Best Value)

**What it is:** Trusted LLC formation service (A+ BBB rating)

**Steps:**
1. Go to [northwestregisteredagent.com](https://www.northwestregisteredagent.com/)
2. Select **Wyoming LLC**
3. Fill in company details:
   - Company name (e.g., "Ade Consulting LLC")
   - Your Nigerian address
   - Email and phone
4. Pay $39 + $100 state fee = **$139 total**
5. Wait 3-5 business days
6. Receive:
   - LLC formation documents
   - EIN (they can get it for you for $70 extra)
   - Operating agreement

**Pros:**
- ✅ Cheapest option ($139)
- ✅ Wyoming (low annual fees)
- ✅ Trusted service (15+ years)
- ✅ Free registered agent (1st year)

**Cons:**
- ❌ You handle EIN yourself (or pay $70 extra)
- ❌ No banking setup (you open accounts separately)

**Best for:** Budget-conscious founders

---

### Method 4: DIY (Cheapest, Most Control)

**What it is:** File directly with Wyoming Secretary of State

**Steps:**

**Step 1: Choose a Company Name**
- Check availability: [wyobiz.wyo.gov](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/Business/FilingSearch.aspx)
- Must end with "LLC"
- Example: "Ade Digital LLC"

**Step 2: Hire a Registered Agent**
- Required (must have US address)
- Options:
  - Northwest Registered Agent: $50/year
  - Incfile: $119/year
  - Registered Agents Inc: $99/year

**Step 3: File Articles of Organization**
- Go to [wyobiz.wyo.gov](https://wyobiz.wyo.gov/)
- File online
- Fee: $100
- Processing: 1-3 days

**Step 4: Get an EIN (Tax ID)**
- Go to [IRS website](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online)
- Fill in SS-4 form
- **Problem:** IRS requires a US phone number
- **Solution:** Use Google Voice (get free US number)
- Wait 10 minutes → EIN issued

**Step 5: Create Operating Agreement**
- Download free template: [rocketlawyer.com](https://www.rocketlawyer.com/)
- Customize for single-member LLC
- Sign and store (not filed with state)

**Total cost:** $150-$200 (Year 1)

- ❌ International wires: $35 fee

**How to open:**
1. Go to [mercury.com](https://mercury.com/)

3. Wait 3-5 days for approval

## Tax Implications

### US Taxes

**Good news:** If your LLC has **no US-sourced income**, you pay **$0 US tax**.

**What counts as US-sourced income?**
- ❌ Services to US clients (if you're outside US) → **NOT US income**
- ❌ Digital products sold to US customers → **NOT US income**
- ✅ Rental income from US property → US income (taxed)
- ✅ US employees' salaries → US income (taxed)

**Example:**
- You're a Nigerian web developer
- US client pays you $50,000/year
- You do work from Nigeria
- **US tax owed:** $0

**Why?**  
IRS taxes based on **where work is performed**, not where client is located. Since you work from Nigeria, it's foreign income.

---

### Filing Requirements

Even if you owe $0 tax, you must file **Form 5472** annually.

**Form 5472:**
- Reports transactions with foreign owners (you)
- Due: March 15 (if calendar year LLC)
- Penalty for not filing: $25,000
- **How to file:** Use [Tax1099.com](https://www.tax1099.com/) ($200-$400) or DIY

**Pro tip:** Hire a US tax accountant ($200-$500/year) to handle this.

---

### Nigerian Taxes

**You STILL pay tax in Nigeria** on your worldwide income.

**How it works:**
- Your US LLC income is taxed as **personal income** in Nigeria
- Report on annual tax return to FIRS
- Tax rate: 7-24% (based on income bracket)

**Example:**
- US LLC earns $50,000
- You withdraw to Nigeria
- Taxable in Nigeria at ~24% = ₦8.5M tax

**Avoid double taxation:** US-Nigeria tax treaty exists, but consult Nigerian tax advisor.

## Accepting Payments (Stripe, PayPal)

### Stripe

**Setup:**
1. Go to [stripe.com](https://stripe.com/)
2. Sign up with:
   - US LLC details
   - EIN
   - Mercury/Relay bank account
3. Verify identity (passport, address proof)
4. Start accepting payments

**Fees:**
- 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
- Instant payouts: 1.5%
- International cards: +1.5%

**Withdrawal to Nigeria:**
- Stripe → Mercury → Wise → Nigerian bank
- Total fees: ~2-3%

---

### PayPal

**Setup:**
1. Go to [paypal.com/business](https://www.paypal.com/business)
2. Sign up as US business
3. Link Mercury/Relay account
4. Start receiving payments

**Fees:**
- 2.9% + $0.30 (same as Stripe)

**Withdrawal:**
- PayPal → Wise → Nigerian bank (3-5 days, 1-2% fee)

## FAQ

### Q: Can I form a US LLC without visiting the US?

**A:** Yes! Everything is done online. You never need to step foot in America.

---

### Q: Do I need a US visa?

**A:** No. Owning a US LLC does NOT give you the right to live/work in the US. It's just a business entity.

---

### Q: Can I hire employees?

**A:** Yes, but:
- Nigerian employees → pay in Nigeria, report on Nigerian payroll
- US employees → must register for US payroll (complicated, expensive)

Most Nigerian LLC owners work solo or hire contractors (not employees).

---

### Q: What if I want to move to the US later?

**A:** Your LLC **does not** qualify you for a visa. You'd need:
- E-2 visa (requires $100K+ investment + proof of business growth)
- L-1 visa (requires physical office + employees)
- EB-5 visa (requires $800K-$1M investment)

An LLC alone is not a path to US residency.

---

### Q: Can I get a US business credit card?

**A:** Difficult. Most US banks require:
- SSN (Social Security Number)
- US credit history

**Workaround:** Some founders use:
- Mercury debit card (works like credit card)
- PayPal Credit (if you have US PayPal)
- Build US credit over time

---

### Q: Is this legal?

**A:** 100% legal. Foreigners can own US LLCs. It's encouraged by US government (creates jobs, brings capital).
