Ras Al Khaimah (RAK) is an emirate in the UAE, adjacent to Dubai. Its economic zone — RAKEZ — offers company formation with one specific feature that attracts attention: the ownership register is not public.

In the UK, anyone can look up a company's directors and shareholders on Companies House in seconds. In RAK, the ownership register is sealed. Your name is filed — but only the UAE government and tax authorities can see it.

This is the privacy that RAK free zone companies offer. It is privacy from the public, not from governments or tax authorities.

Primary source: [rakez.com](https://rakez.com)

## What RAKEZ is

RAKEZ (RAK Economic Zone) is the free zone authority for Ras Al Khaimah. It is one of approximately 45 free zones in the UAE. Free zones allow 100% foreign ownership of companies — unlike UAE mainland companies which historically required a local sponsor (this requirement has been relaxed in many sectors since 2021, but free zones remain the standard structure for foreign-owned businesses).

RAKEZ is one of the more affordable UAE free zones. It is popular with freelancers, consultants, and small trading companies.

## The privacy — what it means and what it does not mean

**What it means:** Your name as a company owner does not appear on any publicly searchable database. A competitor, a journalist, or a stranger cannot look up your company and find your name.

**What it does not mean:** Your name is not hidden from the UAE government. Under UAE Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) regulations introduced in 2020, all companies must file their beneficial owners with the relevant authority within 60 days of incorporation. This information is held by the government — not published publicly.

**CRS:** The UAE participates in the Common Reporting Standard (CRS), the OECD framework for automatic exchange of financial information between tax authorities. If you are a Nigerian tax resident and you have a bank account in the name of your RAK company, the UAE bank will report that account to the UAE tax authority, which will exchange the information with FIRS (Federal Inland Revenue Service) in Nigeria.

A RAK company does not protect you from tax obligations in your country of residence. It protects your ownership information from public view.

## Legitimate use cases

- **Business privacy from competitors:** You do not want competitors to know which companies you own or what business structures you use.
- **Separation of personal and business assets:** A UAE free zone company creates a legal separation between your personal assets and business liabilities.
- **International invoicing:** A UAE company can invoice international clients in USD or EUR without the friction of Nigerian banking infrastructure.
- **UAE residency:** Some RAKEZ licence types allow you to apply for a UAE investor visa, which gives you UAE residency.

## What it costs

RAKEZ fees vary by licence type. Approximate ranges (verify current fees at `rakez.com`):

| Licence type | Approximate setup cost (USD) | Annual renewal (USD) |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancer licence | 900-1,500 | 900-1,500 |
| Consultancy / service company | 1,500-2,500 | 1,500-2,500 |
| Trading company (flexi-desk) | 2,500-4,000 | 2,500-4,000 |
| Trading company (physical office) | 4,000-8,000+ | 4,000-8,000+ |

Additional costs:
- UAE bank account opening: USD 0-500 (some banks charge setup fees)
- Registered agent (if using one): USD 300-800/year
- Accounting/compliance: USD 500-2,000/year depending on complexity

Total first-year cost for a basic consultancy licence: approximately USD 2,000-4,000.

## Comparison with other structures

| Structure | Public register | CRS reporting | 100% foreign ownership | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RAK free zone (RAKEZ) | Private | Yes | Yes | USD 900-2,500/year |
| UAE mainland company | Private | Yes | Yes (most sectors since 2021) | Higher |
| UK limited company | Public (Companies House) | Yes | Yes | GBP 12/year + accounting |
| US LLC (Wyoming/Delaware) | Varies by state | Yes (for non-US owners) | Yes | USD 50-300/year + registered agent |

The UK and US options are significantly cheaper. The RAK advantage is specifically the private register — if that matters for your situation, RAK is worth the cost. If it does not matter, a UK or US company is cheaper and simpler.

## How to form a RAKEZ company

1. **Choose your licence type** at `rakez.com`. Freelancer, consultancy, trading, and industrial licences are available.
2. **Submit application online** at `rakez.com/en/set-up-your-business`. Required documents: passport copy, passport photo, business plan (for some licence types).
3. **Pay fees** — RAKEZ accepts card and bank transfer.
4. **Receive licence** — typically within 1-3 business days for standard applications.
5. **Open a UAE bank account** — required to operate the company. Emirates NBD, Mashreq, and RAK Bank are common choices. Bank account opening takes 2-4 weeks and requires in-person or video verification.
6. **File UBO registration** — within 60 days of incorporation, file your beneficial ownership information with RAKEZ.

## CRS — the mandatory disclosure

If you are a Nigerian tax resident and you open a UAE bank account in the name of your RAK company, FIRS will receive information about that account annually under CRS.

This does not mean you owe tax on the company's income in Nigeria automatically — it depends on Nigerian tax law and your specific situation. But it does mean the account is not invisible to Nigerian tax authorities.

If you are considering a RAK company for tax planning purposes, consult a qualified tax adviser who understands both UAE and Nigerian tax law before proceeding. This post does not constitute tax advice.
