Antigua and Barbuda has operated a Citizenship by Investment Programme since 1981 — one of the oldest in the Caribbean. For high-net-worth Nigerians seeking a second passport with strong global mobility, it is a real option.

The USD 230,000 figure that circulates on social media is the minimum NDF donation for a single applicant. The total cost for a family is significantly higher. This post covers the full picture.

Primary source: [cip.gov.ag](https://cip.gov.ag) — verify all figures here before making any investment decision.

## What you get

An Antigua and Barbuda passport provides:

- Full citizenship — not residency, not a visa, actual citizenship
- Visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 150+ countries (verify current count at Henley Passport Index — `henleypassportindex.com`)
- Access to Schengen Europe (26 countries) visa-free
- Access to the UK (verify current policy — see FAQ above)
- Access to Singapore visa-free
- The right to live and work in Antigua and Barbuda
- The right to pass citizenship to children born after naturalisation

## Investment routes

Antigua offers four investment routes. The NDF donation is the most commonly discussed because it has the lowest headline figure.

| Route | Minimum investment | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| NDF donation (single applicant) | USD 230,000 | Non-refundable donation to National Development Fund |
| NDF donation (family of 4) | USD 230,000 | Same amount for up to 4 family members |
| Real estate | USD 300,000 | Must hold for 5 years; property must be in approved development |
| Business investment | USD 1,500,000 | Direct investment in approved business |
| University of the West Indies Fund | USD 150,000 | For families with one university-age dependent |

The NDF route is the most popular because it has the lowest cost and no holding period. The real estate route requires a 5-year hold before you can sell.

## The full cost — not just the donation

The USD 230,000 NDF donation is not the total cost. Add:

| Fee | Approximate amount |
|---|---|
| NDF donation (single applicant) | USD 230,000 |
| Government processing fee | USD 30,000 |
| Due diligence fee (main applicant) | USD 7,500 |
| Due diligence fee (per dependent 18+) | USD 7,500 |
| Authorised agent fee | USD 20,000-50,000 |
| Passport and certificate fees | USD 500-1,000 |
| **Total (single applicant, approximate)** | **USD 290,000-320,000** |

For a family of four (two adults, two minor children):

| Fee | Approximate amount |
|---|---|
| NDF donation | USD 230,000 |
| Government processing fees | USD 50,000-70,000 |
| Due diligence fees (2 adults) | USD 15,000 |
| Authorised agent fee | USD 30,000-60,000 |
| **Total (family of 4, approximate)** | **USD 325,000-375,000** |

These are estimates. Verify current fees at `cip.gov.ag` and with an authorised agent.

## The due diligence process

Antigua conducts background checks on all applicants. The due diligence process includes:

- Criminal background check
- Source of funds verification — you must demonstrate that the investment funds are from legitimate sources
- Politically Exposed Person (PEP) screening
- International sanctions screening

The due diligence process takes 3-6 months. Applications with complex source-of-funds histories take longer. Applications from individuals on sanctions lists are rejected.

This is where the Allen Stanford story is instructive.

## The Allen Stanford lesson

In 2009, Antigua knighted Allen Stanford — a Texas financier who had invested heavily in Antigua and was presented as a major benefactor. In 2012, Stanford was convicted of running a USD 7 billion Ponzi scheme and sentenced to 110 years in prison. Antigua quietly revoked the knighthood.

The lesson is not that Antigua is corrupt — it is that due diligence processes, even well-intentioned ones, can be gamed by sophisticated fraudsters. The same applies in reverse: if you are a legitimate applicant with a complex business history, the due diligence process will scrutinise your source of funds carefully. Prepare documentation thoroughly.

## CRS — the mandatory disclosure

Antigua participates in the Common Reporting Standard (CRS). A second passport does not change your tax residency. If you remain a Nigerian tax resident, FIRS will receive information about your Antiguan financial accounts.

To change your tax residency, you must actually relocate and establish tax residency in another country. Holding an Antigua passport while living in Nigeria does not make you an Antigua tax resident.

If you are considering Antigua CBI for tax planning purposes, consult a qualified tax adviser who understands both Antiguan and Nigerian tax law. This post does not constitute tax advice.

## Processing timeline

| Stage | Duration |
|---|---|
| Application preparation | 1-2 months |
| Due diligence processing | 3-6 months |
| Approval and oath of allegiance | 1 month |
| Passport issuance | 1-2 months |
| **Total** | **6-12 months** |

## How to apply

Applications must be submitted through an authorised agent — you cannot apply directly to the CIP Unit. The list of authorised agents is published at `cip.gov.ag`.

When selecting an agent:
- Verify they are on the authorised agent list at `cip.gov.ag`
- Ask for references from previous clients
- Get a full fee breakdown in writing before signing any agreement
- Do not pay any fees before receiving a written engagement letter
