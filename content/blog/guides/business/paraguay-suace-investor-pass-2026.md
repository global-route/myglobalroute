Paraguay has attracted significant attention from African digital nomads, remote workers and investors. The territorial tax system is real. The SUACE company formation system is real. The fast-track residency routes are widely discussed. Here is what is confirmed from primary sources - and what is not.

## What is confirmed: the territorial tax system

Paraguay taxes only income sourced within Paraguay. This is confirmed by the Subsecretaria de Estado de Tributacion (SET) at `https://www.set.gov.py/`.

**Personal income tax (IRP):** 10% flat rate on Paraguay-sourced income above the threshold. Foreign-sourced income: 0%.

**Corporate tax (IRACIS):** 10% on Paraguay-sourced profits.

**VAT:** 10% standard rate.

**What this means for remote workers:** If you are tax resident in Paraguay and your income comes entirely from foreign clients, you pay 0% Paraguayan income tax on that income. You pay 10% only on any income sourced within Paraguay.

**Confidence: HIGH** - confirmed from SET primary source.

## What is confirmed: the SUACE system

SUACE (Sistema Unificado de Apertura y Cierre de Empresas) is Paraguay's fast-track company registration system. The portal at `http://formalizacion.suace.gov.py/formalizacion_suace/` is live and operated by the Ministry of Industry and Commerce (MIC).

**What SUACE does:** Allows company formation (Sociedad Anonima or SRL) in 24-72 hours through an online portal.

**What a SUACE company requires:**
- Registered address in Paraguay
- At least one local legal representative (representante legal)
- Annual tax filings with SET

**Confidence: HIGH** - SUACE portal confirmed live, operated by MIC.

## What is confirmed: the standard residency route

Paraguay's standard residency structure is confirmed by multiple sources:

1. Apply for Temporary Residency (Residencia Temporaria) at the Direccion General de Migraciones
2. Hold temporary residency for 2 years
3. Apply to upgrade to Permanent Residency (Residencia Permanente)
4. After 3 years of permanent residency, eligible for citizenship

**Total timeline to citizenship:** Approximately 5 years.

**Confidence: HIGH** - structure confirmed from multiple practitioner sources and consistent with Paraguayan immigration law.

## The SUACE fast-track route - awaiting verification

The following describes the SUACE fast-track route as widely reported by Paraguay immigration practitioners. The specific USD 70,000 capital threshold and the nominal vs deposited capital distinction have NOT been independently verified from the MIC or the Direccion General de Migraciones. Do not act on these figures without confirming them with a qualified Paraguay immigration lawyer.

**What practitioners report:** Registering a SUACE company with a declared nominal capital above a threshold qualifies the foreign investor for direct permanent residency - bypassing the 2-year temporary stage.

**Widely cited threshold:** USD 70,000 nominal capital declared in the company's articles of incorporation.

**The critical distinction practitioners cite:** This is reported as nominal capital - the stated capital of the business - not a bank deposit. It does not need to be deposited in a Paraguayan bank account upfront. However, this distinction is the most important claim to verify - if wrong, it materially changes the accessibility of this route.

**Total timeline to citizenship if confirmed:** Approximately 3 years (direct permanent residency + 3-year clock).

**To verify:** Contact the MIC at `https://www.mic.gov.py/` or engage a Paraguay immigration lawyer to confirm the current decree or resolution establishing this threshold.

## Paraguay passport - confirmed

The Paraguayan passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to approximately 145 countries including the EU Schengen area, UK and most of Latin America.

**Confidence: HIGH** - passport strength confirmed from Henley Passport Index and official sources.
