Eight countries now have formal remote work visa routes that African digital nomads can access. This guide consolidates all of them into one honest comparison - income thresholds, cost of living, internet quality, time zones, and what happens after the visa expires.

## The eight routes compared

| Country | Visa type | Income threshold | Duration | Path to PR | Time zone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Portugal | D8 Digital Nomad | EUR 3,480/month | 1 year renewable | Yes (5 years) | UTC+0/+1 |
| Spain | International Teleworker | EUR 2,646/month | 1 year renewable | Conditional (5 years) | UTC+1/+2 |
| Germany | Opportunity Card | EUR 1,091/month funds | 1 year | Yes (5 years via work) | UTC+1/+2 |
| Mauritius | Premium Visa | Not published | 1 year renewable | No direct path | UTC+4 |
| Cape Verde | Remote Work Visa | ~EUR 1,500/month | 6 months (up to 2 years) | Indirect | UTC-1 |
| Namibia | Digital Nomad Visa | ~USD 2,000/month | 6 months extendable | Indirect | UTC+2 |
| Georgia | Remotely from Georgia | USD 2,000/month | 1 year | No | UTC+4 |
| Costa Rica | Digital Nomad Visa | USD 3,000/month | 1 year renewable | No | UTC-6 |

*Georgia and Costa Rica included for comparison - not African destinations but popular with African nomads*

## Europe options - higher threshold, stronger outcome

### Portugal D8 - the strongest long-term option

Portugal's D8 Digital Nomad visa is the best long-term option for African remote workers who want a path to EU citizenship.

**Income threshold:** EUR 3,480/month (3x the Portuguese minimum wage of EUR 1,020/month in 2026). This is higher than Spain but the outcome - Portuguese citizenship after 5 years - is stronger.

**Cost of living:** Porto and smaller cities are significantly cheaper than Lisbon. A comfortable professional lifestyle costs EUR 1,500-2,500/month outside Lisbon.

**English:** Widely spoken in cities and tourist areas. Portuguese language skills improve your daily life but are not required for the visa.

**Path to citizenship:** After 5 years of legal residence, you can apply for Portuguese citizenship - an EU passport with freedom of movement across all 27 EU member states.

**Primary source:** `https://aima.gov.pt/pt`

### Spain International Teleworker - Beckham Law tax benefit

Spain's International Teleworker visa offers a significant tax benefit: the Beckham Law allows qualifying new residents to pay a flat 24% income tax rate on Spanish-sourced income for 6 years.

**Income threshold:** EUR 2,646/month (200% of Spain's minimum interprofessional wage).

**The Beckham Law:** Applies for the year of arrival and the following 5 years. You must apply within 6 months of registering as a resident. Not all income qualifies - consult a Spanish tax adviser.

**Primary source:** `https://www.inclusion.gob.es/`

### Germany Opportunity Card - no job offer, job search visa

Germany's Chancenkarte is not a digital nomad visa - it is a job search visa. But it functions similarly for remote workers who want to base themselves in Germany while looking for local employment.

**Funds required:** EUR 1,091/month via blocked account or declaration of commitment.

**Work rights:** Up to 20 hours/week in any job while searching for a qualifying skilled position.

**Primary source:** `https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card`

## Africa options - lower cost, simpler process

### Mauritius Premium Visa - best African infrastructure

Mauritius has the best infrastructure of the three African digital nomad destinations. Reliable island-wide connectivity, a well-developed financial sector, and English/French as official languages.

**Income threshold:** Not published. You must demonstrate sufficient financial means.

**Cost of living:** Moderate to high by African standards. A comfortable professional lifestyle costs USD 1,500-2,500/month.

**Time zone:** UTC+4 - good overlap with European morning hours and East African business hours.

**Primary source:** `https://www.edbmauritius.org/`

### Cape Verde - lowest cost, Atlantic time zone

Cape Verde is the most affordable of the three African options. The Euro-pegged currency provides stability. Good connectivity on the main islands.

**Income threshold:** Approximately EUR 1,500/month (not a fixed published figure).

**Cost of living:** Low by international standards. A comfortable lifestyle costs EUR 800-1,200/month.

**Time zone:** UTC-1 - good overlap with West African and European business hours.

**Primary source:** `https://www.sefcv.gov.cv/`

### Namibia - Southern Africa access, English-speaking

Namibia is the best option for remote workers with South African business interests or who want access to the Southern African Development Community (SADC) region.

**Income threshold:** Approximately USD 2,000/month (not a fixed published figure).

**Cost of living:** Moderate. A comfortable lifestyle in Windhoek costs USD 1,200-1,800/month.

**Time zone:** UTC+2 - good overlap with European and East African business hours.

**Primary source:** `https://mhaiss.gov.na/`

## How to choose

**Choose Portugal if:** You want the strongest long-term outcome (EU citizenship), you earn EUR 3,500+/month, and you want to be in Europe.

**Choose Spain if:** You earn EUR 2,700+/month, you want the Beckham Law tax benefit, and you prefer a warmer European climate.

**Choose Mauritius if:** You want to stay in Africa, you prioritise infrastructure and connectivity, and you want a stable financial base.

**Choose Cape Verde if:** You want the lowest cost of living, you earn EUR 1,500+/month, and you prefer a relaxed island environment.

**Choose Namibia if:** You have South African business interests, you want an English-speaking African base, and you earn USD 2,000+/month.

## The tax question - what no one tells you

Moving to a new country as a remote worker does not automatically resolve your tax situation. You may have tax obligations in:

1. **Nigeria** - if you remain tax resident in Nigeria (typically if you spend more than 183 days/year there)
2. **Your destination country** - if you become tax resident there
3. **Both** - if you split your time

> Before relocating, consult a tax adviser in both Nigeria and your destination country. The territorial tax systems of Mauritius, Cape Verde and Namibia are generally favourable for remote workers, but the specifics depend on your income sources and residency status.
