## What to check directly

Australia's subclass 462 Work and Holiday visa is a real programme. Whether Nigerian nationals are eligible depends on the official participating-country list maintained by the Department of Home Affairs.

**Official source to verify:** [homeaffairs.gov.au — Work and Holiday visa (subclass 462)](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-462)

On that page, look for the list of eligible countries. If Nigeria is listed, the programme details below apply. If Nigeria is not listed, this route is not currently available to Nigerian passport holders.

## Programme details (pending eligibility confirmation)

The following information describes the Australia subclass 462 programme. It applies only to passport holders of eligible countries.

**Age limit:** 18–35 (varies by nationality)
**Duration:** 12 months, extendable to 24 or 36 months with regional work
**Work:** Any job, any employer — no more than 6 months with the same employer without permission
**Study:** Up to 4 months
**Application fee:** AUD 635 (approximately USD 415)
**Minimum funds:** AUD 5,000
**Government support letter:** Required from home country Ministry of Foreign Affairs for subclass 462

**Extending to 24 or 36 months:** Complete 3 months (88 days) of specified regional work during your first visa for a second visa; 6 months for a third.
