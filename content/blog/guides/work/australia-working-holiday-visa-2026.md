Australia's Working Holiday Visa is one of the most accessible work visas in the world. No job offer. No employer sponsorship. No points test. You apply, get approved, and go. Here is what Nigerian applicants need to know.

## Subclass 417 vs Subclass 462 - which one applies to Nigerians

Australia has two Working Holiday Visa types:

| Feature | Subclass 417 | Subclass 462 |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible nationalities | UK, Ireland, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, South Korea, and others | USA, Argentina, Chile, China, India, Nigeria, Thailand, Vietnam, and others |
| Age limit | 18-30 (35 for some) | 18-35 |
| Government support letter | Not required | Required from home country government |
| Application fee | AUD 635 | AUD 635 |

**Nigerian nationals apply for subclass 462.** You need a letter of support from the Nigerian Ministry of Foreign Affairs as part of your application.

## Requirements for Nigerian applicants (subclass 462)

**Eligibility:**
- Age 18-35 at time of application
- Nigerian passport
- No dependent children accompanying you
- Meet health and character requirements
- Have sufficient funds for your initial stay (approximately AUD 5,000)

**Documents required:**
- Valid Nigerian passport
- Letter of support from Nigerian Ministry of Foreign Affairs
- Proof of sufficient funds (bank statement)
- Health insurance (recommended)
- Police clearance certificate

**Application fee:** AUD 635 (approximately USD 415 or NGN 622,000 at current rates)

**Primary source:** `https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/work-holiday-462`

## What you can do on the visa

**Work:** Any job, any employer, any industry. The only restriction is no more than 6 months with the same employer without permission.

**Study:** Up to 4 months of study.

**Travel:** Travel freely in and out of Australia during the 12-month validity.

**Earn:** Australia's minimum wage is AUD 23.23/hour (2026). Working full-time at minimum wage earns approximately AUD 3,700/month before tax.

## Extending to 24 or 36 months

**Second visa (24 months total):** Complete 3 months (88 days) of specified work in a regional area during your first visa. Specified work includes: plant and animal cultivation, fishing and pearling, tree farming and felling, mining, construction in regional areas.

**Third visa (36 months total):** Complete 6 months of specified work in a regional area during your second visa.

| Item | Approximate cost |

| Visa application fee | AUD 635 (NGN 622,000) |
| Ministry of Foreign Affairs letter | NGN 10,000 - 30,000 |
| Return flight Lagos-Sydney | NGN 800,000 - 1,500,000 |
| Initial funds (required) | AUD 5,000 (NGN 4,900,000) |

| **Total initial outlay** | **Approximately NGN 6.8M - 8.5M** |

Note: The AUD 5,000 initial funds are not a fee - they are your living costs. You earn them back quickly once working.
