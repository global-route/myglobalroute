Zero deposit appears on visa and work placement offers across Nigerian agent Telegram groups and WhatsApp channels. Canada work permits, Schengen tourist visas, Gulf employment packages, Caribbean visa offers — all with "zero deposit."

Here is what it actually means.

## What zero deposit means in practice

Zero deposit means you do not pay a deposit at the start of the process. It does not mean:

- The service is free
- The total cost is lower
- You have any protection if the service is not delivered

The full fee is still charged — either in instalments during the process, after the visa is approved, or as a lump sum at a later stage. In many cases, the total cost of a zero deposit offer is higher than a straightforward upfront fee because the agent builds in a premium for the deferred payment structure.

## Why agents use it

Zero deposit lowers the barrier to starting. Once you have:

- Submitted your documents
- Had consultations with the agent
- Invested time and emotional energy in the process
- Built a relationship with the agent

...you are significantly more likely to pay the full fee when it is requested, even if the amount is higher than you expected or the service has not delivered what was promised.

This is a standard sales technique. It is not a financial benefit to you.

## The payment structures to watch

**"Zero deposit, pay after visa approval"** — legitimate if the agent has a clear written agreement specifying exactly what they will deliver, by when, and what happens if the visa is refused. Not legitimate if the agreement is verbal or vague.

**"Zero deposit, bank draft"** — bank draft means you will pay via a bank instrument at a later stage. The zero deposit is the hook; the bank draft is the payment mechanism. The total cost is the same.

**"Zero deposit, pay in instalments"** — you pay the full fee in stages. The total is the same or higher. The zero deposit framing makes the initial commitment feel smaller.

**"Zero deposit, limited slots"** — combining zero deposit with artificial scarcity. The zero deposit lowers resistance; the limited slots creates urgency. Both are sales tactics.

## What legitimate payment looks like

For visa application services:
- Clear written agreement specifying the service, the fee, and the deliverable
- Fee paid to a registered business with a receipt
- Refund policy stated in writing for cases where the visa is refused

For work placement services:
- No fee charged to the worker — legitimate employers pay recruitment costs
- If a service fee is charged, it is for a specific, defined service (CV preparation, application support) not for "getting you a job"
- Payment only after a signed employment contract is in hand

## The honest summary

Zero deposit is a marketing phrase. It tells you nothing about the legitimacy of the offer, the quality of the service, or the total cost. Evaluate every offer on its actual terms — what is being delivered, by when, for how much, and what recourse you have if it is not delivered.

An offer with a deposit and a clear written agreement is safer than a zero deposit offer with no written terms.
