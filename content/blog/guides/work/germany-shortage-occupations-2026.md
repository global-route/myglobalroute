# Germany Shortage Occupation List 2026

## What the Engpassberufe is

Germany's Federal Employment Agency (Bundesagentur fur Arbeit) publishes a quarterly report identifying occupations where there are significantly more vacancies than available workers. These are called Engpassberufe (bottleneck occupations or shortage occupations).

Being in a shortage occupation has two practical effects:
1. Your employer may be exempt from the labour market test (Vorrangprufung) — the check that no German or EU worker is available for the role
2. Some visa processing may be faster

The list is updated quarterly. Check the current version at arbeitsagentur.de before applying.

**Primary source:** arbeitsagentur.de/en/shortage-occupations

---

## Key shortage categories (2026)

The following categories consistently appear on the German shortage list. This is not exhaustive — check the official list for your specific occupation code (KldB classification).

### Healthcare and care

- Nurses (Gesundheits- und Krankenpfleger) — consistently the largest shortage
- Elderly care workers (Altenpfleger)
- Physiotherapists
- Medical assistants
- Paramedics

**Note for nurses:** Professional recognition from the relevant German state authority (Landesamt) is required before you can work as a nurse in Germany. This process takes 3-12 months and requires your qualifications to be assessed. Language requirement is typically B2 German for clinical roles.

### IT and software

- Software developers and programmers
- IT systems administrators
- Data analysts
- Cybersecurity specialists
- IT project managers

**Note for IT:** The Chancenkarte points route is particularly well-suited for IT professionals. No job offer required — you come to Germany and search for work.

### Engineering

- Mechanical engineers
- Electrical engineers
- Civil engineers
- Process engineers
- Automotive engineers

**Note for engineers:** Degree recognition via the ANABIN database is required. Some engineering professions are regulated (e.g. civil engineering in some states) and require additional registration.

### Trades

- Electricians (Elektroniker)
- Plumbers and heating engineers (Anlagenmechaniker SHK)
- Carpenters (Tischler/Schreiner)
- Mechatronics engineers (Mechatroniker)
- Welders (Schweisser) — in some regions

**Note for trades:** German vocational qualifications (Ausbildung) are highly structured. Foreign trade qualifications need to be assessed for equivalence. Trades Recognition is handled by the relevant chamber (Handwerkskammer).

### Teaching

- Primary and secondary school teachers (in some states)
- Vocational training instructors
- Special needs educators

## What shortage status means for your application

### Labour market test exemption

For the standard Skilled Worker visa, your employer normally has to prove no German or EU worker was available for the role (Vorrangprufung). For shortage occupations, this test is waived — your employer does not need to advertise the role first.

### Processing time

Shortage occupation applications are typically processed faster by the German embassy. Standard processing is 4-8 weeks. Shortage occupation applications may be processed in 2-4 weeks.

### Chancenkarte points

Being in a shortage occupation does not directly add points to your Chancenkarte score. However, it makes it significantly easier to find a job once you arrive in Germany on the Chancenkarte, which is the main purpose of the card.

## How to check your specific occupation

The Engpassberufe list uses the KldB (Klassifikation der Berufe) occupation classification system. To check your specific occupation:

1. Go to arbeitsagentur.de/en/shortage-occupations
2. Search for your occupation by name or browse by category
3. Check whether your specific KldB code is listed as a shortage occupation
4. Note whether the shortage is national or regional (some shortages are only in certain states)

---

## Qualification recognition

Being in a shortage occupation does not bypass the qualification recognition requirement. You still need your foreign qualification assessed as equivalent to a German qualification.

- University degrees: Check ANABIN database at anabin.kmk.org
- Vocational qualifications: Apply to the relevant chamber (IHK for commercial, HWK for trades)
- Healthcare qualifications: Apply to the relevant state authority (Landesamt)

Recognition can take 3-12 months. Start this process before applying for a visa.
