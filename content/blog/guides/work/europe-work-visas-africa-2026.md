Europe offers the most structured and verifiable work visa routes for African professionals. Unlike the Gulf, where employer sponsorship is the only path, several European countries have introduced points-based or skills-based routes that do not require a prior job offer.

This post covers the five most relevant European work visa routes for African applicants in 2026 - with verified figures from official sources.

## Germany — Two routes, two different profiles

### Route 1: Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte) — no job offer required

The Chancenkarte is Germany's job search visa. You come to Germany for up to 1 year to find work, without needing a job offer first.

**Requirements:**
- Either: full recognition of a foreign qualification in Germany (direct access, no points needed)
- Or: 6 points on the points system. Points are awarded for: qualification (up to 4 points), work experience (up to 2 points), language skills (up to 3 points), age (up to 1 point), previous Germany connection (1 point)
- Minimum funds: EUR 1,091/month via blocked account or declaration of commitment
- Valid passport

**Work rights while searching:** Up to 20 hours per week in any job while searching for a qualifying skilled position.

**Once you find a job:** You can switch to a work visa without leaving Germany.

**Primary source:** `https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-residence/opportunity-card`

### Route 2: Skilled Worker Visa — job offer required

If you already have a job offer from a German employer, you apply for the Skilled Worker visa directly.

**Requirements:**
- Confirmed job offer from a German employer
- Recognised qualification (degree or vocational training)
- Minimum funds: EUR 1,091/month (same as Chancenkarte, for the initial period)

**Primary source:** `https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/en/visa-service`

## UK — Skilled Worker Visa

The UK Skilled Worker visa is the primary route for non-EU professionals. It requires a job offer from a UK employer with a valid sponsor licence.

**Requirements:**
- Confirmed job offer from a UK employer with a sponsor licence
- Minimum salary: GBP 41,700/year (baseline from April 2024) - the applicable occupation going rate may be higher
- Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS) from your employer
- English language requirement (IELTS 4.0 or equivalent, or degree taught in English)

**Maintenance funds:** GBP 1,270 held for 28 consecutive days before applying (unless your employer certifies maintenance).

**Path to settlement:** After 5 years on a Skilled Worker visa, you can apply for Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR).

**Primary source:** `https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa`

## Portugal — D3 Highly Qualified Activity Visa

Portugal's D3 visa is for highly qualified professionals with a job offer from a Portuguese employer. It leads to permanent residence in 5 years and citizenship in 5 years.

**Requirements:**
- Qualifying employment contract with a Portuguese entity
- Higher education degree or equivalent professional qualification (or 3+ years relevant experience for non-degree holders)
- Income above the Portuguese minimum wage (EUR 1,020/month in 2026)

**Path to citizenship:** After 5 years of legal residence, you can apply for Portuguese citizenship - an EU passport with freedom of movement across all 27 EU member states.

**Primary source:** `https://aima.gov.pt/pt`

## Spain — International Teleworker Visa (Digital Nomad)

Spain's International Teleworker visa is for remote workers whose income comes from outside Spain.

**Requirements:**
- Proof of remote employment or professional services relationship with a company or clients outside Spain
- Minimum monthly income: EUR 2,646/month (200% of Spain's minimum interprofessional wage in 2026)
- Spanish business activities capped at 20% of total income

**Tax benefit:** The Beckham Law allows qualifying new residents to pay a flat 24% income tax rate on Spanish-sourced income for 6 years.

**Primary source:** `https://www.inclusion.gob.es/`

## Ireland — Critical Skills Employment Permit

Ireland's Critical Skills Employment Permit is one of the most accessible European work permits for skilled professionals. There is no quota and it leads directly to permanent residence.

**Requirements:**
- Job offer from an Irish employer
- Minimum annual salary: EUR 38,000 for most occupations (EUR 32,000 for occupations on the Critical Skills Occupations List)
- Relevant qualification or experience

**Path to permanent residence:** After 2 years on a Critical Skills permit, you can apply for Stamp 4 permission - which allows you to work without a permit and is a step toward permanent residence.

**Primary source:** `https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/`

| Country | Job offer required | Minimum salary | Path to PR | Timeline to PR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Germany (Chancenkarte) | No | EUR 1,091/month funds | Yes (via skilled work) | 5 years |
| Germany (Skilled Worker) | Yes | Varies by occupation | Yes | 5 years |
| UK (Skilled Worker) | Yes | GBP 41,700/year | Yes (ILR) | 5 years |
