Ireland's Critical Skills Employment Permit is one of the most direct routes from Africa to EU permanent residence for software engineers and IT professionals. No points system, no labour market test, and a clear two-year path to unrestricted work rights.

The EUR 38,000 salary threshold is the number most people focus on. Here is what it actually means in practice — and what the process looks like from Nigeria.

Primary source: [enterprise.gov.ie — Critical Skills Employment Permit](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/permit-types/critical-skills-employment-permit/)

## What the permit is

The Critical Skills Employment Permit is an Irish work permit for occupations on the government's Critical Skills Occupations List. Software engineers, data scientists, cybersecurity professionals, cloud architects, and most IT roles are on this list.

Key features:
- No Labour Market Needs Test — the employer does not need to advertise the role to EU workers first
- Employer applies on behalf of the employee
- Permit is issued for 2 years initially
- After 2 years: apply for Stamp 4 (unrestricted work rights)
- After 5 years legal residence: apply for long-term residence or citizenship

## Salary thresholds

| Category | Minimum salary |
|---|---|
| Most Critical Skills occupations (incl. software engineers) | EUR 38,000/year |
| Certain high-demand roles | EUR 64,000/year |

The salary must be the contracted base salary. Bonuses, allowances, and benefits do not count toward the threshold.

Verify your specific role at: `enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/employment-permit-eligibility/highly-remunerated-occupations/`

## Eligible occupations (selected)

Software engineers, software developers, data scientists, data engineers, machine learning engineers, cybersecurity analysts, cloud architects, DevOps engineers, IT project managers, UX/UI designers (verify current list at enterprise.gov.ie — the list is updated periodically).

## The process

**Step 1 — Secure a job offer from an Irish employer.** The employer must be registered in Ireland and tax-compliant. The job offer must be for a role on the Critical Skills list at the qualifying salary.

**Step 2 — Employer applies for the permit.** The employer submits the application through the Employment Permits Online System (EPOS) at `epos.enterprise.gov.ie`. Processing time is typically 4-6 weeks for a standard application.

**Step 3 — Apply for an Irish employment visa.** Once the permit is approved, you apply for a D visa (long-stay employment visa) at the Irish embassy or consulate in Nigeria. Required documents: permit approval, job offer letter, passport, passport photos, bank statements, accommodation evidence.

**Step 4 — Register with GNIB/IRP on arrival.** Within 90 days of arriving in Ireland, register with the Garda National Immigration Bureau (GNIB) or Immigration Registration Office to get your Irish Residence Permit (IRP) card.

**Step 5 — Apply for Stamp 4 after 2 years.** After 2 years of continuous employment on the Critical Skills permit, apply for Stamp 4 at the Immigration Service Delivery (ISD).

## Finding Irish tech employers

Ireland hosts the European headquarters of most major US tech companies — Google, Meta, Apple, Microsoft, LinkedIn, Salesforce, Stripe, HubSpot, and many others are based in Dublin. These companies regularly hire internationally.

Where to look:
- `jobs.ie` and `irishjobs.ie` — Irish job boards
- LinkedIn — filter by Ireland location, tech roles
- Company career pages directly — most large tech companies in Ireland have dedicated careers portals
- `enterprise-ireland.com` — Irish government agency that supports Irish tech companies, many of which hire internationally

## What EUR 38,000 means in Dublin

EUR 38,000 gross per year is approximately EUR 2,800-3,000 net per month after Irish income tax (USC and PRSI). Dublin is an expensive city — average rent for a one-bedroom apartment is EUR 2,000-2,500/month. At EUR 38,000, you are at the minimum threshold and Dublin living costs will be tight.

Most software engineering roles in Ireland pay significantly above EUR 38,000. Mid-level engineers typically earn EUR 55,000-80,000. The EUR 38,000 threshold is the floor, not the typical offer.

## Family

Your spouse or civil partner can apply for a Stamp 1G, which allows them to work for any employer in Ireland without a separate work permit. Dependent children can attend Irish schools. This makes Ireland one of the more family-friendly EU work permit routes.

## Path to permanent residence

| Timeline | Status |
|---|---|
| Year 0 | Arrive on Critical Skills permit |
| Year 2 | Apply for Stamp 4 — unrestricted work rights |
| Year 5 | Apply for long-term residence (EU Long-Term Resident status) |
| Year 5+ | Apply for Irish citizenship (naturalisation) — requires 5 years reckonable residence |
