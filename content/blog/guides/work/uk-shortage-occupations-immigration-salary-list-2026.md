# UK Shortage Occupation List 2026: What Changed

## The old system vs. the new system

Until April 2024, the UK had a Shortage Occupation List (SOL). Occupations on the SOL benefited from:
- A 20% discount below the occupation going rate
- Reduced minimum salary threshold

In April 2024, the SOL was abolished and replaced by the Immigration Salary List (ISL). The ISL works differently:
- No salary discount
- Only identifies occupations where the MAC-set going rate is below the general GBP 38,700 threshold
- For ISL occupations, the going rate (which may be below GBP 38,700) applies instead of the general threshold

**The practical effect:** The ISL is a narrower concession than the old SOL. Most workers who previously benefited from the SOL discount no longer benefit.

---

## How the Immigration Salary List works

For most Skilled Worker visa applicants, the salary requirement is:

**GBP 38,700 per year** (general threshold from April 2024) OR the occupation going rate, whichever is higher.

For occupations on the Immigration Salary List, the salary requirement is:

**The occupation going rate** (which may be below GBP 38,700) OR GBP 38,700, whichever is lower.

In practice, ISL occupations are those where the MAC has determined the market rate is below GBP 38,700 — typically some healthcare, education and social care roles.

**Primary source:** gov.uk/government/publications/immigration-salary-list

---

## Occupations commonly on the Immigration Salary List

The ISL is updated periodically. Check the current version at gov.uk before applying. Occupations that have appeared on the ISL include:

**Healthcare:**
- Nurses (various grades)
- Midwives
- Paramedics
- Radiographers
- Occupational therapists
- Speech and language therapists
- Social workers

**Education:**
- Secondary school teachers (some subjects)
- Special educational needs teachers

**Technical:**
- Some engineering roles
- Some IT roles at entry level

## What this means for African applicants

### Healthcare workers

NHS nursing and allied health roles are the most common route for African professionals to the UK. These roles are typically on the ISL, meaning the NHS pay scale going rate applies rather than the GBP 38,700 general threshold.

For nurses, the going rate is set by NHS Agenda for Change pay bands. Band 5 (newly qualified nurse) starts at approximately GBP 28,000-30,000 — below the general threshold but above the ISL going rate, so the ISL concession applies.

**NMC registration is required** for nurses before you can work in the UK. The NMC (Nursing and Midwifery Council) assesses overseas qualifications. This process takes 3-12 months.

### IT and engineering

Most IT and engineering roles are not on the ISL — they pay above GBP 38,700 in the UK market. The general threshold applies. If your job offer is below GBP 38,700, check whether your specific occupation code is on the ISL.

### Trades

Most trade occupations are not on the ISL. The general threshold of GBP 38,700 applies. Trades are not a primary route to the UK under the Skilled Worker visa — the UK does not have a DAMA-equivalent for trades.

## Checking your specific occupation

The Skilled Worker visa uses Standard Occupational Classification (SOC) codes. To check your occupation:

1. Find your SOC code at ons.gov.uk/methodology/classificationsandstandards/standardoccupationalclassificationsoc
2. Check the going rate for your SOC code at gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/your-job
3. Check whether your SOC code is on the current Immigration Salary List at gov.uk/government/publications/immigration-salary-list

---

## New entrant rate

If you are under 26 or a recent graduate (within 2 years of completing a UK degree), the new entrant rate applies: GBP 30,960 per year (80% of the general threshold). This applies for a maximum of 4 years.
