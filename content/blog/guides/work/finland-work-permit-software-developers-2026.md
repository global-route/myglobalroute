Finland is one of the less-discussed tech work destinations in Europe — but it is a real option for African software developers, cloud engineers, and AI specialists. No points system, no language requirement for the permit itself, and a clear path to permanent residence.

The salary threshold is EUR 3,000/month. Most software engineering roles in Finland pay significantly above this.

Primary source: [migri.fi — Residence permit for an employed person](https://migri.fi/en/residence-permit-for-an-employed-person)

## The permit

Finland's residence permit for an employed person (työntekijän oleskelulupa) covers most employment situations. For tech workers, the relevant category is the specialist permit — for roles requiring specialist knowledge or skills.

Key features:
- Employer applies together with the employee
- No labour market test for specialist roles (employer does not need to advertise to Finnish workers first — verify current rules at migri.fi)
- Permit issued for the duration of the employment contract, up to 2 years initially
- Renewable
- After 4 years of continuous residence: apply for permanent residence

## Salary threshold

EUR 3,000/month gross is the general threshold for specialist roles. This is the floor — most Finnish tech employers pay EUR 3,500-6,000/month for software engineers depending on experience and location.

Helsinki has the highest salaries and the highest cost of living. Tampere, Turku, and Oulu have lower living costs and active tech sectors.

Verify the current threshold at `migri.fi` — thresholds are reviewed periodically.

## Eligible roles

Software developers, software engineers, full-stack developers, cloud architects, DevOps engineers, data engineers, data scientists, machine learning engineers, cybersecurity specialists, IT project managers.

Finland has a growing tech sector anchored by companies like Nokia, Rovio, Supercell, Wolt, and a large number of Nordic and international tech firms with Finnish offices.

## The process

**Step 1 — Secure a job offer from a Finnish employer.** The employer must be registered in Finland and have a Finnish Business ID (Y-tunnus).

**Step 2 — Joint application.** The employer submits the employer section of the application online at `enterfinland.fi`. You submit the employee section at the same portal. Both sections must be submitted — the application is not complete until both are done.

**Step 3 — Biometrics.** You may need to visit a Finnish embassy or VFS Global service point in Nigeria to provide biometrics (fingerprints and photo). Check current requirements at `migri.fi`.

**Step 4 — Processing.** Migri processes the application. Target time is 2 months. You will receive a decision by email.

**Step 5 — Collect residence permit card.** If approved, collect your residence permit card from the Finnish embassy or on arrival in Finland (depending on current procedures — verify at `migri.fi`).

**Step 6 — Register on arrival.** Register with the Digital and Population Data Services Agency (DVV) within a reasonable time of arrival.

## Cost of living

Helsinki is expensive by European standards but cheaper than London or Zurich. Approximate monthly costs for a single person in Helsinki:

- Rent (1-bedroom): EUR 1,200-1,800/month
- Food: EUR 300-400/month
- Transport: EUR 60-80/month (public transport pass)
- Total: approximately EUR 1,800-2,400/month

At EUR 3,500/month net (approximate take-home on EUR 4,500 gross), you have reasonable disposable income. At EUR 3,000/month gross (the minimum threshold), take-home is approximately EUR 2,400-2,600 — tight in Helsinki but manageable.

## Path to permanent residence

| Timeline | Status |
|---|---|
| Year 0 | Arrive on employed person residence permit |
| Year 2 | Renew permit |
| Year 4 | Apply for permanent residence (pysyvä oleskelulupa) |
| Year 5+ | Apply for Finnish citizenship (requires language test) |

Finnish citizenship requires passing a Finnish or Swedish language test. This is a longer-term consideration — the permanent residence at year 4 does not require language proficiency.
