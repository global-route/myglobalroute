# Australia DAMA Shortage Occupations 2026

## What is DAMA

A Designated Area Migration Agreement (DAMA) is a formal agreement between the Australian federal government and a regional authority (state, territory or local government). It allows employers in that region to sponsor overseas workers in occupations that are not on the standard skilled occupation lists, and with concessions on the usual requirements.

DAMAs exist because regional Australia has different labour needs from the major cities. A region with a shortage of electricians or welders can negotiate a DAMA to bring in workers even if those occupations are not on the national shortage list.

Each DAMA has its own occupation list and concession arrangements. The Northern Territory DAMA is the largest and most active.

## DAMA concessions vs. standard requirements

This is why DAMA is significant for African applicants. Standard skilled visas have requirements that many African applicants cannot meet. DAMA agreements can relax these:

| Requirement | Standard skilled visa | DAMA concession (varies by region) |
|---|---|---|
| Age limit | 45 years | Up to 55 years in some DAMAs |
| English language | IELTS 6.0+ | Lower floor in some DAMAs (IELTS 4.5-5.0) |
| Salary | TSMIT AUD 73,150/year | Salary concession of up to 10% below TSMIT in some DAMAs |
| Occupation list | Must be on skilled occupation list | DAMA-specific list, broader than national list |

These concessions are not universal — they depend on the specific DAMA and the specific occupation. Verify with the regional authority before applying.

## How to get a DAMA job offer

You cannot apply for a DAMA visa independently. The process is:

1. Find an employer in a DAMA region who is an approved DAMA sponsor
2. The employer nominates you for a specific occupation under their DAMA agreement
3. You apply for the TSS visa (subclass 482) or the Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) under the DAMA stream
4. If approved, you work in the designated regional area for the duration of your visa

**Finding DAMA employers:**
- Contact the regional authority directly (e.g. NT Government's migration team)
- Use Australian job boards (Seek.com.au, Indeed.com.au) filtering for regional locations
- Contact industry associations in your trade (Master Electricians Australia, Master Plumbers, etc.)

---

## Skills assessment

For most trade occupations, you will need a skills assessment from the relevant Australian assessing authority before your employer can nominate you:

- Trades Recognition Australia (TRA): for most trade occupations
- VETASSESS: for some technical and trade occupations
- Engineers Australia: for engineering occupations

A skills assessment confirms your overseas qualifications and experience are equivalent to Australian standards. It is separate from the visa application and typically takes 3-6 months.

**Primary source:** immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/working-in-australia/skills-assessment

---

## Path to permanent residence

The DAMA route can lead to permanent residence:

- TSS visa (subclass 482) under DAMA: temporary, 2-4 years
- After 3 years on TSS in a regional area: eligible to apply for Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) permanent residence
- After 3 years on TSS in a regional area: eligible for Regional Sponsored Migration Scheme (subclass 187)

You must remain in the designated regional area for the duration of your temporary visa. Moving to a major city before applying for permanent residence may affect your eligibility.
