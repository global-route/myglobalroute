Ireland has a documented and ongoing shortage of nurses. The Health Service Executive (HSE) and private hospitals actively recruit internationally. Nigerian nurses are eligible and the route is well-established.

The process has two stages: NMBI registration, then employment permit. Here is the exact sequence.

## Stage 1 — NMBI registration

The Nursing and Midwifery Board of Ireland (NMBI) is the regulatory body for nurses and midwives in Ireland. You cannot work as a nurse in Ireland without NMBI registration.

Primary source: NMBI, nmbi.ie/Registration/Overseas-Applicants

**Step 1 — Apply for assessment of your qualification**

Submit your application to NMBI with:
- Completed application form
- Certified copy of your nursing qualification (degree or diploma)
- Official transcripts from your nursing school
- Verification of registration from the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria (NMCN) — NMBI contacts NMCN directly to verify your registration
- Proof of English language proficiency (IELTS 7.0 overall, no band below 6.5, or OET grade B)
- Application fee: EUR 330

**Step 2 — NMBI assessment**

NMBI assesses whether your Nigerian nursing qualification is equivalent to Irish nursing standards. For most Nigerian nurses with a degree-level qualification and current NMCN registration, the assessment is positive.

If NMBI determines there are gaps, they may require you to complete a period of supervised practice (adaptation) in Ireland before full registration.

**Step 3 — Receive decision**

NMBI issues one of three outcomes:
- Full registration — you can work as a registered nurse in Ireland
- Conditional registration — you must complete an adaptation period
- Refusal — your qualification does not meet Irish standards (rare for degree-level Nigerian nurses)

**Processing time:** 3-6 months from submission of complete application.

**Cost:** EUR 330 application fee plus document costs (certified copies, translations if required).

## Stage 2 — Critical Skills Employment Permit

Once you have NMBI registration (or conditional registration), you can apply for a Critical Skills Employment Permit.

Nursing is on the Critical Skills Eligible Occupations List. The permit requires:
- A job offer from an Irish employer (HSE hospital, private hospital, or nursing home)
- Salary of at least EUR 32,000 per year
- NMBI registration (or evidence that registration is in progress)

The employer applies for the permit on your behalf through the Department of Enterprise, Trade and Employment.

**Processing time:** 4-8 weeks for the permit.

**Salary:** Staff nurses in Ireland earn EUR 32,000-50,000 per year under HSE pay scales. The starting salary for a newly registered staff nurse is approximately EUR 32,000-35,000.

## Stage 3 — Irish visa

With the Critical Skills Employment Permit, you apply for an Irish employment visa (D visa) at the Irish embassy or consulate serving Nigeria.

**Processing time:** 4-8 weeks.

## Path to permanent residence

After 2 years on a Critical Skills Employment Permit, you can apply for Stamp 4 — unrestricted permission to work in Ireland without an employment permit. This is a significant benefit: Stamp 4 allows you to work for any employer in any role.

After 5 years of legal residence in Ireland, you can apply for long-term residence. After 5 years, you can also apply for Irish citizenship (naturalisation).

## Finding Irish nursing employers

- HSE jobs: hse.ie/eng/staff/jobs
- Nursing homes and private hospitals: search on irishjobs.ie and indeed.ie
- Recruitment agencies specialising in healthcare: Nurse On Call, Cpl Healthcare, Flexicare

## The honest picture

The Ireland nursing route is real, well-established, and leads to permanent residence. The NMBI registration process is the main time investment — 3-6 months — but it is a one-time process. Once registered, you are registered.

The salary is modest by Irish living cost standards but the path to Stamp 4 and eventual citizenship is clear and documented.

Nigerian nurses with degree-level qualifications and current NMCN registration are well-positioned for this route.
