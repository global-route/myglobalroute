Ireland's healthcare system has documented shortages across multiple allied health professions — not just nursing. Physiotherapists, radiographers, occupational therapists, medical scientists, and speech and language therapists are all on the Critical Skills Occupations List.

This means the same permit structure that applies to nurses and software engineers applies to allied health professionals: no labour market test, EUR 38,000 minimum salary, Stamp 4 after 2 years.

Primary source: [enterprise.gov.ie — Employment permits](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/what-we-do/workplace-and-skills/employment-permits/)

## Professions on the Critical Skills list (selected)

Verify the current full list at `enterprise.gov.ie` — the list is updated periodically.

- Physiotherapist
- Radiographer / Radiation Therapist
- Occupational Therapist
- Medical Scientist
- Speech and Language Therapist
- Dietitian
- Podiatrist / Chiropodist
- Orthoptist
- Social Worker (verify current status)

## The regulatory bodies

Each allied health profession in Ireland is regulated by a specific body. You must be registered (or have applied for registration) with the relevant body to work in the regulated role.

| Profession | Regulatory body | Website |
|---|---|---|
| Physiotherapy | CORU | `coru.ie` |
| Radiography | CORU | `coru.ie` |
| Occupational Therapy | CORU | `coru.ie` |
| Medical Science | CORU | `coru.ie` |
| Speech and Language Therapy | CORU | `coru.ie` |
| Social Work | CORU | `coru.ie` |
| Pharmacy | PSI (Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland) | `psi.ie` |
| Medicine | Medical Council | `medicalcouncil.ie` |

CORU covers 17 professions. If your profession is regulated by CORU, start the registration application early — it takes 3-6 months.

## CORU registration process for internationally qualified professionals

1. **Create a CORU account** at `coru.ie` and submit an application for registration.
2. **Credential assessment:** CORU assesses your qualification against Irish standards. You will need certified translations of your degree and transcripts if they are not in English.
3. **English proficiency:** IELTS Academic 7.0 overall (with no band below 6.5) or OET Grade B in all four components. Verify current requirements at `coru.ie`.
4. **Decision:** CORU may grant full registration, conditional registration (with an adaptation period), or refuse registration. Most internationally qualified professionals receive conditional registration initially.
5. **Adaptation period:** If required, you complete a supervised practice period in Ireland. This is typically done while working — your employer supervises and signs off on competencies.

## The permit process

The Critical Skills Employment Permit process for allied health professionals is the same as for other Critical Skills occupations:

1. Secure a job offer from an Irish employer (HSE, voluntary hospitals, private healthcare providers)
2. Employer applies for the permit through EPOS
3. Apply for a D visa at the Irish embassy in Nigeria
4. Register with GNIB/IRP on arrival
5. Complete CORU adaptation period if required
6. Apply for Stamp 4 after 2 years

## Finding Irish healthcare employers

**HSE (Health Service Executive)** — Ireland's public health system. Careers portal: `hse.ie/eng/staff/jobs/`

**Voluntary hospitals** — Beaumont Hospital, St Vincent's University Hospital, Mater Misericordiae University Hospital, and others hire internationally. Each has its own careers portal.

**Private healthcare** — Beacon Hospital, Mater Private, Blackrock Health, and others hire allied health professionals.

**LinkedIn** — filter by Ireland and your profession.

**CORU-registered employer lists** — CORU publishes lists of approved training sites which are also active employers.

## Salary context

EUR 38,000 is the minimum threshold. HSE salary scales for allied health professionals:

- Physiotherapist (basic grade): EUR 35,000-50,000 (scale — verify current HSE pay scales)
- Radiographer (basic grade): EUR 35,000-52,000
- Occupational Therapist (basic grade): EUR 35,000-50,000
- Medical Scientist (basic grade): EUR 35,000-52,000

Most roles start above EUR 38,000. Senior and specialist grades are significantly higher.
