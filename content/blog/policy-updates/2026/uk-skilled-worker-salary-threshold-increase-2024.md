# UK Skilled Worker Salary Threshold Increase 2024

## What Changed

On April 4, 2024, the UK Home Office raised the general salary threshold for the Skilled Worker visa from GBP 26,200 to GBP 38,700 per year gross.

This was the largest single increase to the Skilled Worker threshold since the route launched in 2021. It followed a recommendation from the Migration Advisory Committee (MAC) and was part of a broader package of immigration tightening measures.

---

## Before and After

| | Before April 4, 2024 | From April 4, 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| General threshold | GBP 26,200 | GBP 38,700 |
| New entrant rate | GBP 20,960 | GBP 30,960 |
| Shortage occupation discount | 20% below going rate | Removed |
| Shortage Occupation List | Active | Replaced by Immigration Salary List |

---

## Who Is Affected

**New applicants:** Anyone applying for a Skilled Worker visa from April 4, 2024 must meet the new threshold.

**Extension applicants:** Anyone extending their Skilled Worker visa after April 4, 2024 must meet the new threshold for their occupation.

**Settlement applicants:** Anyone applying for Indefinite Leave to Remain on the Skilled Worker route must meet the threshold at the time of their ILR application.

**Not affected:** Existing visa holders mid-permission who are not yet applying for an extension or settlement.

---

## The Going Rate Requirement

The GBP 38,700 is a floor, not a ceiling. Every occupation on the Skilled Worker list also has a going rate set by the MAC. Your salary must meet whichever is higher: the general threshold or the occupation going rate.

For many professional roles, the going rate exceeds GBP 38,700. Examples:
- Software developer: going rate typically GBP 40,000-50,000+
- Nurse: separate NHS pay scale applies
- Civil engineer: going rate typically GBP 38,000-45,000+

Check the current going rate for your specific occupation code (SOC code) at gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/your-job.

---

## New Entrant Rate

A reduced threshold applies to new entrants. You qualify as a new entrant if you are:
- Under 26 at the time of application
- A recent graduate (within 2 years of completing a UK degree)
- In a postdoctoral position

New entrant rate from April 2024: GBP 30,960 (80% of the general threshold).

The new entrant rate applies for a maximum of 4 years. After that, you must meet the full general threshold.

---

## The Immigration Salary List

The old Shortage Occupation List (SOL) was abolished in April 2024. It was replaced by the Immigration Salary List (ISL).

Key difference: The SOL gave a 20% discount below the going rate for shortage occupations. The ISL does not give a salary discount. It only identifies occupations where the going rate is set below the general threshold of GBP 38,700.

For occupations on the ISL, the going rate (which may be below GBP 38,700) applies instead of the general threshold. This is a narrower concession than the old SOL discount.

Check the current ISL at gov.uk/government/publications/immigration-salary-list.

---

## Health and Care Workers

Health and care workers have separate salary thresholds set by NHS pay scales and the relevant occupation going rates. The GBP 38,700 general threshold does not apply to most NHS roles.

However, the April 2024 changes also tightened the Health and Care visa by restricting which care sector employers can sponsor overseas workers. Only CQC-regulated employers can sponsor care workers from overseas.

---

## What This Means for African Applicants

The GBP 38,700 threshold is achievable in London and major UK cities for professional roles in tech, finance, engineering, healthcare and law. It is harder to reach in:
- Regional cities outside London
- Entry-level professional roles
- Hospitality, retail and service sectors (which are largely excluded from the Skilled Worker route anyway)

If your job offer is below GBP 38,700, check whether:
1. Your occupation has a going rate below the general threshold (ISL)
2. You qualify for the new entrant rate (GBP 30,960)
3. Your role is on the Skilled Worker eligible occupations list at all
