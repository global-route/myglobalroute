The Germany Chancenkarte is one of the most discussed routes to Germany for Nigerian professionals. This case study breaks down what it actually costs — from the first German lesson to the first month in Germany.

Exchange rate used: EUR 1 = NGN 1,600. Verify current rate before planning.

## The profile

Emeka, 31, civil engineer, Lagos. BSc Civil Engineering (UNILAG, 2016). 5 years work experience. German language: zero at start. Goal: Chancenkarte visa, job search in construction/engineering.

## Phase 1 — Language preparation (months 1-18)

| Cost item | NGN | EUR |
|---|---|---|
| Goethe-Institut A1 course (Lagos) | 120,000 | 75 |
| Goethe-Institut A2 course | 130,000 | 81 |
| Goethe-Institut B1 course | 150,000 | 94 |
| Goethe-Institut B2 course | 180,000 | 113 |
| B1 exam fee | 45,000 | 28 |
| B2 exam fee | 55,000 | 34 |
| Study materials | 30,000 | 19 |
| **Phase 1 total** | **710,000** | **444** |

## Phase 2 — Credential recognition (months 12-20)

| Cost item | NGN | EUR |
|---|---|---|
| Degree certificate authentication | 80,000 | 50 |
| Notarised translations (degree, transcripts) | 120,000 | 75 |
| Recognition application fee | 80,000 | 50 |
| WES or equivalent evaluation | 120,000 | 75 |
| **Phase 2 total** | **400,000** | **250** |

## Phase 3 — Visa application (months 18-22)

| Cost item | NGN | EUR |
|---|---|---|
| Chancenkarte visa fee | 115,200 | 72 |
| Blocked account setup (Fintiba) | 17,932,800 | 11,208 |
| Fintiba service fee | 89,600 | 56 |
| Health insurance (entry) | 80,000 | 50 |
| Biometric photos + courier | 20,000 | 12 |
| **Phase 3 total (excl. blocked account)** | **304,800** | **190** |
| **Phase 3 total (incl. blocked account)** | **18,237,600** | **11,398** |

The blocked account is your own money — released at ~EUR 934/month once in Germany.

## Phase 4 — Travel and first month

| Cost item | NGN | EUR |
|---|---|---|
| Lagos to Frankfurt flight | 800,000 | 500 |
| First night accommodation + transfer | 80,000 | 50 |
| First month rent (shared flat) | 960,000 | 600 |
| SIM card + setup | 16,000 | 10 |
| Monthly transport pass | 96,000 | 60 |
| Food (first month) | 240,000 | 150 |
| **Phase 4 total** | **2,192,000** | **1,370** |

## Total

| | NGN | EUR |
|---|---|---|
| Out-of-pocket (excl. blocked account) | 3,606,800 | 2,254 |
| Blocked account (returned monthly) | 17,932,800 | 11,208 |
| **Total funds needed** | **21,539,600** | **13,462** |

## What the blocked account means in practice

EUR 934/month from blocked account + up to 20hrs/week work (EUR 600-1,200/month at minimum wage) = EUR 1,534-2,134/month. Shared accommodation in Frankfurt or Berlin: EUR 500-800/month. Tight but manageable.

## Not included

German health insurance (Krankenversicherung) — approximately EUR 200-400/month once registered. Professional association fees. Interview travel costs.
