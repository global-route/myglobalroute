# How Chisom Got a Canadian Work Permit in 6 Months

## Quick Summary

- **Profile:** Chisom, 28, Software Engineer, Lagos
- **Education:** B.Sc Computer Science (UNILAG)
- **Experience:** 4 years (2 Nigerian companies)
- **Budget:** CAD 12,000 (~₦6.5 million)
- **Timeline:** 6 months from application to arrival
- **Pathway:** Express Entry (Federal Skilled Worker)
- **Current status:** Working in Toronto, CAD 85,000/year salary

**Key insight:** Chisom had **average credentials** (not top-tier university, not FAANG company)—but strategic planning and proper documentation got him approved.

---

## The Background

### Who is Chisom?

**Age:** 28  
**Location:** Lagos, Nigeria → Toronto, Canada  
**Education:** B.Sc Computer Science, University of Lagos (2018)  
**Work experience:**
- 2 years at a Lagos fintech startup (Backend Engineer)
- 2 years at an e-commerce company (Full-stack Developer)

**Why Canada?**
- Better salary (₦350K/month in Lagos vs. CAD 85K/year in Toronto)
- Career growth (access to global tech scene)
- Family future (better schools for his daughter)

---

## The Timeline

### Month 1: Research & Preparation

**Week 1-2: Choosing the pathway**

Chisom researched:
- ❌ Study route (too expensive: CAD 40K+ for college)
- ❌ Provincial Nominee Program (PNP) (requires job offer first)
- ✅ **Express Entry** (points-based, no job offer needed)

**Why Express Entry?**
- Software engineers = **high demand (NOC 21232)**
- 4 years experience = good points
- Can apply from Nigeria
- 6-month processing time

---

**Week 3-4: Calculating CRS score**

Chisom used the [CRS calculator](https://www.cic.gc.ca/english/immigrate/skilled/crs-tool.asp):

| Factor | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Age (28) | 110 |
| Education (Bachelor's) | 120 |
| Work experience (4 years) | 50 |
| Language (IELTS 8.5) | 124 |
| **Total** | **404 points** |

**Cutoff score (Jan 2026):** 490 points  
**Problem:** Chisom was **86 points short**

---

### Month 2: Boosting CRS Score

Chisom needed more points. Options:

**Option 1: Get a job offer (+50 or +200 points)**
- Difficult without Canadian experience
- Takes 3-6 months to land offer

**Option 2: Get a Master's degree (+30 points)**
- Takes 1-2 years
- Too slow

**Option 3: Improve French language (+50 points)**
- Chisom doesn't speak French
- Would take 6-12 months to learn

**Option 4: Get provincial nomination (+600 points guaranteed ITA)**
- **Best option** (instant approval)

---

**Chisom's Strategy: Ontario HCP (Tech Draw)**

**What is Ontario HCP?**
- Human Capital Priorities Stream
- Ontario invites Express Entry candidates in **tech occupations**
- **Software engineers qualify**
- Adds **600 points** to CRS score
- Guaranteed Invitation to Apply (ITA)

**Requirements:**
- CRS score 350+ (Chisom had 404 ✅)
- 12 months work experience (Chisom had 48 months ✅)
- Bachelor's degree (Chisom had one ✅)
- NOC 21232 (Software Engineer) ✅

**Timeline:** Ontario draws every 2-4 weeks

---

### Month 3: Taking IELTS & Getting ECA

**Week 1: IELTS Test**

Chisom prepared for 3 weeks:
- Watched YouTube tutorials
- Practiced with past papers
- Took mock tests

**Test day:** British Council Lagos  
**Cost:** ₦92,000

**Results (2 weeks later):**
- Listening: 8.5
- Reading: 9.0
- Writing: 7.5
- Speaking: 8.0
- **Overall: 8.5** (CLB 10) ✅

**Language points:** 124 (maximum)

---

**Week 2-4: Educational Credential Assessment (ECA)**

Chisom needed to prove his Nigerian degree = Canadian Bachelor's.

**Steps:**
1. Chose **WES (World Education Services)**
2. Ordered UNILAG transcript (₦15,000)
3. Sent to WES Canada
4. Paid WES fee (CAD 240 = ₦130,000)
5. Wait 6 weeks

**Result:** "Bachelor's Degree (4 years)" ✅

**Total cost (IELTS + ECA):** ₦237,000

---

### Month 4: Creating Express Entry Profile

**Week 1: Gathering documents**

Chisom prepared:
- [ ] Passport (valid 2+ years)
- [ ] IELTS results
- [ ] WES ECA report
- [ ] Proof of work experience
  - Offer letters (2 companies)
  - Resignation letters
  - Payslips (last 3 months per job)
  - Employment reference letters (detailed job duties)
- [ ] Police certificate (Nigeria Police Force)
- [ ] Proof of funds (CAD 13,310 = ₦7.2M in bank account)

**Most important: Employment reference letters**

Chisom's letters included:
- Job title
- Dates of employment
- Salary
- **Detailed job duties** (matched NOC 21232 description):
  - "Developed RESTful APIs using Node.js and Python"
  - "Designed database schemas (PostgreSQL, MongoDB)"
  - "Collaborated with frontend team using React"
  - "Led code reviews and mentored junior developers"

**Tip:** Letters MUST match NOC description. Generic letters = rejection.

---

**Week 2: Creating profile**

Chisom created his Express Entry profile online:
1. Went to [IRCC website](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html)
2. Created GCKey account
3. Filled in profile:
   - Personal details
   - Education (linked WES report)
   - Work experience (linked reference letters)
   - Language (linked IELTS)
   - Proof of funds

**CRS score calculated:** 404 points

**Status:** Entered pool, waiting for Ontario nomination

---

### Month 5: Ontario Nomination & ITA

**Week 1: Ontario Notification of Interest (NOI)**

**Feb 15, 2026:** Ontario held a **Tech Draw**

Chisom received **Notification of Interest (NOI)** email:
> "You may be eligible for nomination by Ontario. You have 45 days to apply."

**What this means:** Ontario is interested. Chisom must apply to Ontario separately.

---

**Week 2: Ontario application**

Chisom submitted Ontario application:
1. Logged into [Ontario PNP portal](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontarios-immigrant-nominee-program)
2. Uploaded:
   - Resume
   - Employment letters
   - Proof he intends to live in Ontario
3. Paid fee: CAD 1,500 (₦810,000)

**Processing time:** 30-60 days

---

**Week 4: Ontario Nomination Approved**

**March 10, 2026:** Chisom received approval email:
> "You have been nominated by Ontario. Your CRS score has increased by 600 points."

**New CRS score:** 404 + 600 = **1,004 points** 🎉

---

**Week 4 (same day): Invitation to Apply (ITA)**

Within **hours** of Ontario nomination, Chisom received ITA from IRCC:
> "You are invited to apply for permanent residence. You have 60 days to submit."

**This is it—guaranteed PR** (if documents are correct).

---

### Month 6: Final Application & Approval

**Week 1-2: Submitting PR application**

Chisom submitted full application:
- All documents (scanned)
- Police certificate
- Medical exam (₦65,000 at IOM Lagos)
- Biometrics (₦27,000 at VFS Global)
- Proof of funds (bank statement + letter)
- Payment: CAD 2,300 (₦1.24M)

**Total submitted:** 47 documents, 238 pages

---

**Week 3-4: Waiting**

IRCC processing time: 6 months (official)  
**Chisom's experience:** 4 weeks

**Status updates:**
- Week 1: Medical passed
- Week 2: Background check in progress
- Week 3: Background check complete
- Week 4: Approved!

---

**April 15, 2026: Confirmation of Permanent Residence (COPR)**

Chisom received COPR email + PDF:
> "Your application for permanent residence has been approved. You must land in Canada by September 15, 2026."

**He did it** 🎉

## Lessons Learned

### What Worked

1. **Ontario PNP strategy:** Instead of waiting for federal draw, targeted provincial nomination (600 points)
2. **Perfect employment letters:** Matched NOC description exactly (critical)
3. **High IELTS score:** Studied seriously, got CLB 10
4. **Organized documents:** Scanned everything, labeled clearly
5. **Applied immediately:** After ITA, submitted within 2 weeks (not 60-day deadline)

---

### What He'd Do Differently

1. **Start saving earlier:** Needed ₦11.4M—took 18 months to save
2. **Network before landing:** Should've connected with Nigerian tech community in Toronto earlier
3. **Get job offer before leaving:** Landed in Canada with no job (risky, but worked out)
4. **Research housing:** Toronto rent shocked him (CAD 2,200/month for 1-bedroom)

---

### Advice for Others

**From Chisom:**

> "If I can do it, you can too. I'm not from a rich family—my dad is a teacher, my mom is a nurse. I saved ₦100K/month for 18 months while working in Lagos.
>
> The key is **strategic planning**. Don't just apply blindly. Calculate your CRS score. Identify your gaps. Boost your points.
>
> For software engineers, **Ontario PNP is the hack**. Federal draws are competitive (490+ points). Ontario invites you at 350+.
>
> Also, **employment letters matter more than you think**. Mine were rejected once because they were generic. I rewrote them to match NOC duties exactly—approved next time.
>
> Don't give up. I had doubts. My friends said I was wasting money. But I'm here now, earning 10x my Lagos salary. It's worth it."

## FAQ

### Q: Do I need a job offer to apply for Express Entry?

**A:** No. Chisom didn't have one. Job offer adds points but isn't required.

---

### Q: How long did it take from start to landing?

**A:** 
- Month 1-2: Research + prep
- Month 3: IELTS + ECA
- Month 4: Express Entry profile
- Month 5: Ontario nomination + ITA
- Month 6: PR approval
- **Total: 6 months**

---

### Q: What if I don't have CAD 13,310 saved?

**A:** You can:
- Borrow from family (return after landing)
- Get a loan
- Show joint funds with spouse
- But you MUST show it (IRCC requirement)

---

### Q: Can I apply for Express Entry while working in Nigeria?

**A:** Yes. Chisom applied while working full-time. It's all done online.

---

### Q: What's the current CRS cutoff?

**A:** Varies:
- Federal draws: 480-510 points (2026)
- Ontario PNP: 350+ points (easier)
- Check [CRS Tracker](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/submit-profile/rounds-invitations.html)
