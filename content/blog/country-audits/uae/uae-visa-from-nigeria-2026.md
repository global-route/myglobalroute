The UAE - and Dubai in particular - is the most active Gulf destination for African professionals. It has four distinct work-related visa routes, each suited to a different profile. Understanding which route applies to you is the first step.

## Route 1 - Employment Visa (employer-sponsored)

The standard route for most African workers in the UAE. Your employer applies for a work permit from MOHRE (Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation) before you apply for the visa.

**Process:**
1. Employer obtains work permit approval from MOHRE
2. You apply for an employment entry visa at the UAE consulate in Nigeria
3. You enter the UAE and complete a medical examination
4. You receive your Emirates ID and residence visa (valid 2 years, renewable)

**No universal minimum salary:** MOHRE does not publish a single minimum salary threshold for all occupations. Salary requirements vary by occupation category, emirate and employer type. Your employment contract must specify your salary.

**Who pays the fees:** Government work permit and visa fees are typically paid by the employer. If you are being asked to pay large upfront fees, ask for a breakdown showing what is government fee and what is agent fee.

**Primary source:** `https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id`

## Route 2 - Green Visa (self-sponsored skilled worker)

Introduced in 2022, the Green Visa allows skilled workers to sponsor themselves without an employer.

**Requirements:**
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent
- Minimum monthly salary of AED 15,000 (approximately USD 4,085 or NGN 6.1M/month)
- Valid employment contract or proof of self-employment income

**Duration:** 5 years, renewable.

**Key difference from Employment Visa:** You are not tied to a specific employer. You can change jobs without losing your visa status.

**Primary source:** `https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/types-of-visa/green-visa`

## Route 3 - Freelance Permit

For self-employed professionals and independent contractors. Issued by UAE free zone authorities, not MOHRE.

**Requirements:**
- Proof of professional qualifications or portfolio
- Application to a free zone authority (Dubai Media City, Dubai Internet City, Fujairah Creative City, etc.)
- Annual fee: AED 7,500 - 15,000 depending on the free zone

**What it allows:** You can work as a freelancer for multiple clients in the UAE. You cannot be employed by a UAE company on a freelance permit - that requires an employment visa.

**Primary source:** Check the specific free zone authority for current fees and requirements.

## Route 4 - Golden Visa (long-term residence)

The Golden Visa is a 10-year renewable residence permit for high-value individuals.

**Categories and thresholds:**

| Category | Minimum requirement |
|---|---|
| Real estate investor | AED 2,000,000 property value |
| Business investor | AED 2,000,000 investment in a UAE company |
| Entrepreneur | Approved startup with AED 500,000 capital |
| Exceptional talent | Accreditation from relevant UAE authority |
| Outstanding student | GPA 3.75+ from UAE or top global university |

**Duration:** 10 years, renewable.

**Primary source:** `https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/visa-and-emirates-id/types-of-visa/golden-visa`

## What to verify before accepting any UAE job offer

> Before paying any agent fees or signing any contract, verify: the employer's trade licence number (searchable on the Dubai Department of Economic Development website), the employment contract terms in writing, and who is responsible for visa fees.

**Documents you will need:**
- Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
- Passport photographs
- Educational certificates (attested)
- Medical fitness certificate
- Police clearance certificate
