# Canada Visa from Nigeria: The 2026 Complete Guide

## Quick Summary

- **Study permit minimum funds (family size 1):** CAD 23,448/year living costs (outside Quebec, from 2026-09-01)
- **Express Entry minimum selection points:** 67/100; CRS cut-offs in the 480–520 range in recent 2026 draws
- **Settlement funds (FSW, family size 1):** CAD 14,690
- **Processing time:** Study permit 4–12 weeks online; Express Entry PR 6 months from ITA
- **Official source:** [IRCC - Government of Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/immigration-citizenship.html)

**Evidence note:** Approval rates by nationality are not published by IRCC. This guide covers documented requirements only.

---

## Study Permit

Canada requires a study permit for any course longer than 6 months at a Designated Learning Institution (DLI).

### Requirements

- **Admission offer:** Acceptance letter from a DLI
- **Minimum living funds (outside Quebec, family size 1, from 2026-09-01):** CAD 23,448/year - [source: IRCC financial support](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/get-documents/financial-support.html)
- **Tuition fees:** Separate - typically CAD 15,000–30,000/year for international students at Canadian universities
- **Genuine student requirement:** You must demonstrate the primary purpose of your stay is study

### Living funds by family size (outside Quebec, from 2026-09-01)

| Family size | Annual living funds required (CAD) |
|---|---|
| 1 | 23,448 |
| 2 | 29,192 |
| 3 | 35,888 |
| 4 | 43,572 |
| 5 | 49,419 |
| 6 | 55,736 |
| 7 | 62,054 |

For family sizes above 7, IRCC adds CAD 6,318 per additional member.

### Post-study work rights

After graduation from an eligible DLI, you may be eligible for a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) of up to 3 years, depending on programme length. The PGWP is a direct pathway into Express Entry.

## Federal Skilled Worker Program (Express Entry)

Express Entry is Canada's invitation-based system for skilled workers seeking permanent residency.

### Requirements

- **Minimum selection factor points:** 67 out of 100 - [source: IRCC FSWP](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/who-can-apply/federal-skilled-workers/six-selection-factors-federal-skilled-workers.html)
- **Minimum work experience:** 1 year continuous full-time (or equivalent part-time) in a NOC TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3 occupation in the past 10 years
- **Language:** CLB 7 minimum in English or French (IELTS General Training 6.0 in each band)
- **Education:** Secondary school minimum; post-secondary education earns more points
- **Settlement funds (family size 1):** CAD 14,690 - exempt if you have a valid Canadian job offer

### How the CRS works

After meeting the 67-point threshold, you enter the Express Entry pool. IRCC conducts draws and issues Invitations to Apply (ITAs) to the highest-scoring candidates. Recent 2026 draw cut-offs have been in the 480–520 CRS range. Your CRS score is calculated from age, education, language, work experience, and adaptability factors.

## Key barriers for Nigerian applicants

These are documented process barriers - not approval rate statistics:

1. **Financial proof:** Bank statements must clearly show the required funds are genuinely accessible. Statements showing sudden large deposits close to the application date are scrutinised.
2. **Document authentication:** Transcripts and certificates must be verified. WES (World Education Services) assessment is required for most Express Entry applications.
3. **Language test:** IELTS or CELPIP results must be recent (within 2 years of application).
4. **Biometrics:** Required for most Canadian visa applications - book early at a VAC in Lagos or Abuja.
