Malta is an English-speaking EU island nation in the Mediterranean. It has two publishable pathways for Nigerian applicants: a study residence permit and a Single Permit for work. Both are issued by Identità Malta.

## Study residence permit

**Requirements:** Acceptance letter from a recognised Maltese educational institution, valid passport, proof of sufficient financial means, health insurance.

**Tuition fees:** The University of Malta charges approximately EUR 4,000-8,000/year for non-EU students depending on the programme. Living costs in Malta are approximately EUR 700-900/month.

**English language:** Malta is English-speaking — no Maltese language requirement for most programmes.

**Processing time:** 1-2 months.

Primary source: `https://identita.gov.mt/residence-permits/`

## Single Permit (work)

**Requirements:** Confirmed job offer from a Maltese employer. The employer must obtain labour market clearance from Jobsplus (Malta's employment agency) before applying for the Single Permit on your behalf.

**Processing time:** 2-3 months.

**Path to PR:** After 5 years of continuous legal residence in Malta.

Primary source: `https://identita.gov.mt/single-permit/`
