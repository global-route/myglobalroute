Poland is an EU member state with affordable tuition fees and a large international student community. It has two publishable pathways for Nigerian applicants: a study residence permit and an Employee Card work permit.

## Study residence permit

**Requirements:** Acceptance or enrolment letter from a recognised Polish higher education institution, valid passport, proof of sufficient financial means, health insurance.

**Tuition fees:** EUR 1,000 to EUR 4,000/year for non-EU students. Living costs in Warsaw are approximately EUR 500-700/month, lower in Krakow, Wroclaw and other cities.

**Work rights:** International students can work without a separate work permit.

**Processing time:** 1-2 months via the Office for Foreigners (UDSC).

**Path to PR:** After 5 years of continuous legal residence in Poland.

Primary source: `https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc-en/study`

## Employee Card (work permit)

**Requirements:** Confirmed job offer from a Polish employer for a position registered in the central vacancy database for Employee Card holders. The employer applies for the Employee Card on your behalf.

**Processing time:** 2-3 months.

Primary source: `https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc-en/work`

## Evidence note

All figures sourced from the Office for Foreigners Poland at `https://www.gov.pl/web/udsc-en`, retrieved October 2026. Verify current requirements before applying.
