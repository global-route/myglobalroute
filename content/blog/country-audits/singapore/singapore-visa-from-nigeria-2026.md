# Singapore Visa from Nigeria 2026

## Overview

Singapore is one of Asia's most active professional hubs, with strong demand in finance, technology, engineering, healthcare and logistics. It does not have a points-based independent skilled migration route — all work visas require employer sponsorship.

The two main routes for African professionals are:

| Route | For | Minimum salary |
|---|---|---|
| Employment Pass (EP) | Professionals, managers, executives | SGD 5,000/month |
| S Pass | Mid-skilled workers | SGD 3,150/month |

---

## Employment Pass (EP)

The Employment Pass is Singapore's primary work visa for professionals, managers and executives.

### Requirements

- Job offer from a Singapore-registered employer
- Minimum monthly salary of SGD 5,000 (SGD 5,500 for financial services)
- Pass the COMPASS points assessment (for new applications)
- Employer applies via EP Online

### COMPASS framework

From September 2023, new EP applications are assessed under the COMPASS (Complementarity Assessment Framework) points system. Your employer submits the assessment. Points are awarded across four criteria:

| Criterion | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Salary | How your salary compares to local professionals in the same occupation |
| Qualifications | Whether you have a degree from a top-ranked university |
| Diversity | Whether hiring you improves nationality diversity at the employer |
| Support for local employment | Whether the employer has a good track record of hiring Singaporeans |

Minimum score: 40 points out of 60. Candidates who score below 40 are not eligible for an EP regardless of salary.

**What this means for African applicants:** The diversity criterion can work in your favour — if your employer has few African employees, hiring you may add diversity points. The qualifications criterion favours degrees from globally ranked universities.

### Validity and renewal

Initial EP: 2 years. Renewal: up to 3 years. No limit on renewals as long as you remain employed and meet the criteria.

### Primary source

mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass

## S Pass

The S Pass is for mid-skilled workers — technicians, supervisors and specialists who do not qualify for the EP.

### Requirements

- Job offer from a Singapore-registered employer
- Minimum monthly salary of SGD 3,150
- Employer applies on your behalf
- Subject to employer sector quota limits

### Quota limits

This is the key constraint for the S Pass. Employers can only hire a fixed percentage of their workforce on S Passes:

- Services sector: up to 10% of total workforce
- Construction, marine, process and manufacturing sectors: up to 15-18%

If your employer has already reached their quota, they cannot hire you on an S Pass regardless of your qualifications.

### Validity

Initial S Pass: 2 years. Renewal: up to 3 years.

### Primary source

mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/s-pass

## How the application process works

Both EP and S Pass applications are employer-initiated:

1. You receive a job offer from a Singapore employer
2. Your employer submits the EP or S Pass application via EP Online (mom.gov.sg/eservices/ep-online)
3. MOM processes the application (typically 3 weeks for EP, 3 weeks for S Pass)
4. If approved, you receive an In-Principle Approval (IPA) letter
5. You apply for an entry visa at the Singapore High Commission in Abuja (if required)
6. You enter Singapore and complete the pass issuance process

### Does Nigeria need a visa to enter Singapore?

Nigerian passport holders require a visa to enter Singapore. Apply at the Singapore High Commission in Abuja or via a Singapore-accredited travel agent. The IPA letter from MOM is required for the visa application.

## Sectors with high demand for African professionals

Singapore's labour market is competitive. Sectors where African professionals have found employment include:

- **Finance and banking:** Singapore is a major financial hub. Nigerian professionals with CFA, ACCA or banking experience have found roles in compliance, risk and corporate banking.
- **Technology:** Software engineers, data analysts and cybersecurity professionals are in demand. Many global tech companies have Singapore offices.
- **Healthcare:** Nurses and allied health professionals can work in Singapore, but professional registration with the Singapore Nursing Board (SNB) or relevant authority is required.
- **Engineering:** Oil and gas, marine and process engineering roles exist, particularly in Jurong Island.
