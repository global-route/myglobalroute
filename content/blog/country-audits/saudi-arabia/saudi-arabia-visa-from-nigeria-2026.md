Saudi Arabia is the largest economy in the Gulf and a major employer of African professionals. Vision 2030 - the kingdom's economic diversification programme - has created new sectors and new visa routes. Here is what official sources confirm.

## Standard work visa - Kafala system

Saudi Arabia's standard work visa operates through the Kafala (sponsorship) system. Your employer applies for a work permit from the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) before you can apply for a visa.

**Process:**
1. Employer applies for work permit from MHRSD
2. Employer receives approval and issues you a visa authorisation
3. You apply for an employment visa at the Saudi consulate in Nigeria
4. You enter Saudi Arabia and complete a medical examination
5. You receive your Iqama (residence permit) - valid 1-2 years, renewable

**Processing time:** 4-8 weeks after employer application.

**Primary source:** `https://www.mol.gov.sa/`

## The Nitaqat quota system

Saudi Arabia's Nitaqat system requires employers to maintain a minimum ratio of Saudi nationals to expatriate workers. This affects which roles are available to foreign workers.

**What this means for African applicants:**
- Employers in the "Platinum" or "Green" Nitaqat band can sponsor more expatriate workers
- Employers in the "Yellow" or "Red" band cannot sponsor new expatriate workers
- Healthcare, construction and domestic services have different Nitaqat thresholds
- Ask any potential employer about their current Nitaqat status before accepting an offer

## Premium Residency - for high-net-worth individuals

The Premium Residency (Iqama Mumayaza) is a long-term residence permit that does not require employer sponsorship.

**Two categories:**

**Permanent Premium Residency:**
- One-time fee: SAR 800,000 (approximately USD 213,000 or NGN 319M)
- No annual renewal required
- Allows you to work, invest and reside in Saudi Arabia without employer sponsorship

**Temporary Premium Residency:**
- Annual fee: SAR 100,000 (approximately USD 26,700 or NGN 40M)
- Renewable annually
- Same rights as permanent category

**Primary source:** `https://www.iqama.gov.sa/`

## What to verify before accepting a Saudi job offer

> Saudi Arabia has strict labour laws but enforcement varies. Get your employment contract in Arabic and English before travelling. Verify the employer's Nitaqat status and ensure your contract specifies salary, working hours, accommodation, and repatriation terms.

**Documents required:**
- Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
- Passport photographs
- Educational certificates (attested and legalised)
- Medical fitness certificate
- Police clearance certificate
