Japan offers two evidence-gated pathways for Nigerian applicants: the student visa and the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services visa. Both require a Certificate of Eligibility (CoE) filed by your institution or employer before you apply at the embassy.

## Quick summary

- Student visa: admission offer required, institution files CoE, MEXT scholarship available
- Engineer/Specialist visa: job offer required, employer files CoE, relevant degree required
- MEXT scholarship: covers tuition + JPY 117,000/month + return airfare
- Processing time: 4-8 weeks after CoE is issued

### MEXT Scholarship

The MEXT scholarship is Japan's government-funded scholarship for international students.

**What it covers:**
- Full tuition waiver
- Monthly living allowance: JPY 117,000 (~USD 780) for graduate students
- Return economy airfare
- Japanese language training before your programme

**How to apply from Nigeria:**
1. Applications open at the Japanese Embassy in Abuja, typically April-May each year.
2. Submit: application form, academic transcripts, research plan (graduate applicants), language test results.
3. Embassy screening: written exam and interview in Abuja.
4. Successful candidates are recommended to Japanese universities.
5. Final selection by the university and MEXT.

### Application process (self-funded)

1. Apply to a Japanese university and receive an admission letter.
2. The university files for a CoE with the Immigration Services Agency (1-3 months).
3. Once the CoE is issued, apply at the Japanese Embassy in Abuja: CoE, passport, visa form, passport photos.
4. Processing: 4-8 weeks.

## Pathway 2 - Engineer / Specialist in Humanities / International Services

**Registry ID:** `JP-engineer` | **Status:** Publishable

Japan's primary work visa for skilled professionals in technology, engineering, finance, law, and international business.

### Requirements

| Requirement | Value | Source | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job offer | Required | moj.go.jp | 2026-09-07 |
| Relevant degree or 10 years experience | Required | moj.go.jp | 2026-09-07 |
| Certificate of Eligibility | Required (filed by employer) | moj.go.jp | 2026-09-07 |

**Source:** [moj.go.jp/isa/applications/resources/nyukan_nyukan69.html](https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/resources/nyukan_nyukan69.html?hl=en&lang=tk) - verified 2026-09-07

### Application process from Nigeria

1. Secure a job offer from a Japanese employer.
2. Employer files for a CoE with the Immigration Services Agency (1-3 months).
3. Once the CoE is issued, apply at the Japanese Embassy in Abuja.
4. Processing: 4-8 weeks after CoE.

### Salary

No fixed minimum is published. Salary must be equivalent to what a Japanese national earns in the same role. Entry-level tech roles in Tokyo: JPY 3,000,000-4,000,000/year (~USD 20,000-27,000).

---

## Common rejection reasons

- CoE not filed before visa application (most common error)
- Degree not in a field relevant to the job offer
- Employer not registered or not in good standing with Japanese authorities
