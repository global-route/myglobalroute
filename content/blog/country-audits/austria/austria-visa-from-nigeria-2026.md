Austria offers two evidence-gated pathways for Nigerian applicants: the Red-White-Red (RWR) Card for skilled workers and the study residence permit. No approval rates are published by Austrian authorities for Nigerian applicants.

## Quick summary

- Red-White-Red Card: 55 points minimum, job offer required, EUR 2,685/month minimum salary
- Study visa: EUR 1,217/month in funds, university admission offer required
- Processing time: 8-12 weeks at the Austrian embassy
- PR pathway: RWR Card holders can apply for permanent residence after 5 years

---

## Pathway 1 - Red-White-Red Card (Other Key Workers)

**Registry ID:** `AT-rwr-other-key-workers` | **Status:** Publishable

The Red-White-Red Card is Austria's points-based skilled worker visa for non-EU nationals in shortage occupations.

### Points system

Minimum score: **55 points out of 100**

| Category | Maximum points |
|---|---|
| Special qualifications / skills | 40 |
| Work experience | 20 |
| Age (under 35 scores highest) | 15 |
| Language skills (German or English) | 15 |
| Job offer in Austria | 10 |

A job offer is mandatory regardless of total score.

**Source:** [migration.gv.at](https://www.migration.gv.at/index.php?id=1051) - verified 2026-09-14

### Salary requirement

Minimum gross monthly salary: **EUR 2,685** (2026). Set by the Austrian Federal Economic Chamber, updated annually.

### Application process from Nigeria

1. Secure a job offer from an Austrian employer (employer must complete a labour market test).
2. Calculate your points score at migration.gv.at.
3. Apply at the Austrian Embassy in Abuja. Book an appointment - waiting times 4-8 weeks.
4. Submit: application form, passport, certified degree certificates, employment contract, proof of accommodation.
5. Processing: 8-12 weeks.

### Common rejection reasons

- Points score below 55
- Employer did not complete the labour market test
- Degree certificates not certified and translated into German
- Insufficient proof of accommodation in Austria

## Pathway 2 - Study Residence Permit

**Registry ID:** `AT-study` | **Status:** Publishable

Non-EU students pay approximately EUR 726/semester (EUR 1,452/year) at most Austrian public universities.

### Requirements

| Requirement | Value | Source | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admission offer | Required | oesterreich.gv.at | 2026-09-13 |
| Minimum monthly funds | EUR 1,217 | oesterreich.gv.at | 2026-09-13 |
| Health insurance | Required | Austrian embassy | 2026-09-13 |

**Source:** [oesterreich.gv.at](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/en/themen/migration_und_integration.html) - verified 2026-09-13

### Costs

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Tuition (public university, non-EU) | EUR 726/semester |
| Living funds required | EUR 1,217/month |
| Visa application fee | EUR 100 |
| Health insurance | EUR 50-100/month |

### Post-study options

After completing a degree in Austria, you can apply for a 12-month job-seeker visa, then transition to the RWR Card if you secure skilled employment.
