# UK Visa from Nigeria: Skilled Worker and Student Visa Guide 2026

## Quick Summary

- **Skilled Worker minimum salary:** £41,700/year (baseline - occupation going rate may be higher)
- **Skilled Worker maintenance funds:** £1,270 available balance (28-day hold) unless employer certifies
- **Student visa minimum age:** 16 years
- **Student visa maintenance funds:** £1,334/month (London) or £1,023/month (outside London) for up to 9 months
- **Official source:** [UK Visas and Immigration - GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/browse/visas-immigration)

**Evidence note:** UKVI does not publish approval rates by nationality. This guide covers documented requirements only.

---

## Skilled Worker Visa

The UK Skilled Worker visa is the primary route for professionals with a confirmed job offer from a UK employer.

### Requirements

- **Job offer required:** Yes - from a UK employer with a valid sponsor licence - [source: UKVI](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa)
- **Certificate of Sponsorship (CoS):** The employer must issue a CoS before you apply
- **Eligible occupation:** The job must be in an eligible occupation at the required skill level (RQF Level 3 or above)
- **Minimum salary:** £41,700/year (baseline threshold, effective 2026-09-12) - [source: UKVI salary requirements](https://www.gov.uk/skilled-worker-visa/your-job). The applicable occupation going rate may be higher. Lower salary routes and transitional rules apply in specific circumstances.
- **Maintenance funds:** £1,270 available balance held for 28 days before application - unless your employer certifies they will cover your maintenance. This is an available balance, not an annual cost.
- **English language:** Required - CEFR B1 level minimum

### Path to settlement

- Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR) after 5 years on a Skilled Worker visa
- British citizenship eligibility 1 year after ILR

### Shortage occupations

Some occupations on the Immigration Salary List qualify for a lower salary threshold. Check the current list on GOV.UK - it is updated periodically.

## Student Visa

The UK Student visa is for international students aged 16 and over studying at a licensed student sponsor.

### Requirements

- **Admission offer:** Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) from a licensed UK student sponsor - [source: UKVI](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa)
- **Minimum age:** 16 years
- **Maintenance funds:** You must show you can cover your course fees and living costs:
  - London: £1,334/month for up to 9 months (£12,006 maximum)
  - Outside London: £1,023/month for up to 9 months (£9,207 maximum)
  - Funds must be held for 28 consecutive days before application
- **English language:** CEFR B2 level minimum (IELTS Academic 5.5 in each band for most courses)

### Tuition fees

UK university tuition for international students is typically £15,000–£35,000/year depending on institution and programme. This is separate from the maintenance funds requirement.

### Post-study work rights

The Graduate Route allows you to work in the UK for 2 years after completing an eligible undergraduate or master's degree (3 years after a PhD). No job offer required for the Graduate Route.

---

## Key barriers for Nigerian applicants

These are documented process barriers - not approval rate statistics:

1. **Financial proof:** Maintenance funds must be in your own account or a parent/legal guardian's account. The 28-day holding period is strictly assessed.
2. **CAS validity:** Your CAS has an expiry date - apply for your visa promptly after receiving it.
3. **Biometrics:** Required - book at a UKVCAS service point in Lagos or Abuja.
4. **Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS):** Required for most visa categories - currently £1,035/year for students, £1,035/year for workers. This is a significant upfront cost paid at application.
