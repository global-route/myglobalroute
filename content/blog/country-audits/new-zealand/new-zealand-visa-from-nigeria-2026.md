New Zealand offers evidence-gated pathways through the student visa and three Skilled Migrant Category (SMC) sub-routes. The SMC is a direct residence pathway - not a temporary work visa.

## Quick summary

- Student visa: NZD 20,000/year in funds, admission offer required
- SMC points-based: 6 points minimum, job offer in a skilled role required
- SMC skilled work experience: job offer + 3 years experience
- SMC trades and technician: job offer + relevant qualification
- Processing time: 4-6 months for SMC residence applications

### Post-study pathway

After a degree of 2+ years, you may be eligible for a Post-Study Work Visa (1-3 years), which can lead to the SMC if you secure skilled employment.

## Pathway 2 — SMC: Points-Based

**Registry ID:** `NZ-smc-points-based` | **Status:** Publishable

Direct residence visa for skilled workers with a job offer in New Zealand.

### Points system — minimum 6 points

| Factor | Points |
|---|---|
| Skilled employment in NZ (job offer or current) | 3 |
| Qualification (bachelor's or higher) | 2 |
| Work experience 2-5 years | 1 |
| Work experience 6-10 years | 2 |
| Age 20-39 | 1 |
| Partner with skilled employment or qualification | 1 |

**Source:** [immigration.govt.nz/visas/skilled-migrant-category-resident-visa](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/skilled-migrant-category-resident-visa/) — verified 2026-09-14

### Application process

1. Submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) at immigration.govt.nz.
2. If selected, receive an Invitation to Apply (ITA).
3. Submit residence application within 4 months of ITA.
4. Processing: 4-6 months.

## Pathway 3 — SMC: Skilled Work Experience

**Registry ID:** `NZ-smc-skilled-work-experience` | **Status:** Publishable

For workers with 3+ years experience in a skilled role (ANZSCO skill level 1, 2, or 3) and a current job offer in New Zealand.

| Requirement | Value | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| Job offer | Required | 2026-09-14 |
| Minimum work experience | 3 years | 2026-09-14 |

## Pathway 4 — SMC: Trades and Technician

**Registry ID:** `NZ-smc-trades-technician` | **Status:** Publishable

For qualified tradespeople (electricians, plumbers, carpenters, mechanics) with a job offer in New Zealand.

| Requirement | Value | Verified |
|---|---|---|
| Job offer | Required | 2026-09-14 |
| Relevant qualification | Required | 2026-09-14 |

Qualifications must be assessed by the New Zealand Qualifications Authority (NZQA) before applying.

---

## Common rejection reasons

- Points score below 6
- Job offer not in a skilled occupation (ANZSCO level 4 or 5 roles do not qualify)
- Qualifications not assessed by NZQA before application
- Chest X-ray not submitted (required for Nigerian applicants)
