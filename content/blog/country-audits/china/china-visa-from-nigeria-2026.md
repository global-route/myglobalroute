China has two publishable pathways for Nigerian applicants: a student visa (X1 for long-stay) and a work visa (Z visa). China is also notable for the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC), which provides fully funded study opportunities for Nigerian students.

## Student visa (X1)

**Admission notice:** A JW201 (CSC scholarship) or JW202 (self-funded) admission notice from a Chinese university approved by the Ministry of Education is required. The university issues this after accepting your application.

**Minimum funds:** Approximately USD 2,500/year for living expenses for self-funded students. China is a relatively low-cost study destination. Tuition fees are additional and vary by institution and programme (typically USD 2,000-5,000/year for English-taught programmes).

**CSC scholarship:** The Chinese Government Scholarship covers tuition, accommodation, a monthly stipend of CNY 2,500-3,500 (approximately USD 350-490/month), and medical insurance. Applications are submitted through the Chinese Embassy in Abuja, typically in January-March each year.

**Processing time:** 1-2 months after receiving the admission notice.

Primary source: `https://en.nia.gov.cn/`

## Work visa (Z visa)

**Qualification requirements:**
- **Category B (professional talent):** Bachelor's degree and at least 2 years of relevant work experience
- **Category A (high-end talent):** Higher thresholds — senior professionals, researchers, executives
- **Category C:** Other permitted occupations

**Process:** The employer must obtain a work permit notice (Z visa notification) from the local human resources authority before you apply for the Z visa at the Chinese Embassy in Abuja.

**Processing time:** 2-3 months.

Primary source: `https://en.nia.gov.cn/`

## Evidence note
