Sweden has two publishable pathways for Nigerian applicants: a study residence permit and a standard work permit. Both are issued by the Swedish Migration Agency (Migrationsverket).

## Study permit

**Minimum funds:** SEK 10,656/month (2026 figure). The authority notes this may be reduced where free food or housing is provided. Tuition fees are additional — non-EU students pay SEK 80,000 to SEK 140,000 per year at most institutions.

**Requirements:** Admission to a Swedish higher education institution, proof of funds, valid passport.

**Work rights:** No formal restriction on working hours alongside studies.

**Processing time:** 1-3 months via the Migrationsverket online portal.

**Path to PR:** After 5 years of continuous legal residence, you can apply for permanent residence.

Primary source: `https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/study/higher-education.html`

## Work permit

**Salary threshold:** SEK 34,470/month gross from 1 June 2026 (90% of the median salary of SEK 38,300). This is the job offer salary threshold — the employer must offer at least this amount. Exemptions and transitional rules apply.

**Requirements:** Job offer from a Swedish employer meeting the salary threshold. The employer advertises the vacancy in Sweden and the EU/EEA for at least 10 days before sponsoring a non-EU national.

**Processing time:** 1-3 months.

**Path to PR:** After 5 years of continuous legal residence.

Primary source: `https://www.migrationsverket.se/en/you-want-to-apply/work.html`

## Evidence note

All figures sourced from the Swedish Migration Agency at `https://www.migrationsverket.se/`, retrieved October 2026. Verify current requirements before applying.
