Hungary has two publishable pathways for Nigerian applicants: a study residence permit and a work residence permit. Hungary is also notable for the Stipendium Hungaricum scholarship, which provides fully funded study opportunities for Nigerian students.

## Study residence permit

**Requirements:** Valid acceptance letter from a Hungarian higher education institution, valid passport, proof of sufficient financial means, health insurance.

**Stipendium Hungaricum:** Hungary's government scholarship covers tuition and provides HUF 43,700/month (approximately EUR 110/month) plus accommodation support. Applications are submitted through the Nigerian Federal Ministry of Education, typically in October-November each year for the following academic year.

**Self-funded study:** Tuition fees range from EUR 2,000 to EUR 8,000/year. Living costs in Budapest are approximately EUR 500-700/month.

**Processing time:** 1-2 months.

Primary source: `https://oif.gov.hu/factsheets/residence-permit-for-the-purpose-of-studies`

## Work residence permit

**Requirements:** Valid job offer from a Hungarian employer, subject to labour-market test and quota rules. The employer must demonstrate that no suitable Hungarian or EU/EEA candidate was available.

**Processing time:** 2-3 months.

**Path to PR:** After 5 years of continuous legal residence in Hungary.

Primary source: `https://oif.gov.hu/factsheets/residence-permit-for-the-purpose-of-employment`

## Evidence note

All figures sourced from the National Directorate-General for Aliens Policing at `https://oif.gov.hu/`, retrieved October 2026. Verify current requirements before applying.
