# France Visa from Nigeria: The 2026 Reality Check

## Evidence status

Approval rates by nationality are not published by the French consular authorities and are not shown here. All requirements below are sourced from [france-visas.gouv.fr](https://france-visas.gouv.fr) with effective dates.

---

## What is available

Two publishable pathways in the MyPathway2Global registry:

| Pathway | Type | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Long-stay study visa | Study | Publishable |
| Talent Passport | Work | Publishable |

---

## Long-stay study visa (visa de long séjour étudiant)

**Official source:** [france-visas.gouv.fr - Student](https://france-visas.gouv.fr/en/web/france-visas/student)

### Core requirements

**Admission offer:** A letter of acceptance from a recognised French educational institution is required. For courses over 3 months, a long-stay visa (D visa) is required before travel.

**Campus France procedure:** Nigerian applicants must complete the Campus France pre-application procedure before submitting a visa application. This is a mandatory step - the visa application cannot proceed without it.

**Minimum funds (from 1 August 2026):**

| Requirement | Amount | Currency | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proof of resources | 877.50 | EUR | per month |

Source: france-visas.gouv.fr, effective 2026-08-01. This is the minimum for long-stay study applications submitted from 1 August 2026. Tuition fees are separate.

### Campus France Nigeria

Campus France manages the pre-application process for Nigerian students. The procedure involves an interview and document review before the visa application is submitted to the consulate.

**Campus France Nigeria:** [nigeria.campusfrance.org](https://nigeria.campusfrance.org)

Allow additional lead time for the Campus France step - it runs before the consulate appointment, not in parallel.

---

## Talent Passport (Passeport Talent)

**Official source:** [france-visas.gouv.fr - Talent Passport](https://france-visas.gouv.fr/en/web/france-visas/talent-passport)

The Talent Passport covers several sub-categories including the EU Blue Card, highly qualified employees, researchers, and company founders. The most commonly applicable sub-category for Nigerian professionals is the highly qualified employee route.

### Core requirements

**Job offer:** A confirmed employment contract from a French employer is required.

**Minimum annual salary (EU Blue Card sub-category, from 21 August 2025):**

| Requirement | Amount | Currency | Period |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum gross salary | 59,373 | EUR | annual |

Source: france-visas.gouv.fr, effective 2025-08-21. The EU Blue Card sub-category requires at least 1.5 times the gross reference salary. This is the job offer salary threshold, not a personal funds requirement.

### What is not published

- Approval rates by nationality
- Processing times by nationality (the consulate publishes general guidance, not nationality-specific data)

---

## Key planning considerations

**Campus France is mandatory for students:** This adds time to the process. Start the Campus France procedure well before your intended application date - do not treat it as a parallel step.

**EUR/NGN exchange rate:** The monthly funds threshold (EUR 877.50) and the Talent Passport salary threshold (EUR 59,373/year) are significant. Confirm current NGN equivalents with your bank.

**Talent Passport salary threshold:** EUR 59,373 gross per year is a high bar. This pathway is designed for senior professionals with confirmed French employer offers, not job seekers.

**Language:** Most undergraduate programmes are taught in French. English-taught Master's programmes exist but are fewer than in Germany or the Netherlands. Confirm the language of instruction before applying.

---

## What this audit does not cover

Visitor visas, working holiday arrangements, and other Talent Passport sub-categories (researcher, company founder, investor) are not currently in the evidence registry.
