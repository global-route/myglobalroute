Rwanda is the most business-friendly country in Africa by regulatory metrics. Business registration takes 1-2 days. The government actively courts foreign talent and investment. Here is what official sources confirm.

## Entry - visa-free for all African nationals

Rwanda offers visa-free entry to all African Union passport holders. Nigerian nationals receive a 30-day entry stamp on arrival, extendable to 90 days.

**This does not give you the right to work.** Employment requires a work permit.

## Business registration - 1-2 days online

Rwanda's business registration through the RDB online portal is the fastest in Africa.

**Process:**
1. Create an account on the RDB portal (`www.rdb.rw`)
2. Submit company name, business activity, directors and shareholders
3. Pay registration fee (approximately USD 50-100)
4. Receive certificate of incorporation within 1-2 working days

**Can be done remotely:** You do not need to be in Rwanda to register a company. The entire process is online.

**Primary source:** `https://www.rdb.rw/`

## Investor permit - for business owners and investors

**Requirements:**
- Minimum investment of USD 250,000 in Rwanda
- Registered company in Rwanda
- Valid passport

**Validity:** 10 years, renewable.

**What it provides:** Residence and work rights in Rwanda without employer sponsorship.

## Skilled worker permit - for employed professionals

**Requirements:**
- Confirmed job offer from a registered Rwandan employer
- Relevant qualifications and work experience
- Valid passport

**Processing time:** 1-2 weeks - significantly faster than most African countries.

## Class G permit - self-employed professionals

**Requirements:**
- Proof of professional qualifications
- Business plan or evidence of self-employment income
- Valid passport

## Why Rwanda for African professionals

**AfCFTA Secretariat:** Rwanda hosts the AfCFTA Secretariat in Kigali - creating professional opportunities in trade facilitation, policy and administration.

**Kigali International Financial Centre:** Rwanda's KIFC is positioning Kigali as a financial hub for East and Central Africa, with tax incentives for financial services firms.

**Technology sector:** Rwanda has invested heavily in technology infrastructure. Kigali has reliable internet, co-working spaces, and a growing startup ecosystem.

**Safety and stability:** Rwanda is consistently ranked among the safest countries in Africa. Kigali is one of the cleanest and most organised cities on the continent.

**Cost of living:** Kigali is more expensive than Kampala or Dar es Salaam but significantly cheaper than Nairobi or Accra.
