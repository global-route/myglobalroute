# Spain Visa from Nigeria: The 2026 Reality Check

## Quick Summary

- **Digital nomad visa minimum income:** €2,646/month (200% of SMI, 2026 rate)
- **Beckham Law tax benefit:** Flat 24% income tax for 6 years
- **Study visa living funds:** ~€600/month (indicative)
- **Processing time:** 20 working days for digital nomad visa (consulate target)
- **Official source:** [Spanish Ministry of Inclusion, Social Security and Migration](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/)

**Evidence note:** Spain does not publish visa approval rates by nationality. This guide covers documented requirements only.

---

## International Teleworker Visa (Digital Nomad Route)

Spain's International Teleworker visa, introduced under the Startup Law (Ley de Startups) in 2023, is the primary route for remote workers and digital nomads. It allows you to live in Spain while working remotely for employers or clients based outside Spain.

### Requirements

- **Remote employment or freelance contract:** Proof of employment with a company outside Spain, or service contracts with clients outside Spain. Spanish business activities are capped at 20% of total income.
- **Minimum monthly income:** €2,646/month (200% of the Spanish Minimum Interprofessional Wage - SMI). The SMI in 2026 is €1,323/month. This threshold applies to the applicant; additional amounts apply for dependants.
- **Work experience or qualification:** At least 3 years of professional experience in your field, or a university degree or higher vocational training certificate
- **Health insurance:** Full coverage health insurance valid in Spain
- **Clean criminal record:** Police certificate from Nigeria and any country of residence in the past 5 years
- **Official source:** [Ministry of Inclusion - International Teleworker](https://www.inclusion.gob.es/en/web/unidadgrandesempresas/teletrabajadores)

### Income threshold detail

The 200% SMI threshold is calculated on the gross monthly income from remote work. The SMI is reviewed annually by the Spanish government - confirm the current figure at the time of application. For 2026, the calculation is:

- SMI 2026: €1,323/month
- Required: 200% × €1,323 = **€2,646/month**

If you have dependants travelling with you, the threshold increases: 75% of SMI per additional adult (€992/month) and 25% per child (€331/month).

### Beckham Law (Ley Beckham) tax benefit

Qualifying new residents can apply for the Special Tax Regime for Impatriates (commonly called the Beckham Law). This gives you:

- **Flat 24% income tax rate** on Spanish-sourced income up to €600,000/year
- Applies for the year of arrival and the following **5 years** (6 years total)
- Income above €600,000 is taxed at 47%

**To qualify:**
- You must not have been a Spanish tax resident in the previous 5 years
- You must apply within 6 months of registering as a resident (empadronamiento)
- The application is made to the Spanish Tax Agency (AEAT)

**Important caveat:** The Beckham Law applies to Spanish-sourced income. If your income is entirely from foreign sources (which is the case for most digital nomad visa holders), the tax benefit may be less significant than it appears. Consult a Spanish tax adviser before relying on this.

### Permit duration and renewal

- Initial permit: 1 year (or 3 years if applying for the residence authorisation rather than the visa)
- Renewable for 2-year periods
- After 5 years of legal residence, you can apply for long-term residency

## Study Visa

Spain requires a student visa for any course longer than 90 days at a recognised Spanish institution.

### Requirements

- **Acceptance letter:** Admission to a recognised higher education or qualifying full-time study institution in Spain, with proof of fee payment or enrolment
- **Financial means:** Approximately €600/month for living costs (indicative - no single fixed threshold is published by the consulate)
- **Health insurance:** Full coverage valid in Spain
- **Accommodation proof:** Evidence of where you will live in Spain
- **Official source:** [Ministry of Inclusion - Study](https://inclusion.gob.es/en/web/migraciones/w/estancia-por-estudios)

### Tuition fees

Spain has both public and private universities. Public university tuition for international (non-EU) students typically ranges from €1,500-€3,500/year for undergraduate programmes - significantly lower than the UK or Ireland. Private universities charge more.

---

## Key barriers for Nigerian applicants

1. **Income documentation:** For the digital nomad visa, you must provide 3 months of payslips or invoices, plus a contract or service agreement. Freelancers must show consistent income over the period - not a single large payment.
2. **Health insurance:** Must be comprehensive and valid in Spain. Travel insurance is not sufficient. Providers like Cigna, Adeslas or Sanitas are commonly accepted.
3. **Criminal record certificate:** Must be apostilled. Nigeria is a signatory to the Hague Convention, so apostille is available through the Federal Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
4. **Consulate processing times:** The Spanish consulate in Lagos processes applications. Processing times can vary - apply well in advance of your intended travel date.
