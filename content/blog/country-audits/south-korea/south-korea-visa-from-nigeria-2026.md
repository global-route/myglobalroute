South Korea offers two evidence-gated pathways for Nigerian applicants: the student visa (D-2) and the work visa for professionals (E-series). The KGSP scholarship is the most viable route to study in Korea without self-funding.

## Quick summary

- Student visa (D-2): USD 9,000 in funds, admission offer required
- KGSP scholarship: covers tuition + KRW 900,000/month + return airfare
- Work visa (E-7): job offer required, relevant qualification required
- Processing time: 4-8 weeks at the Korean embassy

### KGSP Scholarship

The Korean Government Scholarship Program (KGSP) is South Korea's government-funded scholarship for international students.

**What it covers:**
- Full tuition waiver
- Monthly living allowance: KRW 900,000 (~USD 680) for graduate students; KRW 800,000 for undergraduates
- Return economy airfare
- Korean language training (1 year before your programme)
- Settlement allowance on arrival

**How to apply from Nigeria:**
1. Applications open at the Korean Embassy in Abuja, typically February-March each year.
2. Submit: application form, academic transcripts, personal statement, two recommendation letters, language test results.
3. Embassy screening and interview in Abuja.
4. Successful candidates are matched to Korean universities.

### Application process (self-funded)

1. Apply to a Korean university and receive an admission letter.
2. Apply at the Korean Embassy in Abuja: admission letter, proof of USD 9,000 in funds, passport, passport photos, health certificate.
3. Processing: 4-8 weeks.

## Pathway 2 — Work Visa (E-7: Specially Designated Activities)

**Registry ID:** `KR-work` | **Status:** Publishable

The E-7 visa covers professionals in fields designated by the Korean government, including IT, engineering, finance, and education.

### Requirements

| Requirement | Value | Source | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job offer | Required | immigration.go.kr | 2026-09-28 |
| Relevant qualification | Required | immigration.go.kr | 2026-09-28 |

**Source:** [immigration.go.kr](https://www.immigration.go.kr/immigration_eng/index.do) — verified 2026-09-28

### Application process from Nigeria

1. Secure a job offer from a Korean employer.
2. The employer files a visa issuance certificate with the Korean Immigration Service.
3. Apply at the Korean Embassy in Abuja with the visa issuance certificate, passport, and supporting documents.
4. Processing: 4-8 weeks.

### Salary

No fixed minimum is published for the E-7 visa. Salary must be competitive with Korean nationals in the same role. Entry-level IT roles in Seoul: KRW 30,000,000-40,000,000/year (~USD 22,000-30,000).

---

## Common rejection reasons

- Funds below USD 9,000 for student visa
- Admission letter from an institution not recognised by the Korean Ministry of Education
- Qualification not relevant to the job offer for E-7 visa
- Incomplete health certificate
