South Africa is the largest economy in Africa and a major destination for skilled African professionals. It has two primary work visa routes: the Critical Skills Visa (no job offer required) and the General Work Visa (employer-sponsored). Here is what the Department of Home Affairs actually requires.

## Critical Skills Visa - no job offer required

The Critical Skills Visa is the most accessible South African work visa for qualified African professionals. You apply on the basis of your qualifications and experience in a listed critical skill.

**Requirements:**
- Qualification in a field on the Critical Skills List
- Evaluation of your qualification by the relevant South African professional body
- Proof of work experience in the critical skill
- Valid passport, police clearance, medical certificate, radiological report

**The professional body evaluation:** Before applying, you must have your qualification evaluated by the relevant South African professional body. For example:
- IT professionals: IITPSA (Institute of Information Technology Professionals South Africa)
- Engineers: ECSA (Engineering Council of South Africa)
- Healthcare: HPCSA (Health Professions Council of South Africa)
- Accountants: SAICA or SAIPA

**Processing time:** 4-8 weeks at the South African High Commission in Abuja.

**Validity:** 5 years, renewable.

**Path to permanent residence:** After 5 years of continuous residence in South Africa on a Critical Skills Visa, you can apply for permanent residence.

**Primary source:** `https://www.dha.gov.za/`

## General Work Visa - employer-sponsored

The General Work Visa requires employer sponsorship and proof that no South African was available for the role.

**Requirements:**
- Confirmed job offer from a South African employer
- Labour market test: the employer must advertise the position and demonstrate that no suitable South African candidate was available
- Evaluation of your qualification by the relevant professional body
- Valid passport, police clearance, medical certificate

**Processing time:** Longer than the Critical Skills Visa due to the labour market test requirement. Allow 3-6 months.

**Validity:** Up to 3 years, tied to the specific employer.

## The Critical Skills List - key occupations

The following occupations are on the current Critical Skills List (verify the full list at dha.gov.za):

**Technology:** Software developers, data scientists, cybersecurity specialists, network engineers, AI/ML engineers

**Engineering:** Civil, electrical, mechanical, chemical, mining, petroleum engineers

**Healthcare:** Medical doctors (specialists), nurses (professional), pharmacists, physiotherapists, occupational therapists

**Finance:** Chartered accountants, actuaries, financial analysts

**Other:** Architects, urban planners, geologists, environmental scientists
