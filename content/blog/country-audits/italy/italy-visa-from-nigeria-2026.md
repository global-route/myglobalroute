# Italy Visa from Nigeria: Study, Work and DSU Scholarship Guide 2026

## Quick Summary

- **Study visa minimum funds:** Approximately €6,167/year living costs (self-funded)
- **DSU scholarship:** Covers tuition, housing and stipend (~€6,000–€7,000/year) for eligible low-income students
- **Work visa:** Requires Nulla Osta under the annual Decreto Flussi quota - quota opens on specific dates
- **Official source:** [Italian Ministry of Foreign Affairs - Visto per Italia](https://vistoperitalia.esteri.it/)

**Evidence note:** Approval rates by nationality are not published by the Italian MFA. This guide covers documented requirements only.

---

## Study Visa (Higher-education)

### Requirements

- **Admission offer:** Enrolment or pre-enrolment at a recognised Italian university via the [Universitaly portal](https://www.universitaly.it/)
- **Minimum living funds (self-funded):** Approximately €6,167/year (€514/month) - based on the Italian social allowance reference. Exact threshold varies by consulate. - [source: vistoperitalia.esteri.it](https://vistoperitalia.esteri.it/)
- **Tuition:** Varies by university and income bracket - public universities typically €0–€3,000/year for low-income students; higher for others
- **Health insurance:** Required for the duration of stay

### The Universitaly pre-enrolment process

For Nigerian applicants, the standard route is:
1. Register on [universitaly.it](https://www.universitaly.it/) and submit a pre-enrolment application
2. The Italian university confirms your application on the portal
3. You take the pre-enrolment confirmation to the Italian embassy in Lagos or Abuja
4. Apply for the study visa with the confirmed pre-enrolment

Pre-enrolment windows typically open in January–March for the following academic year. Apply early - places are limited.

## DSU Scholarship (Diritto allo Studio Universitario)

The DSU scholarship is Italy's means-tested student support system. For international students, eligibility is assessed using the ISEE/ISEEU calculation based on family income and assets in Nigeria.

### What it covers (if awarded)

- Tuition fees: Waived or significantly reduced
- University accommodation: Free dormitory place (where available)
- Living stipend: Approximately €6,000–€7,000/year (varies by university and region)
- Meal vouchers: Free or subsidised meals at university canteens

### Eligibility

- Enrolled at an Italian university
- Family income and assets below the ISEEU threshold (set annually by each university)
- Academic performance requirements apply for renewal

### Key requirement for Nigerian applicants

Family income documents (tax returns, property valuations, bank statements) must be:
1. Translated into Italian by a certified translator
2. Legalised at the Italian embassy in Lagos or Abuja (apostille or embassy attestation)
3. Submitted to the Italian consulate as part of the ISEEU calculation

This process takes time - start at least 6 months before your intended application date.

## Work Visa (Decreto Flussi)

Italy's work visa for non-EU nationals operates under an annual quota system called the Decreto Flussi.

### Requirements

- **Nulla Osta:** Work authorisation issued by the Italian Sportello Unico per l'Immigrazione - required before applying for a work visa
- **Job offer:** A confirmed offer from an Italian employer - the employer applies for the Nulla Osta on your behalf
- **Quota:** The Decreto Flussi sets annual limits on non-EU work permits. Quota availability is limited and the application window opens on specific dates (typically announced in late autumn for the following year)

### Important note on the quota

The Decreto Flussi is oversubscribed. Applications are processed on a first-come, first-served basis and quotas fill within hours of opening. Monitor the official announcement from the Italian Ministry of Labour.
