Czechia is an EU member state with affordable living costs and a growing international student community. It has two publishable pathways for Nigerian applicants: a long-term study residence permit and an Employee Card work permit.

## Long-term study residence permit

**Requirements:** Acceptance letter from an accredited Czech higher education institution, valid passport, proof of sufficient financial means, health insurance.

**Tuition fees:** Czech-taught programmes are free for all students. English-taught programmes for non-EU students cost EUR 2,000-8,000/year. Living costs in Prague are approximately EUR 600-800/month, lower in other cities.

**Processing time:** 2-3 months via the Ministry of the Interior.

**Path to PR:** After 5 years of continuous legal residence in Czechia.

Primary source: `https://mv.gov.cz/mvcren/article/third-country-nationals.aspx`

## Employee Card (work permit)

**Requirements:** Confirmed job offer from a Czech employer for a position registered in the central vacancy register for Employee Card holders. The employer applies on your behalf.

**Processing time:** 2-3 months.

Primary source: `https://mv.gov.cz/mvcren/article/employee-card.aspx`

## Evidence note

All figures sourced from the Ministry of the Interior of the Czech Republic at `https://mv.gov.cz/mvcren/`, retrieved October 2026. Verify current requirements before applying.
