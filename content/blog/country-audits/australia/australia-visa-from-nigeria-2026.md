# Australia Visa from Nigeria: The 2026 Reality Check

## Evidence status

Approval rates by nationality are not published by the Department of Home Affairs and are not shown here. Processing times change frequently — the Department publishes current medians at [immi.homeaffairs.gov.au](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au). All requirements below are sourced from primary government sources with effective dates.

---

## What is available

Four publishable pathways in the MyPathway2Global registry:

| Pathway | Type | Visa subclass |
|---|---|---|
| Student visa | Study | Subclass 500 |
| Skilled Independent | Work | Subclass 189 |
| Skilled Nominated | Work | Subclass 190 |
| Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) | Work | Subclass 491 |

---

## Student visa — subclass 500

**Official source:** [immi.homeaffairs.gov.au — Student 500](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500)

### Core requirements

**Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE):** A CoE from a registered CRICOS provider is required before applying. Admission alone does not satisfy this — the institution must issue the CoE.

**Genuine Student requirement:** The applicant must demonstrate the primary purpose of the stay is study and that they intend to comply with visa conditions. Assessed holistically — no single document guarantees satisfaction.

**Indicative living funds (2026):**

| Person | AUD per year |
|---|---|
| Primary applicant | 29,710 |
| Accompanying spouse/partner | 10,394 |
| Each school-age child | 4,449 |

Source: effective 2026-09-13. These are indicative amounts — the decision-maker assesses genuine access to funds, not just nominal holdings. Tuition fees are separate and vary by institution and course.

## Skilled Independent visa — subclass 189

**Official source:** [immi.homeaffairs.gov.au — Skilled Independent 189](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skilled-independent-189)

A points-tested permanent residence visa. No employer sponsorship required, but the nominated occupation must be on the Medium and Long-term Strategic Skills List (MLTSSL).

### Requirements

| Requirement | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Minimum points to submit EOI | 65 | Reaching 65 does not guarantee an invitation — cut-offs are typically higher |
| Maximum age at invitation | Under 45 years | |
| Skills assessment | Required | From the relevant Australian assessing authority |
| English proficiency | Competent English | IELTS 6.0 in each band or equivalent |

Source: effective 2026-10-18.

### How invitations work

Submitting an Expression of Interest (EOI) at 65 points places you in the SkillSelect pool. Home Affairs issues invitations periodically, ranked by score. Actual cut-offs in recent rounds have been well above 65 — check the Department's published invitation round data before investing in a skills assessment.

## Skilled Nominated visa — subclass 190

**Official source:** [immi.homeaffairs.gov.au — Skilled Nominated 190](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skilled-nominated-190)

Same points test as subclass 189, but requires nomination by an Australian state or territory government. Nomination adds 5 points. Each state/territory sets its own occupation list and criteria — check the relevant state authority directly.

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum points for EOI | 65 |
| State/territory nomination | Required |
| Maximum age at invitation | Under 45 years |
| Skills assessment | Required |

---

## Skilled Work Regional (Provisional) visa — subclass 491

**Official source:** [immi.homeaffairs.gov.au — Skilled Work Regional 491](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skilled-work-regional-provisional-491)

A provisional visa. Permanent residency requires 3 years of regional work and meeting income thresholds before applying for subclass 191. Nomination adds 15 points (higher than subclass 190).

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum points for EOI | 65 |
| State/territory or eligible family nomination | Required |
| Maximum age at invitation | Under 45 years |
| Skills assessment | Required |
| Regional requirement | Must live and work in a designated regional area for the visa duration |

---

## Key planning considerations

**Skills assessment lead time:** Most assessing authorities take 8–16 weeks. This must be completed before submitting an EOI.

**English test:** IELTS, PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT and Cambridge C1 Advanced are accepted. Higher scores earn more points — IELTS 8.0 in each band earns the maximum English points allocation.

**Occupation eligibility:** Not all occupations are on the MLTSSL. Check [eligible skilled occupations](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/working-in-australia/skillselect/eligible-skilled-occupations) before investing in a skills assessment.

**AUD funds:** The student living funds threshold (AUD 29,710/year) is a significant sum. Confirm the current NGN equivalent with your bank — exchange rates move.

---

## What this audit does not cover

Visitor visas, Working Holiday (subclass 417/462), partner and family visas, and employer-sponsored routes (subclass 482, 186) are not currently in the evidence registry.
