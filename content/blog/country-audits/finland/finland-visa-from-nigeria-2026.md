Finland has two publishable pathways for Nigerian applicants: a study residence permit and a work residence permit (TTOL). Both are issued by the Finnish Immigration Service (Migri).

## Study permit

**Minimum funds:** EUR 800/month. For studies of at least one year, EUR 9,600 must be available at the time of application. Tuition fees are additional - non-EU students pay EUR 6,000 to EUR 18,000 per year depending on institution and programme.

**Requirements:** Admission letter from a Finnish higher education institution, proof of funds, valid passport, health insurance where not covered by the institution.

**Work rights while studying:** Up to 25 hours per week during term time, full time during holidays, without a separate work permit.

**Processing time:** 1-3 months via the Enter Finland online portal.

**Path to PR:** After 4 years of continuous legal residence, you can apply for a permanent residence permit.

Primary source: `https://migri.fi/residence-permit-application-for-studies`

## Work permit (TTOL)

**Requirements:** Binding employment contract from a Finnish employer. The employer must confirm terms meet the collective agreement for the sector. No minimum salary threshold is published as a universal figure - the collective agreement for your sector determines the applicable minimum.

**Processing time:** 2-4 months. The employer submits part of the application.

Primary source: `https://migri.fi/en/residence-permit-for-an-employed-person`

## Evidence note

All figures sourced from Migri at `https://migri.fi/en/home`, retrieved October 2026. Verify current requirements before applying.
