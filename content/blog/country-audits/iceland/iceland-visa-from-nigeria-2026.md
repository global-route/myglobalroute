Iceland is a Nordic island nation with a small population and documented labour shortages in several sectors. It has two publishable pathways for Nigerian applicants: a study residence permit and a work-based residence permit.

## Study residence permit

**Requirements:** Acceptance at a recognised Icelandic educational institution, valid passport, proof of sufficient financial means, health insurance.

**Tuition fees:** The University of Iceland charges approximately ISK 75,000 per semester (approximately EUR 500) for most programmes - low by international standards. Other institutions vary.

**Cost of living:** Iceland is expensive. Budget approximately EUR 1,500-2,000/month in Reykjavik for accommodation, food and transport.

**Processing time:** 1-3 months via the Directorate of Immigration.

Primary source: `https://island.is/en/o/directorate-of-immigration`

## Work-based residence permit

**Requirements:** Confirmed job offer from an Icelandic employer. The employer must satisfy the labour-market test or demonstrate the position is in a shortage occupation. Iceland has documented shortages in healthcare, construction, tourism and IT.

**Processing time:** 2-3 months.

**Path to PR:** After 4 years of continuous legal residence in Iceland.

Primary source: `https://island.is/en/o/directorate-of-immigration`
