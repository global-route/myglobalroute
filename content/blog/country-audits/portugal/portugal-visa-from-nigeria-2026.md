# Portugal Visa from Nigeria: Study, Digital Nomad and D7 Guide 2026

## Quick Summary

- **Study visa:** Admission offer + demonstrable funds required
- **Digital nomad (D8):** Proof of remote employment or service contract with entity outside Portugal
- **D7 passive income:** Demonstrable stable passive or remote income (commonly cited minimum ~€820/month — not a fixed published threshold)
- **Official source:** [AIMA — Portuguese Immigration Authority](https://aima.gov.pt/)

**Evidence note:** Approval rates by nationality are not published by AIMA. This guide covers documented requirements only.

---

## Study Residence Visa

### Requirements

- **Admission offer:** Proof of enrolment or admission to an eligible higher-education institution in Portugal — required under Article 91 — [source: AIMA](https://aima.gov.pt/pt/estudar/autorizacao-de-residencia-emitida-a-estudantes-do-ensino-superior-art-o-91)
- **Minimum funds:** Demonstrable living funds for the duration of study — no single fixed threshold is published; the consulate assesses sufficiency
- **Health insurance:** Required
- **Accommodation proof:** Rental contract or university accommodation confirmation

### Process

1. Apply for admission to a Portuguese university
2. Obtain acceptance letter
3. Apply for a long-stay study visa (Visto D) at the Portuguese consulate in Lagos or Abuja
4. After arrival, apply for a residence permit at AIMA within 4 months

Portugal's AIMA has faced significant processing backlogs — factor this into your timeline.

## Remote Work / Digital Nomad Route (D8)

Portugal's D8 visa is for non-EU nationals who work remotely for employers or clients outside Portugal.

### Requirements

- **Proof of remote employment or service contract:** A foreign employment contract or service relationship with an entity or clients outside Portugal — [source: AIMA](https://aima.gov.pt/pt/trabalhar/autorizacao-de-residencia-para-o-exercicio-de-atividade-profissional-prestada-de-forma-remota-com-visto-de-residencia-para-o-exe)
- **Income:** Sufficient to support yourself in Portugal — the commonly cited minimum is approximately €3,200/month (4× the Portuguese minimum wage), but this is not a fixed published threshold
- **Health insurance:** Required
- **Accommodation proof:** Required

### What D8 gives you

- Legal residence in Portugal
- Schengen area access during residence
- Path to permanent residency after 5 years of legal residence
- EU citizenship eligibility after 5 years (subject to Portuguese language test and other requirements)

---

## Passive Income Route (D7)

The D7 visa is for non-EU nationals with stable passive or remote income who wish to reside in Portugal.

### Requirements

- **Demonstrable income:** Stable passive or remote income — acceptable sources include pensions, rental income, dividends, remote employment, freelance contracts
- **Minimum income:** No fixed published threshold. The Portuguese minimum wage (~€820/month in 2026) is widely cited as a reference, but consulates assess sufficiency holistically. Higher income strengthens the application.
- **Health insurance:** Required
- **Accommodation proof:** Required

### Important note

The D7 income threshold is not published as a fixed figure by AIMA. The €820/month reference is a community estimate based on the minimum wage. Consulates in different countries apply different standards. Confirm the current requirement with the Portuguese consulate in Lagos or Abuja before applying.

---

## Key considerations for Nigerian applicants

- **AIMA processing backlogs:** Portugal's immigration authority has faced multi-month delays for residence permit appointments. Factor 6–12 months into your post-arrival timeline.
- **NHR tax regime:** Portugal's Non-Habitual Resident tax regime offers tax advantages for new residents — consult a Portuguese tax adviser.
- **Language:** Portuguese is required for citizenship but not for the initial visa or residence permit.
