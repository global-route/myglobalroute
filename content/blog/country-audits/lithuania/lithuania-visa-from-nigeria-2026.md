Lithuania is an EU member state with affordable tuition fees and a growing international student community. It has two publishable pathways for Nigerian applicants: a study temporary residence permit and a work temporary residence permit.

## Study temporary residence permit

**Requirements:** Acceptance letter from a recognised Lithuanian higher education institution, valid passport, proof of sufficient financial means, health insurance.

**Tuition fees:** EUR 1,500 to EUR 5,000/year for non-EU students depending on programme and institution. Living costs in Vilnius are approximately EUR 500-700/month.

**Work rights:** Up to 20 hours per week during term time without a separate work permit.

**Processing time:** 1-2 months via the Migration Department online portal.

**Path to PR:** After 5 years of continuous legal residence in Lithuania.

Primary source: `https://migracija.lrv.lt/en/services-for-foreigners/temporary-residence-permit/for-studies/`

## Work temporary residence permit

**Requirements:** Valid employment contract from a Lithuanian employer. The employer must obtain a work permit on your behalf before you apply for the residence permit.

**Processing time:** 2-3 months.

Primary source: `https://migracija.lrv.lt/en/services-for-foreigners/temporary-residence-permit/for-work/`

## Evidence note

All figures sourced from the Migration Department of Lithuania at `https://migracija.lrv.lt/en/`, retrieved October 2026. Verify current requirements before applying.
