Ghana is West Africa's most stable democracy and a growing destination for African professionals and investors. Nigerian nationals can enter without a visa. The Right of Abode provides a unique long-term residence option for the diaspora. Here is what official sources confirm.

## Entry - visa-free for Nigerians

Nigerian nationals can enter Ghana without a visa under the ECOWAS free movement protocol. You can stay for up to 90 days.

**This does not give you the right to work.** Employment in Ghana requires a work permit.

## Right of Abode - for persons of African descent

Ghana's Right of Abode is one of the most significant immigration provisions for the African diaspora. It provides indefinite residence rights in Ghana for persons of African descent.

**Who qualifies:** Any person of African descent - including Nigerians, Ghanaian diaspora with foreign passports, and other African nationals.

**What it provides:**
- Indefinite residence in Ghana without a visa or residence permit
- No annual renewal required
- Freedom to travel in and out of Ghana

**What it does not provide:**
- The right to work for a Ghanaian employer (requires a separate work permit)
- Ghanaian citizenship (separate application process)
- Voting rights

**How to apply:** Application to the Ghana Immigration Service. Required documents include proof of African descent, valid passport, police clearance, and application fee.

**Primary source:** `https://www.gis.gov.gh/`

## Work permit - for employed professionals

Foreign nationals working for a Ghanaian employer require a work permit.

**Requirements:**
- Confirmed job offer from a registered Ghanaian employer
- The employer must demonstrate that no Ghanaian national was available for the role
- Relevant qualifications and work experience
- Valid passport, police clearance

**Processing time:** 4-8 weeks.

## GIPC investor permit - for investors and business owners

The Ghana Investment Promotion Centre (GIPC) administers investment-related permits.

**Minimum investment thresholds:**

| Business type | Minimum investment |
|---|---|
| Joint venture with Ghanaian partner | USD 200,000 |
| Wholly foreign-owned enterprise | USD 500,000 |
| Trading company | USD 1,000,000 |

**What the GIPC permit provides:** Residence and work rights in Ghana. Valid for the duration of the investment.

**Primary source:** `https://www.gipc.gov.gh/`

## Why Ghana for African professionals

**Political stability:** Ghana has had peaceful democratic transitions since 1992 - one of the most stable political environments in West Africa.

**English language:** Ghana is English-speaking, making it accessible for Nigerian professionals without a language barrier.

**Growing economy:** Ghana's economy has grown significantly, with strong sectors in finance, technology, oil and gas, and agriculture.

**AfCFTA headquarters:** Ghana hosts the AfCFTA Secretariat operational headquarters in Accra, creating new business and professional opportunities.

**Cost of living:** Accra is more expensive than most West African cities but significantly cheaper than European or Gulf cities.
