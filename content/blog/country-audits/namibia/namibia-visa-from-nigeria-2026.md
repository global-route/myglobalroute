Namibia is one of Africa's most politically stable countries. It borders South Africa, Botswana, Zambia and Angola, and has a well-developed infrastructure by regional standards. For African remote workers who want a Southern African base, Namibia is the most accessible option with a formal digital nomad visa route.

## Entry - visa required for Nigerians

Nigerian nationals require a visa to enter Namibia. Apply at the Namibia High Commission in Abuja before travel, or check current on-arrival availability at Hosea Kutako International Airport.

## Digital Nomad Visa

Namibia's Digital Nomad Visa is issued by the Ministry of Home Affairs, Immigration, Safety and Security (MHAISS) for an initial period of 6 months, extendable.

**Requirements:**
- Valid passport
- Proof of remote employment or freelance income sourced outside Namibia
- Proof of accommodation in Namibia
- Health insurance
- Clean criminal record

**Income threshold:** Approximately USD 2,000/month. Not a fixed published figure.

**Application fee:** Approximately NAD 1,500 (approximately USD 80).

**What it allows:** Living and working remotely in Namibia. You cannot work for a Namibia-based employer on this visa.

**Primary source:** `https://mhaiss.gov.na/`

## Work permit (for employed professionals)

Foreign nationals employed by a Namibian company require a work permit from MHAISS.

**Requirements:**
- Confirmed job offer from a registered Namibian employer
- Relevant qualifications and work experience
- Valid passport

**Processing time:** 2-3 months.

## Windhoek as a base

Windhoek is the practical base for most remote workers in Namibia.

**Connectivity:** Reliable 4G and fibre broadband in Windhoek. Co-working spaces available. Connectivity drops significantly outside Windhoek.

**Banking:** Namibia uses the Namibian Dollar (NAD), pegged to the South African Rand. South African Rand is also accepted. Banking infrastructure is well-developed - FNB Namibia, Standard Bank Namibia, and Bank Windhoek are the main options.

**Flights:** Windhoek Hosea Kutako International Airport has direct connections to Johannesburg, Frankfurt, Addis Ababa and several regional hubs. Lagos to Windhoek typically requires a connection through Johannesburg or Addis Ababa.

## Time zone

UTC+2 (UTC+1 in winter). Good overlap with European and East African business hours.

## Evidence note

All figures sourced from the Ministry of Home Affairs, Immigration, Safety and Security at `https://mhaiss.gov.na/`, retrieved October 2026. The USD 2,000/month income threshold is the commonly cited figure and is not a fixed published requirement. Verify current requirements before applying.
