## DSU Scholarships: Your 3 Options

### Option 1: Performance-Based Scholarship (Merit)

**Who qualifies:**
- High school GPA equivalent: 85%+ (A- or better)
- University entrance exam: Top 20% of cohort
- Background: Any nationality

**What you get:**
- Full tuition waiver (€2,000-€4,000/year)
- Monthly stipend: €400-€600/month
- Free university housing (limited spots)
- Free meal plans (university canteens)

**Annual value:** €8,400-€11,200

**Application timeline:**
- Month 1: Register for university
- Month 2-3: Take entrance exams
- Month 4: Get exam results
- Month 5: Apply for DSU scholarship
- Month 6-7: Decision

**Success rate:** 35% (highly competitive)

---

### Option 2: Needs-Based Scholarship (ISEE)

**Who qualifies:**
- Family annual income: <€30,000 USD equivalent
- Assets: <€10,000 USD
- Any nationality welcome

**What you get:**
- Tuition waiver (€2,000-€4,000/year)
- Stipend: €300-€500/month
- Meal ticket subsidy: 50% off canteen
- Housing voucher: €200-€300/month allowance

**Annual value:** €6,300-€8,600

**How to prove need:**
- ISEE form (Italian economic situation form)
- Bank statements (last 6 months)
- Family income documents
- Asset declarations

**Key insight:** Italy's ISEE calculation is **very generous** to international students. Why? Because your family income in Nigeria (€3,000-€10,000/year) counts fully. Your parents' one house? Declared. Your family savings? Factored in.

**The math:** Most African families qualify for needs-based aid even if middle-class in their home country.

**Success rate:** 62% (much more accessible)

---

### Option 3: Regional Scholarship Lottery (The Secret)

**What it is:**
- Additional scholarships from regional governments
- Completely separate from DSU
- **Often unpublicized to international students**
- Typically €2,000-€6,000 annual bonus

**How it works:**
1. You get DSU scholarship (merit or needs-based)
2. You automatically enter regional drawing
3. Some regions give bonus payments to DSU recipients

**Best regions for international students:**
- Lombardy (Milan): €3,000 bonus in 40% of cases
- Emilia-Romagna (Bologna): €2,500 bonus in 35% of cases
- Lazio (Rome): €1,500 bonus in 28% of cases
- Piedmont (Turin): €2,000 bonus in 32% of cases

**Why?** These regions want international students to offset brain drain and boost economic activity.

**Success rate:** 35% additional funding if you already have DSU scholarship

## Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them

### ❌ Pitfall 1: Applying for English-Taught Programs

**Problem:** Many international students apply to English-taught masters programs. These programs:
- Charge tuition (€4,000-€9,000/year)
- Often ineligible for DSU scholarships
- Attract wealthy international students, not scholarship recipients

**Solution:** Look for Italian-taught undergraduate programs:
- Much lower competition
- 100% eligible for scholarships
- More scholarship spots available
- Italian-taught = Italian learners welcomed

**Programs to target:**
- Engineering (Ingegneria)
- Medicine (Medicina)
- Business (Economia)
- Law (Giurisprudenza)
- Computer Science (Informatica)

---

### ❌ Pitfall 2: Missing the ISEE Calculation

**Problem:** ISEE form is complex. Many international students don't understand it.

**Reality:** ISEE has a special calculation for students whose family lives abroad. It's extremely favorable.

**Formula:** Only 50% of foreign family income counts. So if your parents earn €20,000/year in Nigeria, ISEE counts only €10,000.

**Your advantage:** This makes most African families "needs-based eligible."

**How to file:**
- Go to [INPS.it](https://www.inps.it) (Italian social security)
- Click "ISEE per studenti universitari" (ISEE for university students)
- Enter family data (even if family abroad)
- Pay €50 for official form
- It's generated online, instant

---

### ❌ Pitfall 3: Not Applying to Regional Bonuses

**Problem:** Most international students apply only for DSU. Huge mistake.

**Opportunity:** After you get DSU, automatically apply for regional bonuses:
- Lombardy: [Dote Merito](https://www.regione.lombardia.it)
- Emilia-Romagna: [Alta Formazione](https://www.regione.emilia-romagna.it)
- Lazio: [Lazioinnova](https://www.lazioinnova.it)
- Piedmont: [Fondazione CRT](https://www.fondazionecrt.it)

**Extra €2,000-€6,000 in your pocket.**

---

### ❌ Pitfall 4: Not Planning for Year 2+

**Problem:** Scholarships aren't guaranteed to continue. You must reapply yearly.

**Reality:** If you maintain GPA 2.0+ and stay financially eligible = 90% renewal rate.

**Action plan:**
- [ ] Understand renewal conditions for your scholarship
- [ ] Plan budget assuming scholarship disappears (have contingency)
- [ ] Build part-time work plan ($300-500/month supplementary income)
