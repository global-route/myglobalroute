## Step-by-Step to UK Work Visa

### Phase 1: Preparation (3 months before)

**Step 1: Credentials & Certifications**
- [ ] Get professional qualifications recognized in UK
  - Doctor: Need GMC registration (6-12 month process)
  - Nurse: Need NMC registration (8 weeks)
  - Engineer: Get CEng/IEng (6-12 months)
  - Accountant: Get ACA/ACCA (varies)
- [ ] Update LinkedIn profile (UK-focused)
- [ ] Get 2-3 professional references ready

**Step 2: Job Search**
- [ ] Research UK companies in your field
- [ ] Network on LinkedIn (connect with UK hiring managers)
- [ ] Apply to sponsored employer list (pre-approved companies)
- [ ] Work with recruitment agencies (they handle visa sponsorship)

**Fastest route:** Use recruitment agency specializing in your industry. They have relationships with sponsor employers.

---

### Phase 2: Job Offer (1-3 months)

**Step 1: Secure Job Offer**
- Company must be licensed visa sponsor (check Home Office list)
- Salary must meet £29,000 minimum (or occupation-specific threshold)
- Contract confirms start date (usually 4-8 weeks out)

**Step 2: Immigration Sponsorship**
- Employer assigns "Certificate of Sponsorship" (CoS)
- This is your permission to apply for visa
- Takes 1-2 weeks after job offer

**Step 3: Gather Documents**
- [ ] Job contract (formal offer letter)
- [ ] Certificate of Sponsorship number
- [ ] Passport (6+ months validity)
- [ ] Birth certificate (original, certified)
- [ ] Education credentials (transcripts, degrees, certifications)
- [ ] Professional references (2-3)
- [ ] Medical exam (if required)
- [ ] Police clearance (from home country)
- [ ] Proof of UK work experience (if applicable)

---

### Phase 3: Visa Application (1-2 months)

**Application process:**
1. **Online application** at gov.uk (UK Home Office portal)
2. **Pay fees:**
   - Application: £719
   - Health surcharge: £284/year (charged for each year of visa)
   - 3-year visa = £719 + (£284 × 3) = $1,571 total
3. **Biometric appointment** (fingerprints, photo, iris scan)
   - Scheduled at UK visa application center
   - Usually 1-2 weeks after online submission
4. **Wait for decision** (8 weeks standard, 4 weeks priority)

---

### Phase 4: Arrival (2 weeks)

**Upon UK arrival:**
- [ ] Collect Biometric Residence Permit (BRP) from Post Office
- [ ] Register with NHS (healthcare)
- [ ] Open UK bank account
- [ ] Get National Insurance number
- [ ] Enroll in employer's benefits (pension, health insurance)

## The Honest Pros & Cons

### ✅ Advantages
1. **Salary is high enough** (£29,000 in most developed countries is comfortable)
2. **NHS healthcare** (free public healthcare, no insurance needed)
3. **Pension contributions** (employer matches, good retirement savings)
4. **Stability** (permanent job, not contract-based like many African roles)
5. **Pathway to settlement** (work visa leads to residency after 5+ years)
6. **EU mobility** (can work across Europe on weekends, holidays)
7. **Financial services** (can get UK mortgage, build credit history)

### ❌ Disadvantages
1. **Weather** (dark 9 months/year, mental health challenge)
2. **Distance from family** (flights home expensive €400-800)
3. **Cost of living** (London especially expensive)
4. **Visa tied to employer** (can't leave job easily without starting visa process over)
5. **Credential recognition process** (takes 6-12 months for regulated professions)
6. **Income tax** (20% tax on £29,000+ salary, vs. 0% in some African countries)
7. **Relocation costs** (flights, shipping, new setup = £2,000-4,000)

## Success Tips

### ✅ Do This

1. **Use recruitment agencies** (they know sponsor employers)
2. **Apply to industries with visa shortages** (healthcare, tech, finance)
3. **Get UK credentials early** (while still in home country if possible)
4. **Be prepared to relocate to regional cities** (easier visa approvals, lower cost)
5. **Save 6 months living expenses** (buffer for job search transition)
6. **Connect on LinkedIn** with UK hiring managers (personal connections help)

### ❌ Avoid This

1. **Expecting to keep job flexibility** (visa tied to employer, switching jobs = new visa process)
2. **Aiming for London-only jobs** (saturated market, higher salary requirement)
3. **Waiting for ideal job** (accept reasonable offer, transition to better job after 1-2 years)
4. **Not preparing credentials early** (UK credential recognition takes time)
5. **Underestimating moving costs** (flights, shipping, initial setup = £3,000-5,000)

### Year 5+: Long-Term Residency

- Path to UK citizenship (1+ year as ILR holder)
