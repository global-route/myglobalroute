Budget-Based Routes

Under $5,000

Best Option: Italy DSU or Hungary Scholarship

Your liquid cash covers application fees and initial setup. The scholarship covers everything else.

$5,000 - $10,000

Best Option: Germany Blocked Account

Covers the blocked account requirement. Fund living costs through part-time work.

$10,000 - $15,000

Best Option: Canada Master's (Atlantic Canada)

Covers partial tuition + blocked account. Consider scholarships for the rest.

$15,000+

Options: All countries open

You have significant flexibility. Focus on best academic fit and career goals.

Step-by-Step: Germany Route

  1. Research tuition-free universities
  2. Apply and get admission letter
  3. Fund blocked account (€11,208)
  4. Write Letter of Motivation
  5. Apply for student visa
  6. Receive visa (2-4 months)
  7. Arrive and enroll

Step-by-Step: Italy DSU Route

  1. Choose Italian university (low fees)
  2. Apply for admission
  3. Get acceptance letter
  4. Gather family income documents
  5. Legalize at Italian embassy
  6. Apply for DSU scholarship
  7. Get scholarship approval
  8. Apply for student visa
  9. Arrive and begin studies

Pro Tips

  • Start 12+ months in advance
  • Document everything officially
  • Get letters of recommendation early
  • Prepare English language test
  • Budget for application fees ($300-500)
  • Have proof of funds (even if small)
