Migration Information Disclaimer

Global Route provides general migration information, research and planning tools. It is not a law firm, immigration authority, university admissions office, recruiter or visa agent.

No outcome guarantee

Eligibility, fees, processing times, admission decisions and immigration outcomes can change and depend on individual circumstances. No calculator score or route recommendation guarantees an outcome.

Evidence standard

Material policy claims should identify their authoritative source and review date. Where an authority does not publish a statistic, Global Route will not invent one.

Verify before acting

Always confirm current requirements with the relevant government, immigration authority, embassy, university or other authoritative institution before paying fees, submitting an application or travelling.
