How it works

How the calculator works

1. Financial assessment

Savings, income and expenses are used to calculate your liquid position and monthly savings potential.

2. Affordability matching

Your savings are compared against verified country cost records only — unverified costs are excluded.

3. Timeline projection

Monthly savings potential projects your 12-month readiness window for planning purposes.

4. Evidence-gated results

Results are sorted by verified cost. Countries with no verified cost data are not shown — not hidden as zero.
