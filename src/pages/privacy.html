Privacy

Global Route is designed to provide migration information without requiring passports, identity documents, bank statements or immigration case files.

What we collect

Route-finder and calculator inputs should be treated as transient product inputs unless an explicit account feature states otherwise.

Data minimisation

We do not need sensitive identity or immigration documents to provide general migration information.

Third parties

Analytics, advertising and other third-party services are documented and must be configured in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

Your choices

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the published contact channel. Do not send identity documents by email.
