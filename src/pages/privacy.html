Privacy

MyPathway2Global is designed to provide migration information without requiring passports, identity documents, bank statements or immigration case files.

What we collect

The current route-finder and calculator are planning tools and do not require passports, identity documents, bank statements or immigration case files. Treat the values you enter as private; do not enter details that are not needed to explore a route.

Analytics and advertising choices

Optional analytics events are gated on an explicit analytics choice. The consent controls let you independently choose analytics measurement and advertising storage; declining either option should not prevent access to migration information. The current measurement contract allowlists event names and coarse dimensions and excludes profile values such as income, savings, budget, nationality, email and identity details.

Use the persistent Privacy settings control to change or withdraw your choices. Where browser storage is available, choices are stored locally in your browser; if storage is unavailable, a choice may not persist between visits. A third-party provider may only be activated after the relevant configuration and consent gates are satisfied.

Newsletter and contact forms

No newsletter provider is currently active. The newsletter sign-up form is present for future use only; submitting it does not send your email address anywhere. When a provider is configured, this page will be updated with the provider name, data flow, retention period and deletion process before launch.

Data minimisation and retention

MyPathway2Global should collect only information needed for a stated purpose and retain it only for a defined period. Any future account, newsletter, lead or partner feature requires its own documented data flow, retention period, deletion process and access controls before launch.

Third parties

Analytics, advertising and other third-party services must be inventoried and configured in accordance with applicable consent requirements. No live advertising or partner lead collection should be assumed from the presence of configuration code alone.

Your choices and contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the published contact channel at hello@mypathway2global.org.uk. Do not send identity documents by email.
