Top Professional Visas

🇩🇪 Germany: Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card)

Points-based job seeker visa

  • 6+ points required (out of 10)
  • Foreign qualification: 4 points
  • Language (German A2): 1 point
  • Professional experience (2+ yrs): 2-3 points
  • Result: Work part-time while searching

🇬🇧 UK: Skilled Worker Visa

Employment-based pathway

  • Minimum salary threshold
  • Job offer from approved sponsor
  • Points-based assessment
  • Family can come
  • Path to indefinite leave to remain

🇨🇦 Canada: Express Entry
