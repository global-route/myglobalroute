The Professional Playbook

Evidence-gated work visa pathways for African professionals — requirements, salary thresholds and official sources.

Evidence note: All salary thresholds and requirements on this page are sourced from official government portals with effective dates. Requirements change — always confirm current thresholds with the official authority before applying. This page is a planning guide, not a legal or eligibility decision.

Employer-sponsored pathways

🇬🇧 UK — Skilled Worker visa

The UK Skilled Worker visa requires a confirmed job offer from an approved sponsor employer.

  • Job offer required: Yes — from a UK employer with a valid sponsor licence, with a Certificate of Sponsorship
  • Minimum salary: £41,700/year (baseline threshold, effective 2026-09-12 — source: UKVI). The applicable occupation going rate may be higher.
  • Maintenance funds: £1,270 available balance (held for 28 days) unless employer certifies maintenance
  • Path to settlement: Indefinite Leave to Remain after 5 years
  • Sectors in demand: Healthcare, technology, engineering, finance
View UK pathway →

🇮🇪 Ireland — Critical Skills Employment Permit

Ireland's Critical Skills permit targets professionals in shortage occupations.

  • Job offer required: Yes — from an Irish employer for an eligible occupation
  • Minimum salary: €38,000/year for most occupations; €30,000 for occupations on the Critical Skills list — source: Government of Ireland
  • Path to settlement: Stamp 4 (unrestricted work rights) after 2 years; permanent residency after 5 years
  • Sectors in demand: Technology, healthcare, financial services
View Ireland pathway →

🇫🇷 France — Talent Passport

France's Talent Passport covers several professional categories including EU Blue Card holders and company employees.

  • Job offer required: Yes — for the EU Blue Card subcategory
  • Minimum salary (EU Blue Card): €59,373 gross/year (effective 2025-08-21 — source: France-Visas). EU Blue Card requires 1.5× this reference salary.
  • Path to settlement: Long-stay residence; permanent residency after 5 years
  • Sectors in demand: Technology, research, finance, creative industries
View France pathway →

🇸🇪 Sweden — Standard work permit

Sweden's work permit requires a job offer meeting the collective agreement salary threshold.

  • Job offer required: Yes — from a Swedish employer
  • Minimum salary: SEK 34,470/month (effective 2026-06-16, based on 90% of median salary — source: Swedish Migration Agency)
  • Path to settlement: Permanent residence after 4 years of work permit
View Sweden pathway →

🇳🇴 Norway — Skilled worker permit

Norway requires a concrete full-time job offer from a Norwegian employer.

  • Job offer required: Yes — full-time (or at least 80%) from a Norwegian employer requiring skilled qualifications — source: UDI
  • Path to settlement: Permanent residence after 3 years of work permit
View Norway pathway →

Points-based and job-search pathways

🇩🇪 Germany — Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)

Germany's Opportunity Card allows qualified professionals to enter Germany to search for work without a prior job offer.

  • Minimum points: 6 points on the criteria catalogue (qualification, language, experience, age, connection to Germany) — source: Make it in Germany
  • Alternative route: Full recognition of a foreign qualification in Germany provides direct access without points
  • Minimum funds: €1,091 net/month via blocked account or declaration of commitment for the job-search period
  • Part-time work: Up to 20 hours/week permitted during the job search
  • Duration: Up to 1 year to find qualifying employment
View Germany pathway →

🇦🇹 Austria — Red-White-Red Card (Other Key Workers)

Austria's points-based Red-White-Red Card allows qualified professionals to work in Austria.

  • Minimum points: 55 points on the Other Key Workers criteria catalogue (education, experience, age, language, job offer) — source: migration.gv.at
  • Job offer required: Yes — from an Austrian employer; contributes points
  • Minimum salary: €2,685 gross/month (2026 reference — subject to annual adjustment)
  • Path to settlement: Red-White-Red Card Plus after 2 years; permanent residence after 5 years
View Austria pathway →

🇨🇦 Canada — Federal Skilled Worker Program (Express Entry)

Canada's Express Entry system is an invitation-based pathway to permanent residency for skilled workers.

  • Minimum selection factor points: 67 out of 100 (education, language, experience, age, arranged employment, adaptability) — source: IRCC
  • Minimum work experience: 1 year continuous full-time (or equivalent) in a NOC TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3 occupation in the past 10 years
  • Settlement funds (family size 1): CAD 14,690 (exempt if you have a valid Canadian job offer)
  • CRS score: Determines invitation order — recent 2026 draw cut-offs in the 480–520 range
  • Path to settlement: Direct permanent residency on approval
View Canada pathway →

Remote work and digital nomad pathways

For professionals working remotely for foreign employers, Portugal and Spain offer dedicated residence routes. See the Business Playbook for full details on PT-digital-nomad and ES-digital-nomad pathways.

High-demand sectors by destination

These are general patterns based on published shortage occupation lists and permit data — not guarantees of approval.

  • Technology: Ireland (Critical Skills list), Germany (Chancenkarte), France (Talent Passport), UK (Skilled Worker)
  • Healthcare: UK (Skilled Worker — NHS shortage occupations), Canada (Express Entry — healthcare NOCs), Norway, Sweden
  • Finance: Luxembourg (Salaried Worker), UK, Ireland, Germany
  • Engineering: Canada, Germany, Austria (RWR Card), Sweden
  • Trades and technicians: New Zealand (SMC Trades and Technician pathway), Canada, Australia (subclass 190/491)
