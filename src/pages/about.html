About Global Route

Our Mission

Empower African talent with radical transparency, actionable intelligence, and strategic pathways for global mobility.

The Problem

Migration information is fragmented, and requirements, costs and outcomes vary by pathway and applicant. Unsupported figures can create false confidence.

Our Approach

We prioritize official sources, pathway-specific requirements and transparent evidence status. We publish approval rates or cost figures only when the scope and source support the claim; otherwise we show that the information is not published, not verified or still under research.

Core Values

  • Radical Transparency
  • Evidence before certainty
  • Actionable Intelligence
  • Cultural Respect
  • Responsible and disclosed revenue

Global Route provides general information and planning tools, not a legal eligibility decision or a guarantee of visa, employment or admission outcomes. Always confirm current requirements with the responsible authority.
