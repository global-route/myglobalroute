About MyPathway2Global

Evidence-gated mobility intelligence for African students, professionals and the agencies and practitioners who serve them.

Mission

MyPathway2Global provides verified, primary-source migration intelligence for African students, professionals and entrepreneurs - and the agencies, practitioners and organisations that serve them. Every pathway record cites its official source, declares its evidence status, and is updated when policy changes. We do not publish what we cannot verify.

Vision

A continent where every African who wants to move - for work, study, investment or opportunity - has access to the same quality of verified information that was previously available only to those who could afford a lawyer or a premium agency. And where the agencies and practitioners who serve that market compete on service quality, not on who can make the most convincing unverified claim.

What the platform does

  • Organises 69 migration pathways across 30 countries with official source trails
  • Shows structured requirements - funds thresholds, job offer conditions, points scores - sourced directly from government authorities
  • Labels data status honestly: where evidence is missing, we say so rather than estimate
  • Provides a route finder that matches your goal, region and route type against evidence-gated pathways
  • Publishes 84 country audits, playbooks, guides and case studies across 7 categories
  • Provides a budget calculator as a planning aid - not an eligibility decision

What the platform does not do

  • Claim approval rates by nationality - these are not published by most authorities
  • Guarantee visa, employment or admission outcomes
  • Provide legal advice or immigration consultancy
  • Publish cost or timeline figures without a primary-source evidence record
  • Rank pathways by commercial relationships - evidence status is never influenced by revenue

The partner model

The agencies and practitioners in the African migration market are not our competitors. They are the distribution layer. They have the client relationships, the local offices, the language, the trust. What they lack is a credible, verifiable source of truth.

We are the evidence layer. They are the service layer. Together we serve the client honestly.

MyPathway2Global works with three types of partners: verified practitioners (immigration lawyers and regulated advisers who review pathway records), agency partners (travel agencies and relocation firms who route clients to the platform), and content partners (organisations that co-produce country audits and guides). All partners accept the trust firewall - commercial relationships never influence pathway rankings or evidence status.

The methodology

Every pathway record must pass four filters before it is published:

  1. Proof-of-funds realism - the actual upfront capital required, assessed against African economic realities
  2. Selection and quota asymmetry - whether the route bypasses overcrowded national pools or benefits from regional concessions
  3. PR conversion vector - whether temporary status offers a predictable legal path to long-term settlement
  4. Primary-source provenance - every claim must cite an active government portal, university gazette, or official programme administrator

Pathways that do not pass all four filters are marked research required with a documented blocker. They are never recommended to users.

Current coverage

  • 30 countries across Europe, North America, Asia-Pacific, Asia and Africa
  • 69 pathways - 63 publishable, 6 research-required with documented blockers
  • 216+ evidence records across primary-source-verified and addenda registries
  • 84 blog posts across country audits, playbooks, guides and case studies
  • All data is evidence-gated: aggregate approval rates and true costs remain null where field-level evidence is missing

Core values

  • Radical transparency - we show uncertainty rather than hide it
  • Evidence before certainty - no claim without a primary source
  • Actionable intelligence - clear next steps, not vague guidance
  • Cultural respect - built for African applicants, not adapted from Western-centric tools
  • Responsible revenue - monetisation is disclosed and consent-gated
  • Trust firewall - commercial relationships never influence pathway rankings or evidence status
