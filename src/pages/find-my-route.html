Find My Route

Filter 55 evidence-gated pathways by goal. Official requirements only — no invented approval rates.

What this tool does

  • Filters 55 publishable pathways by goal (study or work)
  • Shows official requirements from government portals
  • Surfaces nearby routes with documented blockers shown

What this tool does not do

  • Assess your personal eligibility
  • Rank by budget, income or experience
  • Predict approval likelihood
  • Cover business pathways (not yet in scope)

Evidence-gated pathway matches

Select Study or Work to view pathways whose exact-route records have passed the current evidence gate. Unverified pathways will not be recommended.
