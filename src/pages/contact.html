Report a data error

If you find a requirement, cost figure or source link that appears incorrect or outdated, email us with:

  • The pathway or country affected (e.g. "DE-study — blocked account amount")
  • What you believe is incorrect
  • A link to the official source that contradicts it

We take data accuracy seriously. Corrections are reviewed against primary sources before any change is made.
