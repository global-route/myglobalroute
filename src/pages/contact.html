Contact

Three paths - choose the one that fits your purpose.

1. Users - questions about pathways, countries or the platform

Email hello@mypathway2global.org.uk with your question. We aim to respond within 3 business days.

Please do not send passports, identity documents, bank statements or immigration case files. Include only the minimum details needed to explain your question.

What we cannot help with: MyPathway2Global provides general information and planning tools, not legal advice or immigration consultancy. We cannot assess your individual eligibility, review your documents, or advise on a specific visa application. For those needs, consult a registered immigration adviser or lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction.

2. Agencies and practitioners - partnership enquiries

If you are a travel agency, relocation firm, immigration lawyer or regulated adviser interested in working with the platform, email hello@mypathway2global.org.uk with the subject line Partnership enquiry.

Include: your organisation name, your role, the countries or services you cover, and a brief description of how you think we could work together.

Before reaching out, read the partnership overview to understand the three partner types and the trust firewall that applies to all partnerships.

3. Data errors - incorrect or outdated information

If you find a requirement, cost figure or source link that appears incorrect or outdated, email hello@mypathway2global.org.uk with the subject line Data error and include:

  • The pathway or country affected (e.g. "DE-study - blocked account amount")
  • What you believe is incorrect
  • A link to the official source that contradicts it

Corrections are reviewed against primary sources before any change is made. We take data accuracy seriously - it is the foundation of everything the platform does.
