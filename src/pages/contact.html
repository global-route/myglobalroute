Contact Global Route

Report a data error, ask a question, or get in touch.

Email

hello@global-route.app

This is the fastest way to reach us. We aim to respond within 3 business days.

Please do not send passports, identity documents, bank statements or immigration case files. Include only the minimum details needed to explain your question.

Report a data error

If you find a requirement, cost figure or source link that appears incorrect or outdated, email us with:

  • The pathway or country affected (e.g. "DE-study — blocked account amount")
  • What you believe is incorrect
  • A link to the official source that contradicts it

We take data accuracy seriously. Corrections are reviewed against primary sources before any change is made.

What we cannot help with

Global Route provides general information and planning tools, not legal advice or immigration consultancy. We cannot assess your individual eligibility, review your documents, or advise on a specific visa application. For those needs, consult a registered immigration adviser or lawyer in the relevant jurisdiction.
