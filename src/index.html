The Problem with Standard Migration Info

❌ Inflated Statistics

A 95% Finland approval rate includes applicants from 50+ countries. For a Nigerian passport? Often 60% or less.

❌ Hidden Costs

Students discover blocked account requirements AFTER getting admitted. Professionals miss salary thresholds by $100/month due to currency conversion.

❌ Predatory Advice

Fake visa guarantees, forged documents, and "100% approval" schemes prey on families saving for years.
