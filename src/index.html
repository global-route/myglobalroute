Migration information should be auditable

❌ Unsupported rates

Government authorities often do not publish approval rates by nationality. We show “not published” instead of repeating viral estimates.

❌ Hidden assumptions

Costs, thresholds and timelines are pathway-specific. Each material claim needs a source, date and review status.

❌ One-size-fits-all advice

A route is only useful when eligibility, budget, experience, language and evidence fit the applicant.
