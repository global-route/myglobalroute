Why this exists

Migration advice online is full of unverified claims — approval rates that authorities never published, cost estimates with no source, timelines based on hearsay. African applicants deserve better.

MyPathway2Global tracks official requirements from government portals. Where data is missing or unverified, we say so. Every pathway shows its evidence status, source URL, and last review date.

How it works

  1. Primary sources first — Requirements come from immigration authorities, not forums or agents.
  2. Provenance tracked — Every claim has a source URL, retrieval date, and confidence level.
  3. Pathway-specific — Compare study, work, and business routes separately, not as one "visa" option.
  4. Uncertainty visible — Unverified pathways are blocked from recommendations until reviewed.
