# Austria Red-White-Red Card — Other Key Workers Validation

## Exact scope
`AT-rwr-other-key-workers` is an exact child route under `AT-red-white-red`. Evidence for other Red-White-Red categories must not be inherited.

## Current material gates
- Third-country national scope.
- Red-White-Red Card validity is 24 months for this route.
- 2026 remuneration requirement: at least **EUR 3,465 gross monthly**.
- Minimum **55 points** under the official points system.
- Labour-market test applies.
- Application/document evidence must support the claimed points, employment and remuneration rather than relying on the parent pathway.

## Recommendation-engine implication
This route should only be recommended when the applicant/employer facts can satisfy the points and remuneration gates. The engine must not recommend `AT-red-white-red` as though all RWR categories have identical requirements.
