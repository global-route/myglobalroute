# MyPathway2Global — Mission, Vision & Trust Firewall

**Document type:** Internal — strategic foundation  
**Status:** Active  
**Updated:** 2026-10-30

---

## Mission

MyPathway2Global provides verified, primary-source migration intelligence for African students, professionals and entrepreneurs — and the agencies, practitioners and organisations that serve them.

Every pathway record cites its official source, declares its evidence status, and is updated when policy changes. We do not publish what we cannot verify.

---

## Vision

A continent where every African who wants to move — for work, study, investment or opportunity — has access to the same quality of verified information that was previously available only to those who could afford a lawyer or a premium agency.

And where the agencies and practitioners who serve that market compete on service quality, not on who can make the most convincing unverified claim.

---

## The problem we exist to solve

The African migration services market is characterised by:

- **Information asymmetry.** Agents hold information that applicants cannot independently verify. This creates dependency and enables exploitation.
- **Unverified claims.** "Visa in 3 days." "75% approval rate." "Zero deposit." These claims circulate in WhatsApp groups and Telegram channels with no source, no accountability, and no recourse when they prove false.
- **No standard.** There is no shared reference point that separates a legitimate agency from a fraudulent one. Both use the same flyer format, the same language, the same channels.
- **High stakes.** A failed visa application costs money, time, and in some cases a person's entire savings. The consequences of bad information are not abstract.

MyPathway2Global is the reference point that does not exist yet. Not a directory of agents. Not a comparison site. A primary-source intelligence layer that anyone — applicant or agency — can cite.

---

## What we are

An evidence-gated migration intelligence platform. We track official requirements from government immigration portals, university gazettes, and authorised program administrators. We do not track agent claims, forum posts, or social media.

We are not:
- An immigration law firm
- A visa processing service
- A travel agency
- A job placement service
- A guarantee of any outcome

---

## The partner model

The agencies and practitioners in the African migration market are not our competitors. They are the distribution layer. They have the client relationships, the local offices, the language, the trust. What they lack is a credible, verifiable source of truth.

**We are the evidence layer. They are the service layer. Together we serve the client honestly.**

A legitimate agency that partners with MyPathway2Global gains:
- A verified source they can cite to clients
- Differentiation from bad actors who cannot cite any source
- Access to structured pathway data for the countries they serve
- A trust signal that is worth more than any marketing claim

In return, they accept the trust firewall.

## The trust firewall — non-negotiable rules

These rules apply to the platform, to every staff member, and to every partner. They are not negotiable in any commercial agreement.

### Rule 1 — No commercial influence on pathway rankings
No partner payment, referral fee, commission arrangement, or commercial relationship of any kind may influence which pathways are recommended, how they are ranked, or what their evidence status is declared to be.

A pathway is publishable when it passes the evidence gate. It is research-required when it does not. No commercial consideration changes this.

### Rule 2 — No fabricated claims
The platform does not publish:
- Approval rates by nationality (not published by any immigration authority)
- Processing time guarantees
- Success rate statistics
- "Zero deposit" claims where a deposit is required
- "Visa in X days" claims where the official processing time is longer

If a partner asks us to publish any of these, the answer is no. If a partner publishes these claims and attributes them to MyPathway2Global, the partnership ends.

### Rule 3 — No suppression of adverse evidence
If a pathway has a documented blocker — a quota, a high rejection rate for a specific profile, a policy change that makes it less accessible — that information is published. It is not removed because a partner sells that pathway.

### Rule 4 — No PII in the platform
The platform does not collect, store, or transmit passport copies, CV documents, bank statements, or any personally identifiable information. Any partner integration that requires PII collection must handle it entirely outside the platform, under their own data protection obligations.

### Rule 5 — Clear disclosure of commercial relationships
Any partner whose content appears on the platform, or who is listed as a verified practitioner, must be disclosed as such. Users must be able to distinguish between platform-generated content and partner-contributed content.

### Rule 6 — No legal advice
The platform provides information, not legal advice. No content on the platform constitutes immigration legal advice. Partners who are regulated immigration advisers or lawyers may provide legal advice through their own channels — not through the platform.

## The red lines — what will end a partnership immediately

1. A partner uses the MyPathway2Global name or brand to make claims the platform does not support
2. A partner requests removal of adverse evidence about a pathway they sell
3. A partner requests that a pathway be marked publishable before it passes the evidence gate
4. A partner collects PII from users via the platform
5. A partner misrepresents their regulatory status (claiming to be a regulated adviser when they are not)
6. A partner publishes fabricated approval rates, processing time guarantees, or success statistics and attributes them to MyPathway2Global

## The commercial structure (foundation)

Revenue from partnerships must never create a conflict of interest with the evidence gate. The permitted commercial structures are:

**Permitted:**
- Flat-fee verified practitioner listings (partner pays for visibility, not for pathway ranking)
- Referral fees from partners to whom users are directed (disclosed to users)
- Content licensing (partner pays to use platform data in their own materials, subject to accuracy requirements)
- API access fees (partner pays for structured data access, subject to use restrictions)

**Not permitted:**
- Pay-per-recommendation (partner pays per user directed to them from a pathway result)
- Pathway ranking fees (partner pays to appear higher in results)
- Evidence suppression fees (partner pays to remove adverse information)
- Exclusive pathway listings (partner pays to be the only option shown for a country or route)

---

## The foundation principle

This platform is built on the premise that African applicants deserve the same quality of verified information that was previously available only to those who could afford a lawyer. Every commercial decision must be tested against this premise. If a commercial arrangement would reduce the quality or honesty of information available to applicants, it does not happen.

The platform's commercial value depends entirely on its trustworthiness. The moment it becomes another source of unverified claims, it has no value — to applicants, to legitimate partners, or as a business.
