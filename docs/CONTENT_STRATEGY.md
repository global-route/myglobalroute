## 📊 Content Pillars

Global Route content is organized into **4 primary pillars**, each targeting specific user intent and keywords.

**Purpose:** Establish authority on true visa approval rates and costs

**Content Types:**
- Country profiles (all 26 countries)
- Regional comparisons (Anglosphere vs. Scandinavia)
- Policy change updates (quarterly)
- Hidden cost breakdowns

**Target Keywords:**
- "Germany visa from Nigeria" (CPC: $5-12)
- "Canada student visa rejection rate" (CPC: $6-10)
- "True cost of UK Masters visa" (CPC: $4-8)
- "Italy visa for Africans 2026" (CPC: $3-7)

**Deliverables:**
- 26 country deep-dives (2,500-3,500 words each)
- 5 regional comparison articles
- 12 quarterly policy updates

## ✍️ Content Standards

### **Tone & Voice**
- **Honest:** Never sugar-coat rejection rates or costs
- **Actionable:** Every article should have a clear next step
- **Respectful:** Acknowledge emotional weight of migration decisions
- **Data-driven:** Back claims with sources and citations

### **Article Structure**
```markdown
# [Keyword-Rich Title]

## Quick Summary
- Key finding 1
- Key finding 2

## The Official Story
- What governments claim
- Published statistics

## The African Reality
- True approval rate for passport holders
- Hidden barriers

## Cost Breakdown
- Tuition
- Application fees
- Blocked account requirements
- Translation/legalization fees
- Total true cost

## Sweet Spots (Realistic Pathways)
- Option 1 with conditions
- Option 2 with conditions

## Pros & Cons
- Advantages
- Disadvantages

## Step-by-Step Guide
1. Step 1
2. Step 2

## Common Questions
- FAQ 1
- FAQ 2

## Resources
- Official links
- Embassy contacts
- Supporting documents
```

### **SEO Best Practices**
- **Title:** 50-60 characters, keyword-rich
- **Meta Description:** 150-160 characters, include keyword
- **Headings:** H1 once, H2s for sections
- **Keywords:** Natural placement, 1-2% keyword density
- **Images:** Alt text describing content
- **Links:** Internal linking to related articles
- **Length:** 2,000-3,500 words for authority

### **Fact-Checking Protocol**
1. Source all claims to official government websites
2. Include publication dates (policies change)
3. Add "Updated: [Date]" to outdated content
4. Request community corrections in comments
5. Monthly audit of linked sources for dead links

## 🎯 SEO Strategy

### **Technical SEO**
- ✅ Mobile-responsive design (100% mobile-first)
- ✅ Fast page load (<2s on 4G)
- ✅ Clean URL structure (/blog/germany-visa-from-nigeria)
- ✅ XML sitemap + robots.txt
- ✅ HTTPS only
- ✅ Structured data (schema.org for articles)

### **On-Page SEO**
- ✅ Keyword in H1, first 100 words, meta description
- ✅ Internal linking (3-5 related articles per post)
- ✅ Alt text on all images
- ✅ Descriptive anchor text
- ✅ Table of contents for long articles

### **Off-Page SEO**
- ✅ Social media promotion (Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit)
- ✅ Guest posts on African diaspora blogs
- ✅ Backlinks from universities/government resources
- ✅ Business listings (Google My Business if applicable)
- ✅ Press releases for major policy changes

### **Content Distribution**
- **Week 1:** Publish article, share on Twitter/LinkedIn
- **Week 2:** Guest post on 1-2 partner blogs
- **Week 3:** Include in newsletter
- **Month 2:** Update with new data/links, promote again
- **Month 3:** Create related pillar + cluster content
