## How to source images

**For country images (landmarks, cityscapes, flags):**
- Unsplash: unsplash.com — search country name, filter by landscape/architecture
- Pexels: pexels.com — same approach
- Wikimedia Commons: commons.wikimedia.org — government buildings, official photos
- Official tourism boards often have press/media kits with free-use images

**For policy/news posts:**
- Screenshot of the official government announcement page (with attribution)
- Official government press release image
- Ministry/department building photograph

**For case studies:**
- Real photo of the subject (with their permission) OR
- A relevant landmark of the destination country

**For guides/playbooks:**
- Relevant country landmark OR
- A professional working in the relevant field (Unsplash/Pexels)

**Attribution:** Add a caption line in the post body: `Photo: [Photographer], [Source]`
