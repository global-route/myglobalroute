## Sweet Spots (Realistic Pathways)

### Option 1: Public University Route
- Zero tuition
- Need blocked account
- Part-time work allowed (20hrs/week)

### Option 2: Studienkolleg Pathway
- Preparatory college
- Lower blocked account (older rules)
- Easier admission

## Pros & Cons

**Pros:**
- ✅ No tuition fees
- ✅ 18-month job search visa
- ✅ Path to permanent residency

**Cons:**
- ❌ High blocked account requirement
- ❌ German language needed (many programs)
- ❌ Long processing times

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Phase 1: Preparation (Months 1-3)
1. Research universities
2. Prepare documents
3. Take language tests

### Phase 2: Application (Months 4-6)
1. Apply to universities
2. Get admission letter
3. Open blocked account

### Phase 3: Visa (Months 7-9)
1. Book embassy appointment
2. Submit documents
3. Attend interview
4. Wait for decision

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I fund the blocked account from Nigeria?**
A: Yes, via Wise or Western Union. Allow 5-7 days for transfer.

**Q: What if I'm rejected?**
A: You can reapply immediately. Check reason and fix gaps.

**Q: Do I need German language?**
A: Depends on program. English-taught programs available.
