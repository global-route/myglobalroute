### 3A. Canada tech — Express Entry (verified)

**Primary source:** `canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry`

**What is verified:**
- Software developers (NOC 21232), data scientists (NOC 21211), cybersecurity analysts (NOC 21220), DevOps/cloud engineers (NOC 21223), network engineers (NOC 21222), QA engineers (NOC 21231), web developers (NOC 21234), IT project managers (NOC 20012) — all NOC TEER 0 or 1
- All qualify for Federal Skilled Worker Program (FSWP) under Express Entry
- Minimum 67 points on FSWP selection factors required to enter the pool
- CRS score then determines invitation — recent tech category draws have had cutoffs around 481-500 CRS
- Processing target after ITA: 6 months
- Minimum funds (family of 1): CAD 14,690 settlement funds
- English: CLB 7 minimum (IELTS General: 6.0 each band)
- Experience: 1 year continuous full-time in NOC TEER 0/1/2/3 in past 10 years

**What HelpOverseas gets wrong:**
- "PR in less than 11 months" — possible only if CRS score is high enough for an early draw invitation. Most applicants wait 12-24 months from profile creation to PR landing
- "Free PR eligibility check in 60 seconds" — a CRS calculator, not an eligibility assessment. Does not account for credential recognition, language scores, or draw cutoffs

**Salary reality (verified from Job Bank Canada):**
- Software Developer: CAD 70,000-110,000 (median CAD 85,000)
- Data Scientist: CAD 75,000-120,000 (median CAD 95,000)
- Cybersecurity Analyst: CAD 70,000-105,000
- DevOps/Cloud Engineer: CAD 80,000-130,000
- Network Engineer: CAD 65,000-100,000

**Degree recognition:** Foreign degrees assessed by WES (World Education Services) or other designated organisations. Nigerian university degrees are generally recognised but must be assessed.

  - Electrician: OMR 180-350/month

**Red flags in the Telegram offer:**

- "Visa ready in 1 week" — employer-sponsored visa takes 2-4 weeks minimum

### 3D. Qatar work visa reality (verified)

**Primary source:** `adlsa.gov.qa` (Qatar Ministry of Labour)

**What is verified:**
- Qatar Freelance Visa (Self-Employment Permit) — real category, introduced as part of post-2022 labour reforms
- Requirements for Qatar Freelance Visa: Proof of professional qualification, bank statement showing QAR 10,000+ minimum, health insurance, valid passport
- Processing time: 2-4 weeks minimum — NOT 3-5 days
- Official fees: QAR 1,000-2,000 (NGN 150,000-300,000 at current rates) — NOT NGN 3.2m
- Qatar Employment Visa (standard): Employer applies on behalf of worker. Worker does not pay for the visa — employer pays. Any agent charging NGN 3m+ for a Qatar employment visa is charging for services the employer should provide.
- Minimum wage: QAR 1,000/month (non-domestic) — NGN 650k/month salary claim (~QAR 1,600) is plausible for low-skill work
- Kafala reform status: Exit permit abolished 2020. Workers can change jobs without employer permission since 2021. But enforcement gaps remain.

### 3E. Bulgaria study visa (verified)

**Primary source:** `mfa.bg` (Bulgarian Ministry of Foreign Affairs), `mon.bg` (Ministry of Education)

**What is verified:**
- Bulgaria joined Schengen Area on 1 January 2024 (air and sea borders). Land borders joined 1 January 2025. This is a significant and recent fact.
- Bulgarian public university tuition for non-EU students: EUR 2,000-4,000/year (medicine higher at EUR 8,000-10,000) — genuinely cheaper than UK/DE/IE
- Living costs in Sofia: EUR 500-700/month — among the cheapest EU capitals
- Installment payments: Some Bulgarian universities do offer semester-based payment — partially true
- Study visa (Type D): Required for courses over 90 days. Issued by Bulgarian embassy.
- Post-study work rights: As a Schengen member, a Bulgarian residence permit allows travel across Schengen. However, work rights in OTHER Schengen countries require that country's work permit — the Bulgarian residence permit alone does not grant work rights in Germany or France.
- Language: Most undergraduate programmes are in Bulgarian. English-taught programmes exist but are fewer and more expensive.
- Key institutions: Sofia University, Technical University of Sofia, Medical University Sofia, New Bulgarian University (private, English-taught)

**What the Facebook post gets wrong:**
- Does not mention that most programmes are in Bulgarian
- Does not clarify that Schengen travel ≠ Schengen work rights in other countries
- "Installments" is true for some institutions but not universal
