## 🚨 Risk Mitigation

### **Risk 1: Outdated Information**
- **Mitigation:** Add "Updated: [Date]" to all content
- **Frequency:** Monthly policy audits
- **Escalation:** Quarterly comprehensive review

### **Risk 2: Competitor Content**
- **Mitigation:** Focus on unique angle (African perspective)
- **Escalation:** Faster publishing, deeper research
- **Review:** Monthly competitive analysis

### **Risk 3: Fact Accuracy**
- **Mitigation:** Multiple source verification
- **Escalation:** Community fact-checking in comments
- **Review:** Editorial review before publishing

### **Risk 4: Traffic Plateau**
- **Mitigation:** Expand to long-tail keywords
- **Escalation:** Guest posts, social media, PR
- **Review:** Monthly SEO audit

### **Risk 5: Revenue Underperformance**
- **Mitigation:** Aggressive direct sponsorship outreach
- **Escalation:** Premium content tier
- **Review:** Monthly monetization review
