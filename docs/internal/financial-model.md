## 💰 Revenue Streams

### **Stream 1: Google AdMob**

**Model:** Contextual ads on high-traffic pages

**Pricing:**
- Average CPM: $5-$12 (travel/finance keywords)
- Average CTR: 1-3% (contextual relevance)
- Average CPC: $3-$8

**Calculation:**
```
Monthly Revenue = (Monthly Impressions / 1000) × CPM
50,000 visitors × 3 pages/visit × 2 ads/page = 300,000 impressions
300,000 / 1000 × $8 CPM = $2,400/month
```

**Year 1 Projection:**
- Q1: $800/month (low traffic)
- Q2: $1,500/month (growing traffic)
- Q3: $2,200/month (SEO rankings)
- Q4: $3,000/month (authority)
- **Total Year 1: $24,000**

**Year 2 Projection:**
- Steady: $4,000-$6,000/month
- **Total Year 2: $60,000**

## 📈 Break-Even Analysis

### **Break-Even Point**

**Monthly Revenue Needed:**
- Fixed costs: $140/month
- Variable costs: $4,300/month
- **Required monthly revenue: $4,440**

**Traffic Needed to Break-Even:**
```
$4,440 needed
÷ $10 average RPM (AdMob + sponsorships)
= 444,000 monthly impressions

444,000 impressions
÷ 5 ads per visitor (avg)
= ~88,800 monthly visitors

88,800 visitors
÷ 30 days
= ~3,000 daily visitors
```

**Break-Even Timeline:**
- Month 1-3: Heavy loss (low traffic)
- Month 4-6: Reducing loss (traffic growing)
- Month 7-8: Near break-even (SEO rankings improving)
- Month 9+: Profitable (authority established)

**Estimated break-even: Month 8-9 (August-September 2026)**
