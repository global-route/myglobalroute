## 📋 Role Definitions

### **Tier 1: Leadership**

**Responsibilities:**
- Overall vision & strategy
- Team coordination
- Stakeholder communication
- Final decision-making
- Revenue target accountability

**Time Commitment:** 40+ hours/week

**Skills Required:**
- Project management
- Strategic thinking
- Content understanding
- Business acumen

**Success Metrics:**
- On-time milestone delivery
- Revenue targets met
- Team satisfaction

- Content calendar management

## 🤝 Cross-Functional Collaboration

### **Content → Tech Handoffs**

**Content Writer ↔ Frontend Developer:**
- Markdown files → HTML templates
- SEO requirements → Page structure
- User flow designs → Interactive features

**Weekly sync:** Tuesday 2pm (30 min)

---

### **Editorial → Fact-Check Process**

**Editor → Fact-Checker:**
- Draft article → Fact-check review
- Fact-Checker flags issues
- Editor revises → Final publication

**SLA:** 48 hours fact-check turnaround

---

### **Business → Content Strategy**

**Business Lead ↔ Content Director:**
- Sponsorship needs → Sponsored content
- Keyword research → Editorial priorities
- Analytics insights → Content optimization

**Weekly sync:** Wednesday 3pm (30 min)

---

## 📅 Meeting Schedule

### **Weekly Meetings**

**Monday 10am: Team Standup** (30 min)
- All departments
- Status updates
- Blockers/escalations

**Tuesday 2pm: Content Sync** (30 min)
- Content Director + Editors + Writers
- Editorial calendar review
- Publishing schedule

**Wednesday 3pm: Business Review** (30 min)
- Business Lead + Growth metrics
- Revenue tracking
- Partnership status

**Thursday 4pm: Tech Review** (30 min)
- Technical Lead + Developers
- Performance metrics
- Bug triage

---

### **Bi-Weekly Meetings**

**Every 2 weeks: Full Leadership** (1 hour)
- CEO + Department Heads
- Strategic planning
- Budget review
- Overall progress
