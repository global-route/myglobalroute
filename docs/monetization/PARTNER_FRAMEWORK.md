# Partner Framework — MyPathway2Global

**Document type:** Internal — commercial foundation
**Status:** Active — pre-launch
**Updated:** 2026-10-30
**Prerequisite:** Legal review required before any partner agreement is signed

---

## Overview

MyPathway2Global operates a three-tier partner model. Each tier has defined obligations, permitted activities, prohibited activities, and a commercial structure. All tiers are subject to the Trust Firewall in `docs/product/MISSION_VISION.md`.

---

## Tier A — Verified Practitioner

**Who:** Licensed immigration lawyers, regulated immigration advisers, accredited consultants operating in the African market.

**What they contribute:**
- Primary-source sign-off on pathway records for countries within their practice area
- Their name and firm appear on the pathway record as the verified reviewer
- They replace the current "owner action" for country source sign-off
- They may contribute anonymised case study content

**What they receive:**
- Verified Practitioner badge on the platform
- A practitioner profile page linked from relevant pathway records
- Referral traffic from users who need legal advice beyond what the platform provides

**Obligations:**
- Must hold current regulatory authorisation in their jurisdiction
- Must review pathway records against primary sources, not agent knowledge
- Must disclose any conflict of interest
- Must not use practitioner status to influence pathway rankings

**Commercial structure:** Annual flat fee for listing. No per-referral fee.

**Disqualifying factors:** Regulatory sanction, history of fabricated claims, refusal to disclose conflicts.

---

## Tier B — Agency Partner

**Who:** Travel agencies, relocation firms, HR platforms, migration service providers. Examples from the market: Liberty Track Travels, Yellowlane, HY-BAZE Travels and Tours, Aventria Tours and Events.

**What they contribute:**
- Distribution — they route clients to the platform as the verified source
- Co-branding rights (subject to accuracy requirements)
- Embedding platform pathway data in their client communications

**What they receive:**
- Agency Partner badge and listing in a partner directory
- Co-branding rights
- Access to structured pathway data for the countries they serve
- A trust signal that differentiates them from unverified competitors

**Obligations:**
- Must not make claims about pathways that contradict the platform's published data
- Must not use the platform's name to imply endorsement of their specific services
- Must not collect PII from users via any platform integration
- Must maintain a written agreement with clients before commencing services
- Must disclose to clients that MyPathway2Global is an information source, not a guarantee

**Commercial structure:** Annual flat fee for listing. Revenue share on qualified referrals (disclosed to users) — subject to legal review.

**Disqualifying factors:** History of fabricated claims, unresolved client complaints involving financial loss, operating without required regulatory authorisation, collecting PII without adequate data protection.

---

## Tier C — Content Partner

**Who:** Research organisations, universities, diaspora organisations, professional associations, media organisations.

**What they contribute:**
- Country audits, playbooks, case studies, or policy updates co-produced with the platform
- All content goes through the same evidence gate as platform-generated content

**What they receive:**
- Co-authorship attribution on published content
- Brand visibility and SEO attribution

**Obligations:**
- All content must meet the platform's evidence standards
- No content may be published that the partner cannot support with primary sources
- The partner may not veto adverse information about pathways relevant to their interests

**Commercial structure:** Content licensing fee for use of platform data. No fee for co-produced content published on the platform.

---

## Partner onboarding process

1. Expression of interest via the partners page
2. Due diligence — verify regulatory status, review marketing materials for fabricated claims
3. Written partnership agreement signed before any co-branding or data access
4. Onboarding — partner listed, data access granted, obligations confirmed
5. Annual review of partner compliance
6. Immediate suspension/termination if any red line is crossed

---

## What every partner agreement must contain

1. Explicit acceptance of the Trust Firewall (Rules 1-6 from MISSION_VISION.md)
2. Prohibition on fabricated claims attributed to MyPathway2Global
3. Prohibition on PII collection via platform integration
4. Disclosure obligations to end users
5. Termination clause for red line violations (immediate, no notice required)
6. Jurisdiction and governing law
7. Limitation of liability (platform not liable for partner service failures)
8. Data protection obligations (partner is data controller for their client data)

**This agreement requires solicitor review before use.**

## Market context

The Wakanow Affiliates Telegram group (1,660 members) is representative of the African migration services market:

- Legitimate operators exist with physical offices and written agreement practices (Liberty Track Travels, Yellowlane, HY-BAZE, Aventria) — these are Tier B candidates
- The market has a documented trust problem: "If travel agents don't trust each other... honestly it is worrisome" (HecTour Consult, October 2026)
- The claims that circulate are exactly the red lines: "visa out in 3-5 days", "zero deposit", "75-80% approval rate", "guaranteed approval"
- A legitimate agency that can say "our pathway data is verified by MyPathway2Global" has a genuine differentiator in a market where no one else can say that
