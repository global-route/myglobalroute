# Partner Revenue

**Status:** Planned

## Objective
Create a B2B revenue layer for organizations that serve people moving internationally.

Potential customers:
- education providers;
- relocation companies;
- insurance providers;
- recruitment businesses;
- licensed immigration/legal professionals where appropriate;
- accommodation providers;
- mobility companies.

## Productized partner offerings
### Partner Starter
Basic verified listing and qualified referral access.

### Partner Pro
- enhanced profile;
- lead dashboard;
- analytics;
- pathway/category targeting;
- priority commercial inventory subject to quality rules.

### Partner Enterprise
- API/data integrations;
- larger lead volumes;
- reporting;
- custom integrations;
- account support;
- contractual SLAs where appropriate.

Pricing is to be validated through experiments; no price is final until market validation.

## Partner MRR
Partner MRR = sum of active recurring partner contracts.

Separate:
- partner subscription MRR;
- sponsored placement revenue;
- lead revenue;
- referral commissions.

Only recurring contractual amounts enter MRR.

## Lead pricing
Potential models:
- pay-per-qualified-lead;
- pay-per-accepted-lead;
- pay-per-conversion;
- monthly lead bundle;
- hybrid subscription + lead fee.

Select by category. Regulated/high-trust categories require additional review.

## Sponsored placement
All sponsored inventory must be clearly identified.

Example structure: Sponsored partner → Company → Service → Why it may be relevant → Learn more.

Commercial placement must not be presented as the MyGlobalRoute route recommendation itself.

## Partner ranking
Default ranking should prioritize:
1. user relevance;
2. verified service availability;
3. quality;
4. geography;
5. price/value;
6. partner performance;
7. commercial terms as a controlled secondary factor.

Never allow the highest payer to automatically become the best migration route.

## Partner dashboard
Future dashboard:
- leads;
- accepted leads;
- conversion;
- revenue;
- clicks;
- placements;
- pathway categories;
- invoices;
- subscription status.

## Partner governance
Maintain:
- partner registry;
- contract start/end;
- status;
- verification date;
- service regions;
- disclosure requirements;
- payout terms;
- complaints;
- suspension history.

## B2B expansion
Public migration intelligence → Professional tooling → Partner portal → API/data products → Enterprise mobility intelligence.

The public product remains evidence-first as commercial products expand.

## MRR objective
Partner revenue is strategically important because it can become recurring and less dependent on advertising traffic.
