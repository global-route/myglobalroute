# Affiliate Strategy

**Status:** Planned

## Objective
Monetize high-intent migration users through relevant products/services without allowing commercial relationships to corrupt route intelligence.

## Priority categories
### Tier 1
- international/student insurance;
- education providers;
- accommodation;
- relocation services.

### Tier 2
- language testing/preparation;
- document services;
- international financial services;
- travel-related services where genuinely relevant.

### Tier 3
Future categories should be evaluated individually for user benefit, regulatory risk, trust impact, commission economics and geographic coverage.

## Matching principle
Route → User need → Relevant service → Partner.

Never use: Highest commission → Recommended route.

Commission must never determine migration-pathway ranking.

## Partner qualification
Before listing a partner:
- verify legal business identity;
- verify service availability;
- verify geography;
- verify pricing/claims;
- verify terms;
- assess complaints/reputation;
- confirm affiliate/referral terms;
- confirm required disclosures;
- establish a compliance contact.

## Disclosure
Affiliate relationships must be clearly disclosed near the commercial recommendation and/or in an appropriate disclosure policy.

Do not describe a paid partner as:
- government approved;
- officially endorsed;
- an official immigration authority;
- evidence for legal eligibility.

## Attribution
Every partner link should support:
- partner ID;
- placement ID;
- page/pathway ID;
- campaign/source;
- timestamp;
- consent state where required.

Do not pass unnecessary personal or sensitive migration data into affiliate URLs.

## Economics
CTR = partner clicks / partner impressions.

Conversion rate = conversions / eligible clicks.

EPC = affiliate revenue / clicks.

Revenue per visitor = affiliate revenue / visitors.

Use actual partner statements for reconciliation rather than estimated conversions.

## Link architecture
Use a controlled tracking layer where appropriate:

MyGlobalRoute placement → tracked partner click → partner.

Do not hide the commercial destination deceptively.

## Partner lifecycle
1. Prospect.
2. Due diligence.
3. Commercial agreement.
4. Tracking setup.
5. Disclosure.
6. Limited launch.
7. Performance review.
8. Quality review.
9. Renew, improve or remove.

## Removal triggers
Remove or suspend partners for:
- misleading claims;
- material service deterioration;
- regulatory concerns;
- unresolved complaints;
- broken attribution;
- non-payment;
- evidence/trust conflicts;
- attempts to influence route rankings.

## Strategic objective
Affiliate revenue should grow from contextual usefulness, not from increasing ad-like clutter.
